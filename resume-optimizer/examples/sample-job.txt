Growth Marketing Manager — Learnovate (Singapore)

About the role
We are hiring a Growth Marketing Manager to own our acquisition
programmes across APAC. You will drive paid media, lifecycle email
marketing and social media strategy, and report on performance weekly.

Responsibilities
- Own paid media campaigns (Meta Ads, Google Ads) and manage the
  monthly budget
- Build lifecycle email marketing programmes in HubSpot that convert
  trial users into paying customers
- Lead social media strategy and content planning across LinkedIn and
  Instagram
- Analyse campaign data in Excel and Google Analytics; present KPI
  dashboards to stakeholders
- Run A/B testing on landing pages and email subject lines
- Work cross-functional with product and sales teams

Requirements
- 4+ years in growth marketing or digital marketing, ideally B2C SaaS
  or education technology
- Hands-on experience with HubSpot, Google Analytics and paid media
- Strong data analysis skills and stakeholder management
- Experience with A/B testing and conversion optimisation
- Bachelor's degree in marketing, business or a related field

# Before / after — one bullet, honestly rewritten

From `sample-resume.txt`, tailored toward `sample-job.txt`. Every fact in
the "after" version was already in the original; bracketed marks are for
the candidate to fill with real numbers, and come out before submission.

**Before**
> Responsible for social media accounts and posting schedule

**After**
> Own social media strategy and content planning across LinkedIn and
> Instagram, running a [add metric: posts per week] schedule that grew
> followers from [add metric: start] to [add metric: current]

What changed:
- "Responsible for" (hides ownership) → "Own"
- The posting's own terms — "social media strategy", "content planning" —
  used because they truthfully name the same work (bucket 2, rephraseable)
- Two metric slots marked instead of invented

**A gap that stays a gap:** the posting asks for HubSpot and paid media
(Meta Ads, Google Ads). The sample resume shows neither, so neither is
written in. They appear in the delivery as bucket-3 gaps for the
candidate to address in a cover note or by upskilling.

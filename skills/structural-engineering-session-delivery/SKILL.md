# Session Delivery Guide — Structural Engineering

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for architects, builders and building owners in design offices and construction sites. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a structural engineer working with architects, builders and building owners in design offices and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a structural engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review structural calculations before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as calculation errors
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and structural analysis software; check everything works and is safe before architects, builders and building owners arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to calculation check completion
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

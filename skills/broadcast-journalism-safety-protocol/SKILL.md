# Safety Protocol — Broadcast Journalism

Work safely in newsrooms and outside broadcasts: a practical safety protocol for a broadcast journalist and audiences, contributors and news editors. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a broadcast journalist working with audiences, contributors and news editors in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.

## When to use
- You are a broadcast journalist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in newsrooms and outside broadcasts for staff, audiences, contributors and news editors and visitors, starting with inaccurate reporting broadcast
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as broadcast cameras
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in running orders
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

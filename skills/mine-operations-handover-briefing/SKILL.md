# Handover Briefing — Mine Operations

Hand over mining companies and mineral buyers or active work to another professional without losing critical detail. The deliverable is a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions.
This skill is written for a mine operations manager working with mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a mine operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mines and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Grade | the concentration of valuable mineral in ore |
| Ventilation | supplying fresh air and removing gases underground |
| Stope | an excavated area from which ore is extracted |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Summarise the current position from production reports: what is done, what is in progress, what is waiting
2. **Step 2** - Highlight live risks first — underground incidents — and the controls or watchpoints already in place
3. **Step 3** - State the next actions with owners and deadlines, and the results expected for production tonnage
4. **Step 4** - Walk the receiving professional through the brief, answer questions, and confirm understanding
5. **Step 5** - Record that handover happened, who accepted it, and where the full record lives in mines and processing plants

**Context:** [Situation in mines and processing plants] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [mining companies and mineral buyers and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions, structured as:

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

- Never let this happen: underground incidents.
- Never let this happen: ventilation failures.
- Never let this happen: equipment breakdowns.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a mine operations manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to mines and processing plants's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

# Supplies & Procurement — Investment Analysis

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in investment firms. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a investment analyst working with fund managers, investors and research subscribers in investment firms.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on research reports and the kit centred on market data terminals, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to conflicts of interest undisclosed
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

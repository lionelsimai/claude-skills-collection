# Ethics & Professional Standards — Restaurant Management

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a restaurant manager. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in restaurants and cafes: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams first where duties conflict, and be alert to how food hygiene failures can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from menus and costing sheets, and support colleagues who do the same

# Handover Briefing — Wind Energy

Hand over energy companies and grid operators or active work to another professional without losing critical detail. The deliverable is a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions.
This skill is written for a wind turbine technician working with energy companies and grid operators in wind farms onshore and offshore.

## When to use
- You are a wind turbine technician preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in wind farms onshore and offshore.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Summarise the current position from maintenance records: what is done, what is in progress, what is waiting
2. **Step 2** - Highlight live risks first — falls from height — and the controls or watchpoints already in place
3. **Step 3** - State the next actions with owners and deadlines, and the results expected for turbine availability
4. **Step 4** - Walk the receiving professional through the brief, answer questions, and confirm understanding
5. **Step 5** - Record that handover happened, who accepted it, and where the full record lives in wind farms onshore and offshore

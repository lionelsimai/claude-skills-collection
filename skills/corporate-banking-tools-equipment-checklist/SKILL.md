# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Corporate Banking

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a corporate relationship manager relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a corporate relationship manager working with companies and institutions in bank offices and client sites.

## When to use
- You are a corporate relationship manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank offices and client sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Facility | an agreed lending arrangement such as a loan or overdraft |
| Covenant | a condition a borrower must maintain |
| Credit paper | the document presenting a lending case for approval |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in bank offices and client sites, centred on lending systems, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against credit papers
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to credit losses from weak analysis
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: credit losses from weak analysis.
- Never let this happen: covenant breaches spotted late.
- Never let this happen: concentration risks.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a corporate relationship manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank offices and client sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

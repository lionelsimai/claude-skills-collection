# Risk Assessment — Dietetics

Identify, score and control the risks in work for patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment in hospital wards, clinics and community settings. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a dietitian working with patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment in hospital wards, clinics and community settings.

## When to use
- You are a dietitian preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hospital wards, clinics and community settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Enteral feeding | nutrition delivered through a tube into the stomach or bowel |
| Malnutrition | deficiency or imbalance of nutrients harming health |
| BMI | body mass index, weight relative to height |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to malnutrition unrecognised
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using anthropometric equipment where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in nutrition assessments, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in hospital wards, clinics and community settings change

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in hospital wards, clinics and community settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: malnutrition unrecognised.
- Never let this happen: refeeding syndrome.
- Never let this happen: unsafe feeding regimens.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a dietitian; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hospital wards, clinics and community settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

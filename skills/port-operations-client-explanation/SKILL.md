# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Port Operations

Explain complex port operations manager matters to shipping lines, hauliers and cargo owners in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a port operations manager working with shipping lines, hauliers and cargo owners in ports and container terminals.

## When to use
- You are a port operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in ports and container terminals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Berth | the place where a vessel moors to load or unload |
| TEU | a standard unit equal to a twenty-foot container |
| Turnaround | the time a vessel or truck spends in port |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what shipping lines, hauliers and cargo owners actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind berth plans — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as crane accidents
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking shipping lines, hauliers and cargo owners to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

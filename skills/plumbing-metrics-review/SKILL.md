# Metrics & Outcomes Review — Plumbing

Track, interpret and act on the outcome metrics that matter for a plumber. The deliverable is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
This skill is written for a plumber working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, building sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a plumber preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, building sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| First fix | the initial installation stage of pipework |
| Pressure test | checking a system for leaks under pressure |
| Backflow | contaminated water flowing back into the clean supply |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define each metric precisely — start with first-time fix rate — including source data in job sheets and collection method
2. **Step 2** - Set review cadence and comparison baselines so trends, not single data points, drive decisions
3. **Step 3** - Investigate movements before celebrating or panicking: check data quality, case mix and one-off causes
4. **Step 4** - Link metric movements to practice changes and to risks such as water damage from leaks; test one change at a time where possible
5. **Step 5** - Report results honestly to stakeholders in homes, building sites and commercial premises, including where outcomes fell short and what changes

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: water damage from leaks.
- Never let this happen: gas safety risks.
- Never let this happen: scalding from faulty systems.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a plumber; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to homes, building sites and commercial premises's rules.

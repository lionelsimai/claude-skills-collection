# Records & Documentation — Commercial Leasing

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with landlords, tenants and investors. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a commercial leasing agent working with landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices.

## When to use
- You are a commercial leasing agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial properties and agency offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Heads of terms | the agreed key points before a lease is drafted |
| Break clause | a right to end a lease early on set terms |
| Alienation | transferring or sharing a lease interest |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use heads of terms as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format commercial properties and agency offices requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed time to let exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in commercial properties and agency offices, guarding against misdescribed premises
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

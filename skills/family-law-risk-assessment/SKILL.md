# Risk Assessment — Family Law

Identify, score and control the risks in work for clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to missed court deadlines
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using case management systems where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in client care letters, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in a law firm and family court change

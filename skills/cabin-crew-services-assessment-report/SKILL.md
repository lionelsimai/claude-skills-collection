# Assessment & Findings Report — Cabin Crew Services

Assess airline passengers and flight crew and write a defensible findings report as a cabin crew member. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a cabin crew member working with airline passengers and flight crew in aircraft cabins.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and flight reports so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using safety equipment, following the recognised method for aircraft cabins
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind safety check compliance and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for evacuation readiness gaps; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: evacuation readiness gaps.
- Never let this happen: unruly passenger escalation.
- Never let this happen: galley injuries.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a cabin crew member; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to aircraft cabins's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

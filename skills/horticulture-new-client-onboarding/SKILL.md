# New Client Onboarding Pack — Horticulture

Onboard garden centres, landscapers and the public smoothly into a horticulturist and grower's service in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a horticulturist and grower working with garden centres, landscapers and the public in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.

## When to use
- You are a horticulturist and grower preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from planting plans
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on crop success rates will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to nurseries, glasshouses and gardens, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or pest outbreaks

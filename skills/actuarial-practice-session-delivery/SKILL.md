# Session Delivery Guide — Actuarial Practice

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a actuary working with insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices.

## When to use
- You are a actuary preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance and pensions offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review valuation reports before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as model errors affecting reserves
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and statistical software; check everything works and is safe before insurers, pension schemes and regulators arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to model validation results
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

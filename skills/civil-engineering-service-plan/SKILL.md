# Service Plan Design — Civil Engineering

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for infrastructure clients, contractors and the public as a civil engineer. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a civil engineer working with infrastructure clients, contractors and the public in engineering consultancies and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a civil engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in engineering consultancies and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with infrastructure clients, contractors and the public — specific enough to measure against design review completion
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for engineering consultancies and construction sites, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around design calculations so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, CAD and BIM software and materials, with contingencies for structural failures
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

# Referral & Coordination — Community Nursing

Refer patients receiving care in their own homes onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a community nurse working with patients receiving care in their own homes in patients' homes and community clinics.

## When to use
- You are a community nurse preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in patients' homes and community clinics.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what patients receiving care in their own homes need that sits outside the community nurse scope in patients' homes and community clinics
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from care plans — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and visit completion rate stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with patients receiving care in their own homes: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of lone working incidents

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in patients' homes and community clinics] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients receiving care in their own homes and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in care plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against visit completion rate.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in patients' homes and community clinics.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: lone working incidents.
- Never let this happen: missed deterioration at home.
- Never let this happen: medication errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a community nurse; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to patients' homes and community clinics's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

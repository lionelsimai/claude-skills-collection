# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Electrical Trades

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for homeowners, businesses and contractors are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a electrician working with homeowners, businesses and contractors in homes, sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a electrician preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Consumer unit | the distribution board controlling circuits in a property |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how first-time fix rate is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using multimeters where measurement is needed, and record it in installation certificates
3. **Step 3** - Give homeowners, businesses and contractors clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to electric shock shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

- Never let this happen: electric shock.
- Never let this happen: faulty installations causing fire.
- Never let this happen: damaged cables left live.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a electrician; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

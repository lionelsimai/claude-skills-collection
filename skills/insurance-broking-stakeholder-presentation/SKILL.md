# Stakeholder Presentation — Insurance Broking

Present insurance broker findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a insurance broker working with businesses and individuals buying insurance in brokerages and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a insurance broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in brokerages and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Placement | arranging cover with an insurer |
| Renewal | continuing a policy for a further period |
| Schedule | the document listing the specific cover details |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from needs assessments and movement in renewal retention, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including cover gaps at renewal and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in brokerages and client premises
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: cover gaps at renewal.
- Never let this happen: missed deadlines leaving clients uninsured.
- Never let this happen: advice errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a insurance broker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Urban Planning

Explain complex town planner matters to developers, councils and communities in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a town planner working with developers, councils and communities in planning departments and consultancy offices.

## When to use
- You are a town planner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in planning departments and consultancy offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Local plan | the statutory document guiding development in an area |
| Determination | the formal decision on a planning application |
| Material consideration | a planning factor legally relevant to a decision |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what developers, councils and communities actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind planning applications — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as missed consultation deadlines
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking developers, councils and communities to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in planning departments and consultancy offices] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [developers, councils and communities and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against application determination times.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in planning departments and consultancy offices.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed consultation deadlines.
- Never let this happen: policy conflicts.
- Never let this happen: appeals lost on process.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a town planner; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to planning departments and consultancy offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

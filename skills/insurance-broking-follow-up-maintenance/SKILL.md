# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Insurance Broking

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for businesses and individuals buying insurance are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a insurance broker working with businesses and individuals buying insurance in brokerages and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a insurance broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in brokerages and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Placement | arranging cover with an insurer |
| Renewal | continuing a policy for a further period |
| Schedule | the document listing the specific cover details |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how renewal retention is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using broking platforms where measurement is needed, and record it in needs assessments
3. **Step 3** - Give businesses and individuals buying insurance clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to cover gaps at renewal shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

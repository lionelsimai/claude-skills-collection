# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Chemical Engineering

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a chemical engineer relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a chemical engineer working with process plants and manufacturing clients in chemical plants and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a chemical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in chemical plants and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in chemical plants and design offices, centred on process simulators, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against process flow diagrams
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to process safety incidents
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

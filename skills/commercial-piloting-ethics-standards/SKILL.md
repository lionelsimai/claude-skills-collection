# Ethics & Professional Standards — Commercial Piloting

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a commercial pilot. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a commercial pilot working with airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations.

## When to use
- You are a commercial pilot preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in cockpits and flight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Flight plan | the planned route and details filed before a flight |
| Type rating | qualification to fly a specific aircraft type |
| Sterile cockpit | periods when only flight-critical conversation is allowed |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in cockpits and flight operations: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect airlines, passengers and cargo customers first where duties conflict, and be alert to how fatigue risks can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from flight plans, and support colleagues who do the same

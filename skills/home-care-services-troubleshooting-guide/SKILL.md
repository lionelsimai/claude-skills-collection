# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Home Care Services

Diagnose and resolve common problems a home care manager and carer meets in clients' homes. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a home care manager and carer working with people receiving care at home and their families in clients' homes.

## When to use
- You are a home care manager and carer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clients' homes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Call monitoring | verifying care visits happened as scheduled |
| Continuity | being cared for by familiar, regular carers |
| Double-up visit | a visit requiring two carers for safe support |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in care plans — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to missed visits leaving people unsafe early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using scheduling systems, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against visit punctuality that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in clients' homes] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [people receiving care at home and their families and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in care plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against visit punctuality.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in clients' homes.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed visits leaving people unsafe.
- Never let this happen: medication errors.
- Never let this happen: lone working incidents.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a home care manager and carer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clients' homes's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Session Delivery Guide — Masonry

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for builders, homeowners and heritage organisations in construction sites and historic buildings. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a mason and bricklayer working with builders, homeowners and heritage organisations in construction sites and historic buildings.

## When to use
- You are a mason and bricklayer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites and historic buildings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review laying records before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as structural defects from poor work
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and trowels and levels; check everything works and is safe before builders, homeowners and heritage organisations arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to work completion rates
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

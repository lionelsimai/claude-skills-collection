# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Mortgage Advisory

Explain complex mortgage adviser matters to home buyers and homeowners in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a mortgage adviser working with home buyers and homeowners in advice firms, branches and online.

## When to use
- You are a mortgage adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in advice firms, branches and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Affordability | assessment of whether a borrower can sustain repayments |
| Loan to value | the loan as a percentage of the property's value |
| Offer | the lender's formal agreement to lend |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what home buyers and homeowners actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind fact finds — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as unsuitable product recommendations
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking home buyers and homeowners to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unsuitable product recommendations.
- Never let this happen: affordability miscalculated.
- Never let this happen: deadlines missed losing purchases.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a mortgage adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to advice firms, branches and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

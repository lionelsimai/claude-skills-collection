# Session Delivery Guide — Oil & Gas Operations

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for energy companies and regulators in offshore platforms and onshore terminals. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a petroleum operations engineer working with energy companies and regulators in offshore platforms and onshore terminals.

## When to use
- You are a petroleum operations engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in offshore platforms and onshore terminals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Permit to work | a formal authorisation for hazardous work |
| Well control | preventing uncontrolled flow from a well |
| Shutdown | a planned or emergency stop of production |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review production reports before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as hydrocarbon releases
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and drilling and production equipment; check everything works and is safe before energy companies and regulators arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to production rates
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

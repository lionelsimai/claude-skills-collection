# Session Delivery Guide — Ship Navigation

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review voyage plans before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as groundings and collisions
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and ECDIS chart systems; check everything works and is safe before ship owners, crews and port authorities arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to voyage plan compliance
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

# Incident Reporting & Response — Actuarial Practice

Respond to, record and learn from incidents involving insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices. The deliverable is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
This skill is written for a actuary working with insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices.

## When to use
- You are a actuary preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance and pensions offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if model errors affecting reserves is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in valuation reports
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in insurance and pensions offices
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with insurers, pension schemes and regulators about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: model errors affecting reserves.
- Never let this happen: stale assumptions.
- Never let this happen: regulatory breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a actuary; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to insurance and pensions offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

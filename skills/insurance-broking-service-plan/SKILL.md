# Service Plan Design — Insurance Broking

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for businesses and individuals buying insurance as a insurance broker. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a insurance broker working with businesses and individuals buying insurance in brokerages and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a insurance broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in brokerages and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Placement | arranging cover with an insurer |
| Renewal | continuing a policy for a further period |
| Schedule | the document listing the specific cover details |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with businesses and individuals buying insurance — specific enough to measure against renewal retention
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for brokerages and client premises, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around needs assessments so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, broking platforms and materials, with contingencies for cover gaps at renewal
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in brokerages and client premises] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [businesses and individuals buying insurance and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in needs assessments.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against renewal retention.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in brokerages and client premises.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: cover gaps at renewal.
- Never let this happen: missed deadlines leaving clients uninsured.
- Never let this happen: advice errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a insurance broker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to brokerages and client premises's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

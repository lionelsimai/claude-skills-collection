# Quality Audit — Actuarial Practice

Audit the quality of actuary work in insurance and pensions offices and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a actuary working with insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices.

## When to use
- You are a actuary preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance and pensions offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to model validation results and the standards for insurance and pensions offices
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of valuation reports and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including model errors affecting reserves
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

# Assessment & Findings Report — Plumbing

Assess homeowners, landlords and commercial clients and write a defensible findings report as a plumber. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a plumber working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, building sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a plumber preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, building sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and job sheets so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using pipe threading tools, following the recognised method for homes, building sites and commercial premises
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind first-time fix rate and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for water damage from leaks; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

# Referral & Coordination — Estate Agency

Refer home sellers, buyers and landlords onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a estate agent working with home sellers, buyers and landlords in estate agency offices and properties.

## When to use
- You are a estate agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in estate agency offices and properties.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Instruction | being appointed to sell a property |
| Particulars | the marketing description of a property |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what home sellers, buyers and landlords need that sits outside the estate agent scope in estate agency offices and properties
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from market appraisals — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and instruction conversion stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with home sellers, buyers and landlords: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of misrepresentation of properties

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misrepresentation of properties.
- Never let this happen: offers not passed on.
- Never let this happen: data protection breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a estate agent; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to estate agency offices and properties's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

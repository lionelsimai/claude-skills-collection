# Scheduling & Workload Management — Warehouse Management

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a warehouse manager in warehouses and distribution centres. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a warehouse manager working with retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients in warehouses and distribution centres.

## When to use
- You are a warehouse manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in warehouses and distribution centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Pick accuracy | how often the correct items are picked |
| Goods-in | receiving and checking deliveries |
| Cycle counting | counting sections of stock on a rolling basis |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive pick accuracy
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in warehouses and distribution centres first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind inventory records — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving forklift accidents) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or warehouse management systems availability

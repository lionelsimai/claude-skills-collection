# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Strata Management

Diagnose and resolve common problems a strata manager meets in residential complexes and management offices. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a strata manager working with lot owners, owners corporations and residents in residential complexes and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a strata manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in residential complexes and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Levy | owners' regular contribution to shared costs |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in meeting minutes — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to fund mismanagement early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using strata software, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against levy collection rates that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

- Never let this happen: fund mismanagement.
- Never let this happen: meeting procedure errors.
- Never let this happen: by-law disputes escalated.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a strata manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to residential complexes and management offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

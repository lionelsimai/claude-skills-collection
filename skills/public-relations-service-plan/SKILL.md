# Service Plan Design — Public Relations

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for clients, journalists and stakeholders as a public relations consultant. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a public relations consultant working with clients, journalists and stakeholders in PR agencies and client organisations.

## When to use
- You are a public relations consultant preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in PR agencies and client organisations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Embargo | information released for publication at a set future time |
| Coverage | media reporting about a client or story |
| Key message | a core point a campaign aims to communicate |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with clients, journalists and stakeholders — specific enough to measure against coverage quality and reach
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for PR agencies and client organisations, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around media lists so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, media databases and materials, with contingencies for inaccurate statements to media
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Public Relations |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | media lists; press releases; campaign plans; coverage reports |
| Core tools & equipment | media databases; monitoring tools; social platforms; design tools |
| People involved | clients, journalists and stakeholders; the public relations consultant; colleagues and other services in PR agencies and client organisations |
| Primary record | media lists — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in PR agencies and client organisations] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [clients, journalists and stakeholders and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in media lists.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against coverage quality and reach.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in PR agencies and client organisations.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: inaccurate statements to media.
- Never let this happen: crises mishandled.
- Never let this happen: embargo breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a public relations consultant; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to PR agencies and client organisations's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

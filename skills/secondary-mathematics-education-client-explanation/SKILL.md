# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Secondary Mathematics Education

Explain complex secondary mathematics teacher matters to secondary school students and their parents in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a secondary mathematics teacher working with secondary school students and their parents in a secondary school mathematics department.

## When to use
- You are a secondary mathematics teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a secondary school mathematics department.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Scheme of work | the planned sequence of topics across a term or year |
| Intervention | targeted extra teaching for pupils who are behind |
| Working mathematically | applying maths to problem solving, not just procedures |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what secondary school students and their parents actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind schemes of work — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as misconceptions unaddressed
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking secondary school students and their parents to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a secondary school mathematics department] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [secondary school students and their parents and other stakeholders]

2. Detail, following the workflow above

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misconceptions unaddressed.
- Never let this happen: exam entry errors.
- Never let this happen: low-attaining pupils overlooked.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a secondary mathematics teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a secondary school mathematics department's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

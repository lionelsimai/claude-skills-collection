# Safety Protocol — Mine Operations

Work safely in mines and processing plants: a practical safety protocol for a mine operations manager and mining companies and mineral buyers. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a mine operations manager working with mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a mine operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mines and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in mines and processing plants for staff, mining companies and mineral buyers and visitors, starting with underground incidents
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as mining machinery
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in production reports
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

# Assessment & Findings Report — Environmental Law

Assess developers, businesses and community groups and write a defensible findings report as a environmental solicitor. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a environmental solicitor working with developers, businesses and community groups in a law firm and planning inquiries.

## When to use
- You are a environmental solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and planning inquiries.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and permit applications so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using planning portals, following the recognised method for a law firm and planning inquiries
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind permit success rates and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for permit breaches unadvised; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

- Never let this happen: consultation deadlines missed.
- Never let this happen: contaminated land liabilities.

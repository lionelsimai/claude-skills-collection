# Progress Review & Adjustment — Strata Management

Review progress for lot owners, owners corporations and residents against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a strata manager working with lot owners, owners corporations and residents in residential complexes and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a strata manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in residential complexes and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Levy | owners' regular contribution to shared costs |
| By-laws | the rules governing behaviour and use in a strata scheme |
| Quorum | the minimum attendance for a meeting to make decisions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together meeting minutes and the latest results for levy collection rates since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with lot owners, owners corporations and residents honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in residential complexes and management offices

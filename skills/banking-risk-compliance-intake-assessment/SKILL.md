# Intake & Initial Assessment — Banking Risk & Compliance

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for banks, regulators and boards in bank risk and compliance departments. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a risk and compliance officer working with banks, regulators and boards in bank risk and compliance departments.

## When to use
- You are a risk and compliance officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank risk and compliance departments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Risk register | the recorded list of risks, owners and controls |
| Three lines | the model separating risk ownership, oversight and audit |
| Conduct risk | the risk of harm to customers or markets from behaviour |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold risk registers
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from banks, regulators and boards, using the standard intake structure for bank risk and compliance departments
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to monitoring completion, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially undetected misconduct — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for banks, regulators and boards in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of risk registers

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in bank risk and compliance departments] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [banks, regulators and boards and other stakeholders]

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: undetected misconduct.
- Never let this happen: late regulatory returns.
- Never let this happen: control failures.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a risk and compliance officer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank risk and compliance departments's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

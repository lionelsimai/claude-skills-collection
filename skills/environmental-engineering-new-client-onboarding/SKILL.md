# New Client Onboarding Pack — Environmental Engineering

Onboard water companies, industries and regulators smoothly into a environmental engineer's service in treatment plants, client sites and design offices. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a environmental engineer working with water companies, industries and regulators in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a environmental engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Consent | permission to discharge treated water or waste |
| Effluent | liquid waste discharged from a process |
| Remediation | cleaning up contaminated land or water |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from impact assessments
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on permit compliance will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to treatment plants, client sites and design offices, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or pollution incidents

- Never let this happen: pollution incidents.
- Never let this happen: consent breaches.
- Never let this happen: design underperformance.

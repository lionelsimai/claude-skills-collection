# Service Plan Design — Financial Planning

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for individuals and families planning their financial future as a financial planner. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a financial planner working with individuals and families planning their financial future in a financial planning firm and client meetings.

## When to use
- You are a financial planner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a financial planning firm and client meetings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Fact find | the detailed gathering of a client's circumstances and goals |
| Suitability | how well a recommendation matches a client's needs and risk tolerance |
| Cashflow modelling | projecting income, spending and assets over a lifetime |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with individuals and families planning their financial future — specific enough to measure against review completion rates
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for a financial planning firm and client meetings, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around fact finds so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, cashflow modelling software and materials, with contingencies for unsuitable recommendations
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a financial planning firm and client meetings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [individuals and families planning their financial future and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in fact finds.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against review completion rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a financial planning firm and client meetings.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unsuitable recommendations.
- Never let this happen: risk profiles mismatched.
- Never let this happen: reviews overdue.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a financial planner; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a financial planning firm and client meetings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

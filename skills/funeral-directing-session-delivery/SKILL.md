# Session Delivery Guide — Funeral Directing

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for bereaved families in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a funeral director working with bereaved families in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.

## When to use
- You are a funeral director preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Arrangement | the meeting where a funeral is planned |
| Embalming | preserving and preparing a deceased person |
| Cortege | the procession of vehicles at a funeral |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review funeral arrangements before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as wrong details in services
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and chapel facilities; check everything works and is safe before bereaved families arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to arrangement accuracy
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: wrong details in services.
- Never let this happen: documentation errors delaying funerals.
- Never let this happen: identification mistakes.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a funeral director; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries's rules.

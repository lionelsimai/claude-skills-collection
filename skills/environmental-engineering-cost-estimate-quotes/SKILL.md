# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Environmental Engineering

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a environmental engineer. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a environmental engineer working with water companies, industries and regulators in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a environmental engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Consent | permission to discharge treated water or waste |
| Effluent | liquid waste discharged from a process |
| Remediation | cleaning up contaminated land or water |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and impact assessments; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, modelling software usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against pollution incidents
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as permit compliance
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

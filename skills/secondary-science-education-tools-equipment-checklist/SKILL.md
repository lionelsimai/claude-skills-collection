# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Secondary Science Education

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a secondary science teacher relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a secondary science teacher working with secondary school students in a school science department and laboratories.

## When to use
- You are a secondary science teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a school science department and laboratories.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in a school science department and laboratories, centred on laboratory equipment, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against schemes of work
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to practical safety incidents
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: practical safety incidents.
- Never let this happen: misconceptions in core concepts.
- Never let this happen: equipment faults.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a secondary science teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a school science department and laboratories's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

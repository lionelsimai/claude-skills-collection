# Safety Protocol — Strata Management

Work safely in residential complexes and management offices: a practical safety protocol for a strata manager and lot owners, owners corporations and residents. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a strata manager working with lot owners, owners corporations and residents in residential complexes and management offices.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in residential complexes and management offices for staff, lot owners, owners corporations and residents and visitors, starting with fund mismanagement
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as strata software
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in meeting minutes
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

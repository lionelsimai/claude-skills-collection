# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Locksmith Services

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a locksmith relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a locksmith working with homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners in clients' premises and vehicles.

## When to use
- You are a locksmith preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clients' premises and vehicles.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Non-destructive entry | opening a lock without damaging it |
| Cylinder | the part of a lock the key turns |
| Master key | a key that opens multiple locks in a system |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in clients' premises and vehicles, centred on lock picking tools, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against job records
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to damage during entry
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

- Never let this happen: unauthorised access by impostors.

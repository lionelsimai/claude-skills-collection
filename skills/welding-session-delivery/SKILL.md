# Session Delivery Guide — Welding

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for fabrication companies, construction and repair clients in workshops, shipyards and construction sites. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a welder working with fabrication companies, construction and repair clients in workshops, shipyards and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a welder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in workshops, shipyards and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| WPS | a weld procedure specification defining how a weld is made |
| Penetration | how deeply a weld fuses into the base metal |
| Slag | a residue that must be removed from some welds |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review weld procedure specifications before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as weld failures in service
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and welding machines; check everything works and is safe before fabrication companies, construction and repair clients arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to weld defect rates
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: weld failures in service.
- Never let this happen: eye and skin burns.
- Never let this happen: fumes exposure.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a welder; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to workshops, shipyards and construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Clinical Research

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a clinical research practitioner. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a clinical research practitioner working with trial participants, sponsors and investigators in hospitals and research sites.

## When to use
- You are a clinical research practitioner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hospitals and research sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Protocol | the detailed plan for a clinical trial |
| Informed consent | a participant's voluntary agreement after full explanation |
| Adverse event | an untoward medical occurrence in a participant |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and protocols; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, electronic data capture usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against consent failures
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as recruitment to target
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in hospitals and research sites] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [trial participants, sponsors and investigators and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in protocols.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against recruitment to target.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in hospitals and research sites.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: consent failures.
- Never let this happen: protocol deviations unreported.
- Never let this happen: participant safety signals missed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a clinical research practitioner; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hospitals and research sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

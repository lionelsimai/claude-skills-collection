# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Tax Advisory

Diagnose and resolve common problems a tax adviser meets in an accountancy or advisory firm. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a tax adviser working with individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## When to use
- You are a tax adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy or advisory firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in tax computations — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to missed filing deadlines early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using tax software, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against filing deadline compliance that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed filing deadlines.
- Never let this happen: aggressive schemes challenged.
- Never let this happen: relief claims overlooked.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a tax adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to an accountancy or advisory firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Formal Report Writing — Estate Agency

Write formal reports as a estate agent that stand up to scrutiny from any reader. The deliverable is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
This skill is written for a estate agent working with home sellers, buyers and landlords in estate agency offices and properties.

## When to use
- You are a estate agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in estate agency offices and properties.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Instruction | being appointed to sell a property |
| Particulars | the marketing description of a property |
| Chain | a sequence of linked property sales dependent on each other |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Establish the report's purpose, audience and deadline before writing; gather source material from market appraisals
2. **Step 2** - Structure: summary, background, method, findings, analysis, recommendations — in that order
3. **Step 3** - Write findings factually and separately from opinion; label professional judgement as judgement
4. **Step 4** - Support key points with evidence and with instruction conversion where outcomes are claimed; acknowledge limitations openly
5. **Step 5** - Proofread for accuracy and tone, check names, dates and figures twice, and consider misrepresentation of properties before release

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misrepresentation of properties.
- Never let this happen: offers not passed on.
- Never let this happen: data protection breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a estate agent; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to estate agency offices and properties's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

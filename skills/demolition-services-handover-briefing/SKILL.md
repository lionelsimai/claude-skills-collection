# Handover Briefing — Demolition Services

Hand over developers, councils and property owners or active work to another professional without losing critical detail. The deliverable is a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions.
This skill is written for a demolition contractor working with developers, councils and property owners in demolition sites.

## When to use
- You are a demolition contractor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in demolition sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Soft strip | removing non-structural elements before demolition |
| Asbestos survey | inspection identifying asbestos before work starts |
| Arisings | materials produced by demolition for reuse or disposal |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Summarise the current position from demolition plans: what is done, what is in progress, what is waiting
2. **Step 2** - Highlight live risks first — asbestos exposure — and the controls or watchpoints already in place
3. **Step 3** - State the next actions with owners and deadlines, and the results expected for project completion times
4. **Step 4** - Walk the receiving professional through the brief, answer questions, and confirm understanding
5. **Step 5** - Record that handover happened, who accepted it, and where the full record lives in demolition sites

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in demolition sites] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [developers, councils and property owners and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: asbestos exposure.
- Never let this happen: uncontrolled collapse.
- Never let this happen: dust and noise breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a demolition contractor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to demolition sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

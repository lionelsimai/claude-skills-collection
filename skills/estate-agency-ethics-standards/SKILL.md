# Ethics & Professional Standards — Estate Agency

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a estate agent. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a estate agent working with home sellers, buyers and landlords in estate agency offices and properties.

## When to use
- You are a estate agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in estate agency offices and properties.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Instruction | being appointed to sell a property |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in estate agency offices and properties: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect home sellers, buyers and landlords first where duties conflict, and be alert to how misrepresentation of properties can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from market appraisals, and support colleagues who do the same

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misrepresentation of properties.
- Never let this happen: offers not passed on.
- Never let this happen: data protection breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a estate agent; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to estate agency offices and properties's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

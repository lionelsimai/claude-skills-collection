# Emergency Response Procedure — Building Inspection

Respond to emergencies in properties and construction sites calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a building inspector and surveyor working with homeowners, buyers and local authorities in properties and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a building inspector and surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in properties and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for homeowners, buyers and local authorities, especially serious defects missed
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a building inspector and surveyor is trained and authorised for, using moisture meters as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in properties and construction sites, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep homeowners, buyers and local authorities and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in survey reports afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

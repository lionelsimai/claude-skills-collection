# Intake & Initial Assessment — Primary Education

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for primary-aged pupils and their parents in a primary school classroom. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a primary school teacher working with primary-aged pupils and their parents in a primary school classroom.

## When to use
- You are a primary school teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a primary school classroom.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold lesson plans
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from primary-aged pupils and their parents, using the standard intake structure for a primary school classroom
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to pupil progress measures, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially safeguarding disclosures mishandled — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for primary-aged pupils and their parents in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of lesson plans

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a primary school classroom] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [primary-aged pupils and their parents and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against pupil progress measures.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a primary school classroom.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding disclosures mishandled.
- Never let this happen: gaps in progress unaddressed.
- Never let this happen: workload overload.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a primary school teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a primary school classroom's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

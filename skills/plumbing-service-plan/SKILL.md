# Service Plan Design — Plumbing

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for homeowners, landlords and commercial clients as a plumber. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a plumber working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, building sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a plumber preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, building sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| First fix | the initial installation stage of pipework |
| Pressure test | checking a system for leaks under pressure |
| Backflow | contaminated water flowing back into the clean supply |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients — specific enough to measure against first-time fix rate
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for homes, building sites and commercial premises, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around job sheets so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, pipe threading tools and materials, with contingencies for water damage from leaks
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format

1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

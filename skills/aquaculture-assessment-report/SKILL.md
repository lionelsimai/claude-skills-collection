# Assessment & Findings Report — Aquaculture

Assess seafood buyers and processors and write a defensible findings report as a fish farm manager. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## When to use
- You are a fish farm manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and stock records so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using feeding systems, following the recognised method for lakes, coastal sites and fish farms
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind growth rates and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for disease outbreaks in stock; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: disease outbreaks in stock.
- Never let this happen: escapes into the wild.
- Never let this happen: water quality crashes.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a fish farm manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to lakes, coastal sites and fish farms's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

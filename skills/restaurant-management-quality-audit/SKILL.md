# Quality Audit — Restaurant Management

Audit the quality of restaurant manager work in restaurants and cafes and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to food cost percentage and the standards for restaurants and cafes
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of menus and costing sheets and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including food hygiene failures
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

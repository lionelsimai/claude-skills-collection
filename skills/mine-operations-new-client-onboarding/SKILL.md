# New Client Onboarding Pack — Mine Operations

Onboard mining companies and mineral buyers smoothly into a mine operations manager's service in mines and processing plants. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a mine operations manager working with mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a mine operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mines and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from production reports
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on production tonnage will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to mines and processing plants, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or underground incidents

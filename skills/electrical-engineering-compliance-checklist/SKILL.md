# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Electrical Engineering

Check work in design offices, plants and substations against the laws, standards and codes that govern a electrical engineer. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a electrical engineer working with energy, manufacturing and building clients in design offices, plants and substations.

- You are a electrical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, plants and substations.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in design offices, plants and substations
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and circuit designs, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching electric shock risks — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

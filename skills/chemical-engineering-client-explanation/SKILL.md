# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Chemical Engineering

Explain complex chemical engineer matters to process plants and manufacturing clients in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a chemical engineer working with process plants and manufacturing clients in chemical plants and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a chemical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in chemical plants and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| HAZOP | a systematic study of hazards and operability problems |
| Yield | the amount of desired product obtained from a process |
| Mass balance | accounting for all material entering and leaving a process |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what process plants and manufacturing clients actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind process flow diagrams — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as process safety incidents
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking process plants and manufacturing clients to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

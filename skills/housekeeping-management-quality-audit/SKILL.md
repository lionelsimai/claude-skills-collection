# Quality Audit — Housekeeping Management

Audit the quality of housekeeping manager work in hotels and serviced apartments and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a housekeeping manager working with hotel guests and housekeeping teams in hotels and serviced apartments.

## When to use
- You are a housekeeping manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hotels and serviced apartments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to inspection pass rates and the standards for hotels and serviced apartments
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of room inspection checklists and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including chemical injuries
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

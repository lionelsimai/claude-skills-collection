# New Client Onboarding Pack — Construction Site Management

Onboard clients, contractors and construction teams smoothly into a site manager's service in construction sites. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a site manager working with clients, contractors and construction teams in construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a site manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from site diaries
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on programme adherence will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to construction sites, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or site accidents

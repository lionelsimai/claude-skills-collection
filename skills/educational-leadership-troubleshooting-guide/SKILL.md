# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Educational Leadership

Diagnose and resolve common problems a headteacher and school leader meets in a school or multi-school trust. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a headteacher and school leader working with schools, staff, pupils and governing bodies in a school or multi-school trust.

## When to use
- You are a headteacher and school leader preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a school or multi-school trust.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in school improvement plans — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to safeguarding failures early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using management information systems, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against inspection outcomes that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a school or multi-school trust] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [schools, staff, pupils and governing bodies and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in school improvement plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against inspection outcomes.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a school or multi-school trust.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding failures.
- Never let this happen: budget deficits.
- Never let this happen: staff burnout.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a headteacher and school leader; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a school or multi-school trust's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Assessment & Findings Report — Water Treatment

Assess the public, via water companies and write a defensible findings report as a water treatment operator. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a water treatment operator working with the public, via water companies in treatment works and pumping stations.

## When to use
- You are a water treatment operator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in treatment works and pumping stations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and process logs so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using treatment plant systems, following the recognised method for treatment works and pumping stations
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind water quality compliance and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for contamination reaching supply; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

# Feedback & Complaints Handling — Oil & Gas Operations

Collect feedback from energy companies and regulators and handle complaints fairly and visibly. The deliverable is a feedback and complaints process with response standards and learning log.
This skill is written for a petroleum operations engineer working with energy companies and regulators in offshore platforms and onshore terminals.

## When to use
- You are a petroleum operations engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in offshore platforms and onshore terminals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a feedback and complaints process with response standards and learning log.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Permit to work | a formal authorisation for hazardous work |
| Well control | preventing uncontrolled flow from a well |
| Shutdown | a planned or emergency stop of production |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Invite feedback routinely — not only when things go wrong — and make it easy to give in offshore platforms and onshore terminals
2. **Step 2** - Acknowledge complaints quickly, thank the person, and explain the process and timeframe honestly
3. **Step 3** - Investigate from evidence in production reports; separate the facts from the feelings, and respect both
4. **Step 4** - Respond with findings, an apology where warranted, and concrete remedies or explanations
5. **Step 5** - Log themes and feed them into quality work — repeated complaints about hydrocarbon releases are an early-warning system, not a nuisance

- Never let this happen: well control incidents.
- Never let this happen: permit breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a petroleum operations engineer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

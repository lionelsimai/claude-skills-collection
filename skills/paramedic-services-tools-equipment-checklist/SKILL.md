# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Paramedic Services

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a paramedic relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a paramedic working with patients with emergency and urgent care needs in the community in ambulances and community settings.

## When to use
- You are a paramedic preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in ambulances and community settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Conveyance | transporting a patient to hospital |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in ambulances and community settings, centred on ambulance equipment, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against patient report forms
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to scene safety hazards
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

- Never let this happen: scene safety hazards.
- Never let this happen: delayed conveyance decisions.
- Never let this happen: incomplete handovers.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a paramedic; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to ambulances and community settings's rules.

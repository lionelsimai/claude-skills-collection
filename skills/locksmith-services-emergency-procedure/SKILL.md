# Emergency Response Procedure — Locksmith Services

Respond to emergencies in clients' premises and vehicles calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a locksmith working with homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners in clients' premises and vehicles.

## When to use
- You are a locksmith preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clients' premises and vehicles.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners, especially damage during entry
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a locksmith is trained and authorised for, using lock picking tools as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in clients' premises and vehicles, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in job records afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

# Formal Report Writing — Waste Management

Write formal reports as a waste operations manager that stand up to scrutiny from any reader. The deliverable is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
This skill is written for a waste operations manager working with households, businesses and councils in waste facilities and collection rounds.

## When to use
- You are a waste operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in waste facilities and collection rounds.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Establish the report's purpose, audience and deadline before writing; gather source material from waste transfer notes
2. **Step 2** - Structure: summary, background, method, findings, analysis, recommendations — in that order
3. **Step 3** - Write findings factually and separately from opinion; label professional judgement as judgement
4. **Step 4** - Support key points with evidence and with collection completion where outcomes are claimed; acknowledge limitations openly
5. **Step 5** - Proofread for accuracy and tone, check names, dates and figures twice, and consider hazardous waste mishandling before release

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in waste facilities and collection rounds] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [households, businesses and councils and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against collection completion.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: hazardous waste mishandling.
- Never let this happen: vehicle accidents.
- Never let this happen: site fires.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a waste operations manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to waste facilities and collection rounds's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

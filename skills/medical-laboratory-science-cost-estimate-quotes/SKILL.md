# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Medical Laboratory Science

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a biomedical scientist. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a biomedical scientist working with patients whose diagnosis depends on laboratory results, via clinical teams in a hospital pathology laboratory.

## When to use
- You are a biomedical scientist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a hospital pathology laboratory.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Quality control | checks that a test system is performing correctly |
| Turnaround time | time from sample receipt to result |
| Haemolysis | breakdown of red blood cells that can spoil a sample |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and test requests; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, laboratory analysers usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against sample mislabelling
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as result turnaround time
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: sample mislabelling.
- Never let this happen: delayed critical results.
- Never let this happen: analyser faults unnoticed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a biomedical scientist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a hospital pathology laboratory's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

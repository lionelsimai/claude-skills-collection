# Safety Protocol — Commercial Piloting

Work safely in cockpits and flight operations: a practical safety protocol for a commercial pilot and airlines, passengers and cargo customers. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a commercial pilot working with airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations.

## When to use
- You are a commercial pilot preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in cockpits and flight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in cockpits and flight operations for staff, airlines, passengers and cargo customers and visitors, starting with fatigue risks
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as aircraft systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in flight plans
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

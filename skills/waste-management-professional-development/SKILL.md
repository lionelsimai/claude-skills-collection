# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Waste Management

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising waste operations manager. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a waste operations manager working with households, businesses and councils in waste facilities and collection rounds.

## When to use
- You are a waste operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in waste facilities and collection rounds.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for waste facilities and collection rounds and the evidence in your recent waste transfer notes
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on hazardous waste mishandling — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for collection completion
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in waste facilities and collection rounds] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [households, businesses and councils and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in waste transfer notes.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against collection completion.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in waste facilities and collection rounds.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: hazardous waste mishandling.
- Never let this happen: vehicle accidents.
- Never let this happen: site fires.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a waste operations manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to waste facilities and collection rounds's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

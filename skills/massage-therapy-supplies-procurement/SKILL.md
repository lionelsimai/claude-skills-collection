# Supplies & Procurement — Massage Therapy

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in clinics, spas and sports settings. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a massage therapist working with clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation in clinics, spas and sports settings.

## When to use
- You are a massage therapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, spas and sports settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Contraindication | a condition making a treatment unsafe or requiring adaptation |
| Deep tissue | massage working on deeper layers of muscle |
| Aftercare | advice given to a client following treatment |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on client health forms and the kit centred on massage tables, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to contraindications missed causing harm
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

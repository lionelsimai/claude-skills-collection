# Referral & Coordination — Restaurant Management

Refer diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams need that sits outside the restaurant manager scope in restaurants and cafes
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from menus and costing sheets — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and food cost percentage stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of food hygiene failures

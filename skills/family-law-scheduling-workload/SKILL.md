# Scheduling & Workload Management — Family Law

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a family law solicitor in a law firm and family court. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive case progression times
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in a law firm and family court first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind client care letters — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving missed court deadlines) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or case management systems availability

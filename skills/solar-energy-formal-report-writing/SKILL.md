# Formal Report Writing — Solar Energy

Write formal reports as a solar PV engineer that stand up to scrutiny from any reader. The deliverable is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
This skill is written for a solar PV engineer working with homeowners, businesses and energy companies in rooftops, solar farms and offices.

## When to use
- You are a solar PV engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in rooftops, solar farms and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Establish the report's purpose, audience and deadline before writing; gather source material from system designs
2. **Step 2** - Structure: summary, background, method, findings, analysis, recommendations — in that order
3. **Step 3** - Write findings factually and separately from opinion; label professional judgement as judgement
4. **Step 4** - Support key points with evidence and with system performance ratio where outcomes are claimed; acknowledge limitations openly
5. **Step 5** - Proofread for accuracy and tone, check names, dates and figures twice, and consider falls from roofs before release

- Never let this happen: electrical faults.
- Never let this happen: underperforming systems unnoticed.

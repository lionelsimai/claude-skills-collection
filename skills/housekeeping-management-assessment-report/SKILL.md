# Assessment & Findings Report — Housekeeping Management

Assess hotel guests and housekeeping teams and write a defensible findings report as a housekeeping manager. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a housekeeping manager working with hotel guests and housekeeping teams in hotels and serviced apartments.

## When to use
- You are a housekeeping manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hotels and serviced apartments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and room inspection checklists so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using housekeeping software, following the recognised method for hotels and serviced apartments
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind inspection pass rates and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for chemical injuries; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: chemical injuries.
- Never let this happen: guest property losses.
- Never let this happen: missed safety hazards in rooms.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a housekeeping manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hotels and serviced apartments's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

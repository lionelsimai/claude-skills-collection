# Referral & Coordination — Locksmith Services

Refer homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a locksmith working with homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners in clients' premises and vehicles.

## When to use
- You are a locksmith preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clients' premises and vehicles.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Non-destructive entry | opening a lock without damaging it |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners need that sits outside the locksmith scope in clients' premises and vehicles
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from job records — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and response times stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of damage during entry

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: damage during entry.
- Never let this happen: unauthorised access by impostors.
- Never let this happen: security advice gaps.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a locksmith; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clients' premises and vehicles's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

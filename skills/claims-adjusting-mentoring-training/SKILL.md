# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Claims Adjusting

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe claims adjuster practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a claims adjuster working with policyholders, insurers and repairers in insurance offices and loss sites.

## When to use
- You are a claims adjuster preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance offices and loss sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Reserve | money set aside for the expected cost of a claim |
| Leakage | claim payments higher than they should be |
| Indemnity | restoring the insured to their pre-loss position |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in insurance offices and loss sites
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using claims systems under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching fraudulent claims paid — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from claims files, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in insurance offices and loss sites] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [policyholders, insurers and repairers and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against claims cycle time.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in insurance offices and loss sites.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: fraudulent claims paid.
- Never let this happen: under-reserving.
- Never let this happen: distressed customers mishandled.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a claims adjuster; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to insurance offices and loss sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

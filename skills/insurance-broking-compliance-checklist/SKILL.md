# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Insurance Broking

Check work in brokerages and client premises against the laws, standards and codes that govern a insurance broker. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a insurance broker working with businesses and individuals buying insurance in brokerages and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a insurance broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in brokerages and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in brokerages and client premises
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and needs assessments, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching cover gaps at renewal — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

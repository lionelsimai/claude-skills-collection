# Service Plan Design — Sustainability Management

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for businesses, investors and regulators as a sustainability manager. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a sustainability manager working with businesses, investors and regulators in organisations across all sectors.

## When to use
- You are a sustainability manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in organisations across all sectors.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Scope 3 | indirect emissions in a value chain |
| Carbon inventory | a measured record of an organisation's emissions |
| Assurance | independent checking of reported sustainability data |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with businesses, investors and regulators — specific enough to measure against emissions reduction progress
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for organisations across all sectors, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around carbon inventories so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, carbon accounting software and materials, with contingencies for greenwashing accusations
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in organisations across all sectors] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [businesses, investors and regulators and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in carbon inventories.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against emissions reduction progress.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in organisations across all sectors.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: greenwashing accusations.
- Never let this happen: data gaps in reporting.
- Never let this happen: missed regulatory deadlines.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a sustainability manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to organisations across all sectors's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

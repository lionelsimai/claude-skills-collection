# Emergency Response Procedure — Academic Research

Respond to emergencies in a university or research institute calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a academic researcher working with funders, collaborators and the wider research community in a university or research institute.

## When to use
- You are a academic researcher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a university or research institute.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for funders, collaborators and the wider research community, especially ethics breaches
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a academic researcher is trained and authorised for, using statistical software as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in a university or research institute, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep funders, collaborators and the wider research community and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in research proposals afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a university or research institute] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [funders, collaborators and the wider research community and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against grant success rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a university or research institute.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: ethics breaches.
- Never let this happen: data loss.
- Never let this happen: unreproducible results.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a academic researcher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a university or research institute's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Feedback & Complaints Handling — Solar Energy

Collect feedback from homeowners, businesses and energy companies and handle complaints fairly and visibly. The deliverable is a feedback and complaints process with response standards and learning log.
This skill is written for a solar PV engineer working with homeowners, businesses and energy companies in rooftops, solar farms and offices.

## When to use
- You are a solar PV engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in rooftops, solar farms and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a feedback and complaints process with response standards and learning log.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Inverter | the device converting panel DC output to usable AC |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Invite feedback routinely — not only when things go wrong — and make it easy to give in rooftops, solar farms and offices
2. **Step 2** - Acknowledge complaints quickly, thank the person, and explain the process and timeframe honestly
3. **Step 3** - Investigate from evidence in system designs; separate the facts from the feelings, and respect both
4. **Step 4** - Respond with findings, an apology where warranted, and concrete remedies or explanations
5. **Step 5** - Log themes and feed them into quality work — repeated complaints about falls from roofs are an early-warning system, not a nuisance

- Never let this happen: electrical faults.
- Never let this happen: underperforming systems unnoticed.

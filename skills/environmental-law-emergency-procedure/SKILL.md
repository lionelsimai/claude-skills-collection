# Emergency Response Procedure — Environmental Law

This skill is written for a environmental solicitor working with developers, businesses and community groups in a law firm and planning inquiries.

2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a environmental solicitor is trained and authorised for, using planning portals as drilled

# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Criminal Defence Law

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe criminal defence solicitor practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a criminal defence solicitor working with clients accused of criminal offences in police stations, law firms and criminal courts.

## When to use
- You are a criminal defence solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in police stations, law firms and criminal courts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in police stations, law firms and criminal courts
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using case management systems under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching missed disclosure deadlines — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from police station attendance notes, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

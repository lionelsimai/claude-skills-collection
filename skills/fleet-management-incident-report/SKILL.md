# Incident Reporting & Response — Fleet Management

Respond to, record and learn from incidents involving businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices. The deliverable is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
This skill is written for a fleet manager working with businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices.

## When to use
- You are a fleet manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in transport depots and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Telematics | technology tracking vehicle location and use |
| Utilisation | how much of the time vehicles are productively used |
| Defect report | a driver's record of vehicle faults |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if unroadworthy vehicles used is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in vehicle records
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in transport depots and offices
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in transport depots and offices] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unroadworthy vehicles used.
- Never let this happen: driver fatigue.
- Never let this happen: maintenance backlogs.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a fleet manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to transport depots and offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

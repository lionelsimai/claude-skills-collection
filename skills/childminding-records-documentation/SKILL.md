# Records & Documentation — Childminding

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with young children and their parents. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a registered childminder working with young children and their parents in the childminder's home.

## When to use
- You are a registered childminder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in the childminder's home.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Ratio | the legal limit of children per adult |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use learning records as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format the childminder's home requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed occupancy rates exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in the childminder's home, guarding against safeguarding concerns missed
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in the childminder's home] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [young children and their parents and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against occupancy rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in the childminder's home.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding concerns missed.
- Never let this happen: accidents from unsafe environments.
- Never let this happen: ratio breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a registered childminder; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to the childminder's home's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

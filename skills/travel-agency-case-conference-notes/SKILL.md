# Case Conference & Meeting Notes — Travel Agency Practice

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Prepare a one-page brief from booking records: current position, key evidence, the questions the meeting must answer
2. **Step 2** - Present your contribution concisely, leading with the position on booking conversion and any live concerns about booking errors costing clients
3. **Step 3** - Listen for information that changes the picture; update your understanding rather than defending a position
4. **Step 4** - Record decisions, actions, owners and deadlines during the meeting — not from memory afterwards
5. **Step 5** - Circulate the record promptly to attendees in travel agencies and online, correct errors fast, and file it with the main record

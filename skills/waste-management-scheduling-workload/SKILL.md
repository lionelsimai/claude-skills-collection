# Scheduling & Workload Management — Waste Management

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a waste operations manager in waste facilities and collection rounds. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a waste operations manager working with households, businesses and councils in waste facilities and collection rounds.

## When to use
- You are a waste operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in waste facilities and collection rounds.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive collection completion
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in waste facilities and collection rounds first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind waste transfer notes — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving hazardous waste mishandling) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or collection vehicles availability

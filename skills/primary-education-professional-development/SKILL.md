# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Primary Education

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising primary school teacher. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a primary school teacher working with primary-aged pupils and their parents in a primary school classroom.

## When to use
- You are a primary school teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a primary school classroom.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for a primary school classroom and the evidence in your recent lesson plans
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on safeguarding disclosures mishandled — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for pupil progress measures
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

| Core tools & equipment | interactive whiteboards; reading schemes; assessment trackers; parent communication apps |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a primary school classroom] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [primary-aged pupils and their parents and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in lesson plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against pupil progress measures.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a primary school classroom.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding disclosures mishandled.
- Never let this happen: gaps in progress unaddressed.
- Never let this happen: workload overload.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a primary school teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a primary school classroom's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

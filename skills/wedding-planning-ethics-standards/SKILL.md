# Ethics & Professional Standards — Wedding Planning

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a wedding planner. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a wedding planner working with couples and their families in venues, suppliers and planning offices.

## When to use
- You are a wedding planner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in venues, suppliers and planning offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in venues, suppliers and planning offices: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect couples and their families first where duties conflict, and be alert to how supplier failures on the day can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from planning timelines, and support colleagues who do the same

# Referral & Coordination — Solar Energy

Refer homeowners, businesses and energy companies onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a solar PV engineer working with homeowners, businesses and energy companies in rooftops, solar farms and offices.

## When to use
- You are a solar PV engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in rooftops, solar farms and offices.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what homeowners, businesses and energy companies need that sits outside the solar PV engineer scope in rooftops, solar farms and offices
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from system designs — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and system performance ratio stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with homeowners, businesses and energy companies: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of falls from roofs

# Ethics & Professional Standards — Broadcast Journalism

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a broadcast journalist. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a broadcast journalist working with audiences, contributors and news editors in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.

## When to use
- You are a broadcast journalist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Running order | the planned sequence of items in a programme |
| Compliance | checking content against broadcast rules and law |
| Package | a pre-recorded report within a programme |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in newsrooms and outside broadcasts: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect audiences, contributors and news editors first where duties conflict, and be alert to how inaccurate reporting broadcast can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from running orders, and support colleagues who do the same

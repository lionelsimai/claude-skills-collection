# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Environmental Engineering

Check work in treatment plants, client sites and design offices against the laws, standards and codes that govern a environmental engineer. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a environmental engineer working with water companies, industries and regulators in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a environmental engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Consent | permission to discharge treated water or waste |
| Effluent | liquid waste discharged from a process |
| Remediation | cleaning up contaminated land or water |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in treatment plants, client sites and design offices
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and impact assessments, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching pollution incidents — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

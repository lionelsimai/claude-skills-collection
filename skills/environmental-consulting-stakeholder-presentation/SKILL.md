# Stakeholder Presentation — Environmental Consulting

Present environmental consultant findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a environmental consultant working with developers, industries and regulators in client sites and consultancy offices.

## When to use
- You are a environmental consultant preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in client sites and consultancy offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Baseline | conditions measured before a change, for comparison |
| Receptor | a person, habitat or resource that could be harmed |
| Mitigation | actions reducing an environmental impact |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from assessment reports and movement in report turnaround, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including missed contamination and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in client sites and consultancy offices
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in client sites and consultancy offices] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [developers, industries and regulators and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in assessment reports.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against report turnaround.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in client sites and consultancy offices.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed contamination.
- Never let this happen: sampling errors.
- Never let this happen: missed permit deadlines.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a environmental consultant; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to client sites and consultancy offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

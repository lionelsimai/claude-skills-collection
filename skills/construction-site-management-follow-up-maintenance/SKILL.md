# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Construction Site Management

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for clients, contractors and construction teams are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a site manager working with clients, contractors and construction teams in construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a site manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Programme | the schedule of construction activities |
| Snagging list | defects to fix before handover |
| Toolbox talk | a short safety briefing for site workers |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how programme adherence is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using project management software where measurement is needed, and record it in site diaries
3. **Step 3** - Give clients, contractors and construction teams clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to site accidents shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in construction sites] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [clients, contractors and construction teams and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in site diaries.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against programme adherence.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in construction sites.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: site accidents.
- Never let this happen: programme slippage hidden.
- Never let this happen: quality defects at handover.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a site manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Progress Review & Adjustment — Immigration Law

Review progress for individuals, families and employers navigating immigration rules against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a immigration solicitor working with individuals, families and employers navigating immigration rules in a law firm and immigration tribunals.

## When to use
- You are a immigration solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and immigration tribunals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together application bundles and the latest results for application success rates since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with individuals, families and employers navigating immigration rules honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in a law firm and immigration tribunals

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed application deadlines.
- Never let this happen: incorrect evidence submitted.
- Never let this happen: status expiry overlooked.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a immigration solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a law firm and immigration tribunals's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Risk Assessment — Environmental Engineering

Identify, score and control the risks in work for water companies, industries and regulators in treatment plants, client sites and design offices. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a environmental engineer working with water companies, industries and regulators in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a environmental engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Consent | permission to discharge treated water or waste |
| Effluent | liquid waste discharged from a process |
| Remediation | cleaning up contaminated land or water |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for water companies, industries and regulators and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to pollution incidents
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using modelling software where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in impact assessments, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in treatment plants, client sites and design offices change

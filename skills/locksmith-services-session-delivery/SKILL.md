# Session Delivery Guide — Locksmith Services

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners in clients' premises and vehicles. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a locksmith working with homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners in clients' premises and vehicles.

## When to use
- You are a locksmith preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clients' premises and vehicles.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review job records before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as damage during entry
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and lock picking tools; check everything works and is safe before homeowners, businesses and vehicle owners arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to response times
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Commercial Leasing

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe commercial leasing agent practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a commercial leasing agent working with landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices.

## When to use
- You are a commercial leasing agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial properties and agency offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Heads of terms | the agreed key points before a lease is drafted |
| Break clause | a right to end a lease early on set terms |
| Alienation | transferring or sharing a lease interest |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in commercial properties and agency offices
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using property portals under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching misdescribed premises — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from heads of terms, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misdescribed premises.
- Never let this happen: missed break clauses.
- Never let this happen: unagreed incentives.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a commercial leasing agent; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to commercial properties and agency offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

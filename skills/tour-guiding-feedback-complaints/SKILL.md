## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Invite feedback routinely — not only when things go wrong — and make it easy to give in tourist sites, cities and attractions
2. **Step 2** - Acknowledge complaints quickly, thank the person, and explain the process and timeframe honestly
3. **Step 3** - Investigate from evidence in tour itineraries; separate the facts from the feelings, and respect both
4. **Step 4** - Respond with findings, an apology where warranted, and concrete remedies or explanations
5. **Step 5** - Log themes and feed them into quality work — repeated complaints about group members lost are an early-warning system, not a nuisance

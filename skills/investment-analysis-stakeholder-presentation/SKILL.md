# Stakeholder Presentation — Investment Analysis

Present investment analyst findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a investment analyst working with fund managers, investors and research subscribers in investment firms.

## When to use
- You are a investment analyst preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in investment firms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from research reports and movement in forecast accuracy, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including conflicts of interest undisclosed and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in investment firms
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest undisclosed.
- Never let this happen: model errors.
- Never let this happen: insider information mishandled.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a investment analyst; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to investment firms's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

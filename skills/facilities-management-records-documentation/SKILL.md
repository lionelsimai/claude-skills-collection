# Records & Documentation — Facilities Management

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with building occupants, owners and service teams. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a facilities manager working with building occupants, owners and service teams in commercial buildings and campuses.

## When to use
- You are a facilities manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial buildings and campuses.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| CAFM | computer-aided facilities management software |
| PPM | planned preventive maintenance scheduled in advance |
| Asset register | the inventory of building equipment and its condition |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use maintenance schedules as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format commercial buildings and campuses requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed work order completion exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in commercial buildings and campuses, guarding against life-safety systems unmaintained
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

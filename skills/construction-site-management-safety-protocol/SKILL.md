# Safety Protocol — Construction Site Management

Work safely in construction sites: a practical safety protocol for a site manager and clients, contractors and construction teams. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a site manager working with clients, contractors and construction teams in construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a site manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in construction sites for staff, clients, contractors and construction teams and visitors, starting with site accidents
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as project management software
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in site diaries
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

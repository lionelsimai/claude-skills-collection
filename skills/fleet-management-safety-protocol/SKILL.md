# Safety Protocol — Fleet Management

Work safely in transport depots and offices: a practical safety protocol for a fleet manager and businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a fleet manager working with businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices.

## When to use
- You are a fleet manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in transport depots and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Telematics | technology tracking vehicle location and use |
| Utilisation | how much of the time vehicles are productively used |
| Defect report | a driver's record of vehicle faults |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in transport depots and offices for staff, businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers and visitors, starting with unroadworthy vehicles used
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as telematics systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in vehicle records
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

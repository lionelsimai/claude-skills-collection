## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in a law firm and immigration tribunals for staff, individuals, families and employers navigating immigration rules and visitors, starting with missed application deadlines
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as case management systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in application bundles
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

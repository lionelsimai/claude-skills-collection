# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Tour Guiding

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for tourists and tour operators are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a tour guide working with tourists and tour operators in tourist sites, cities and attractions.

## When to use
- You are a tour guide preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in tourist sites, cities and attractions.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how tour ratings is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using booking platforms where measurement is needed, and record it in tour itineraries
3. **Step 3** - Give tourists and tour operators clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to group members lost shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

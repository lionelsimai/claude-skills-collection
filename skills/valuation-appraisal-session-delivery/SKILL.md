# Session Delivery Guide — Valuation & Appraisal

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for lenders, buyers, sellers and investors in properties and surveying offices. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a chartered valuation surveyor working with lenders, buyers, sellers and investors in properties and surveying offices.

## When to use
- You are a chartered valuation surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in properties and surveying offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review valuation reports before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as overvaluation losses
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and valuation software; check everything works and is safe before lenders, buyers, sellers and investors arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to report turnaround
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: overvaluation losses.
- Never let this happen: missed defects affecting value.
- Never let this happen: conflicts undeclared.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a chartered valuation surveyor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to properties and surveying offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# New Client Onboarding Pack — Crop Farming

Onboard food companies, markets and land owners smoothly into a crop farmer and agronomist's service in farms and fields. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a crop farmer and agronomist working with food companies, markets and land owners in farms and fields.

## When to use
- You are a crop farmer and agronomist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in farms and fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Rotation | changing crops grown on a field across seasons |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from crop plans
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on yield per hectare will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to farms and fields, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or pesticide misuse

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: pesticide misuse.
- Never let this happen: crop failure from weather.
- Never let this happen: soil degradation.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a crop farmer and agronomist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to farms and fields's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

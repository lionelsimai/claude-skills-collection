# Quality Audit — Special Educational Needs

Audit the quality of SEN coordinator and specialist teacher work in mainstream and specialist school settings and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a SEN coordinator and specialist teacher working with children and young people with special educational needs, and their families in mainstream and specialist school settings.

## When to use
- You are a SEN coordinator and specialist teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mainstream and specialist school settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| EHCP | an education, health and care plan for a child with complex needs |
| Provision | the support and resources provided to meet needs |
| Annual review | the yearly meeting reviewing a child's plan and progress |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to plan review timeliness and the standards for mainstream and specialist school settings
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of education and health care plans and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including unmet needs in plans
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in mainstream and specialist school settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [children and young people with special educational needs, and their families and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in education and health care plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against plan review timeliness.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in mainstream and specialist school settings.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unmet needs in plans.
- Never let this happen: review deadlines missed.
- Never let this happen: provision not delivered as specified.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a SEN coordinator and specialist teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to mainstream and specialist school settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

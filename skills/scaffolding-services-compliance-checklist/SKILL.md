# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Scaffolding Services

Check work in construction sites and buildings against the laws, standards and codes that govern a scaffolder. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a scaffolder working with construction companies and property owners in construction sites and buildings.

## When to use
- You are a scaffolder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites and buildings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in construction sites and buildings
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and scaffold designs, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching scaffold collapse — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

- Never let this happen: unauthorised alterations.

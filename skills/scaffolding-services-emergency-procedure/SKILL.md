# Emergency Response Procedure — Scaffolding Services

Respond to emergencies in construction sites and buildings calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a scaffolder working with construction companies and property owners in construction sites and buildings.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for construction companies and property owners, especially scaffold collapse
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a scaffolder is trained and authorised for, using scaffold tubes and boards as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in construction sites and buildings, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep construction companies and property owners and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in scaffold designs afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

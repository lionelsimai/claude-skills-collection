# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Tax Advisory

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe tax adviser practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a tax adviser working with individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## When to use
- You are a tax adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy or advisory firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in an accountancy or advisory firm
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using tax software under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching missed filing deadlines — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from tax computations, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Output Format

The finished output is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against filing deadline compliance.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed filing deadlines.
- Never let this happen: aggressive schemes challenged.
- Never let this happen: relief claims overlooked.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a tax adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to an accountancy or advisory firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

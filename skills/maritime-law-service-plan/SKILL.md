# Service Plan Design — Maritime Law

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for ship owners, insurers and cargo interests as a maritime solicitor. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a maritime solicitor working with ship owners, insurers and cargo interests in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts.

## When to use
- You are a maritime solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with ship owners, insurers and cargo interests — specific enough to measure against case resolution times
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for a shipping law firm and admiralty courts, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around charterparties so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, shipping databases and materials, with contingencies for cargo deadlines missed
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

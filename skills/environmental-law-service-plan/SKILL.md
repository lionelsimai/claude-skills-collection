# Service Plan Design — Environmental Law

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for developers, businesses and community groups as a environmental solicitor. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a environmental solicitor working with developers, businesses and community groups in a law firm and planning inquiries.

## When to use
- You are a environmental solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and planning inquiries.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Environmental permit | official permission for activities affecting the environment |
| EIA | environmental impact assessment of a proposed development |
| Contaminated land | land polluted by previous use |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with developers, businesses and community groups — specific enough to measure against permit success rates
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for a law firm and planning inquiries, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around permit applications so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, planning portals and materials, with contingencies for permit breaches unadvised
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: permit breaches unadvised.
- Never let this happen: consultation deadlines missed.
- Never let this happen: contaminated land liabilities.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a environmental solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a law firm and planning inquiries's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

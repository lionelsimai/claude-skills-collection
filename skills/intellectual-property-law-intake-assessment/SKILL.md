# Intake & Initial Assessment — Intellectual Property Law

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for creators, brands and technology companies in a law firm and intellectual property offices. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a intellectual property solicitor working with creators, brands and technology companies in a law firm and intellectual property offices.

## When to use
- You are a intellectual property solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and intellectual property offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Trade mark | a sign distinguishing one business's goods or services |
| Infringement | unauthorised use of protected intellectual property |
| Licence | permission to use intellectual property on agreed terms |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold trade mark applications
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from creators, brands and technology companies, using the standard intake structure for a law firm and intellectual property offices
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to filing success rates, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially missed renewal deadlines — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for creators, brands and technology companies in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of trade mark applications

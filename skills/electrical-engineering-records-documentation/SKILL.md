# Records & Documentation — Electrical Engineering

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with energy, manufacturing and building clients. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a electrical engineer working with energy, manufacturing and building clients in design offices, plants and substations.

## When to use
- You are a electrical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, plants and substations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use circuit designs as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format design offices, plants and substations requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed test pass rates exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in design offices, plants and substations, guarding against electric shock risks
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

# Emergency Response Procedure — Tax Law

Respond to emergencies in a law firm and tax tribunals calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a tax solicitor working with individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions in a law firm and tax tribunals.

## When to use
- You are a tax solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and tax tribunals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions, especially filing deadlines missed
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a tax solicitor is trained and authorised for, using tax research databases as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in a law firm and tax tribunals, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in tax opinions afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Mine Operations

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe mine operations manager practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a mine operations manager working with mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a mine operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mines and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Grade | the concentration of valuable mineral in ore |
| Ventilation | supplying fresh air and removing gases underground |
| Stope | an excavated area from which ore is extracted |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in mines and processing plants
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using mining machinery under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching underground incidents — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from production reports, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: underground incidents.
- Never let this happen: ventilation failures.
- Never let this happen: equipment breakdowns.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a mine operations manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to mines and processing plants's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

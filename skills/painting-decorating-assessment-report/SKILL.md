# Assessment & Findings Report — Painting & Decorating

Assess homeowners, landlords and commercial clients and write a defensible findings report as a painter and decorator. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a painter and decorator working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, offices and building sites.

- You are a painter and decorator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, offices and building sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and quotes so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using sanders, following the recognised method for homes, offices and building sites
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind job completion times and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for falls from height; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

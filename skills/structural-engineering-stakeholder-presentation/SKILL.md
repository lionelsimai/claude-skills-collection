# Stakeholder Presentation — Structural Engineering

Present structural engineer findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a structural engineer working with architects, builders and building owners in design offices and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a structural engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from structural calculations and movement in calculation check completion, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including calculation errors and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in design offices and construction sites
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

- Never let this happen: building movement.

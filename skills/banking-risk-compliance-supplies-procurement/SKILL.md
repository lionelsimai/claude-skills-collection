# Supplies & Procurement — Banking Risk & Compliance

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in bank risk and compliance departments. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a risk and compliance officer working with banks, regulators and boards in bank risk and compliance departments.

## When to use
- You are a risk and compliance officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank risk and compliance departments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Risk register | the recorded list of risks, owners and controls |
| Three lines | the model separating risk ownership, oversight and audit |
| Conduct risk | the risk of harm to customers or markets from behaviour |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on risk registers and the kit centred on risk systems, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to undetected misconduct
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

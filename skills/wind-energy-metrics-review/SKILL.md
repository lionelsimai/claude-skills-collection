# Metrics & Outcomes Review — Wind Energy

Track, interpret and act on the outcome metrics that matter for a wind turbine technician. The deliverable is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
This skill is written for a wind turbine technician working with energy companies and grid operators in wind farms onshore and offshore.

## When to use
- You are a wind turbine technician preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in wind farms onshore and offshore.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define each metric precisely — start with turbine availability — including source data in maintenance records and collection method
2. **Step 2** - Set review cadence and comparison baselines so trends, not single data points, drive decisions
3. **Step 3** - Investigate movements before celebrating or panicking: check data quality, case mix and one-off causes
4. **Step 4** - Link metric movements to practice changes and to risks such as falls from height; test one change at a time where possible
5. **Step 5** - Report results honestly to stakeholders in wind farms onshore and offshore, including where outcomes fell short and what changes

# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Civil Engineering

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a civil engineer relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a civil engineer working with infrastructure clients, contractors and the public in engineering consultancies and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a civil engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in engineering consultancies and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| BIM | building information modelling, a shared digital model of an asset |
| Load path | the route forces take through a structure to the ground |
| Specification | the detailed technical requirements for works or materials |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in engineering consultancies and construction sites, centred on CAD and BIM software, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against design calculations
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to structural failures
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

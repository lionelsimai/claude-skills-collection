# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Commercial Diving

Explain complex commercial diver matters to offshore, construction and salvage clients in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a commercial diver working with offshore, construction and salvage clients in underwater work sites and support vessels.

## When to use
- You are a commercial diver preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in underwater work sites and support vessels.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Umbilical | the cable and hoses supplying air and communications to a diver |
| Decompression | controlled ascent stops letting gases leave the body safely |
| Dive plan | the documented method and limits for a dive |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what offshore, construction and salvage clients actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind dive plans — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as decompression sickness
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking offshore, construction and salvage clients to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

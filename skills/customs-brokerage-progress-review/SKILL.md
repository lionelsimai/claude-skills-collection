# Progress Review & Adjustment — Customs Brokerage

Review progress for importers and exporters against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a customs broker working with importers and exporters in customs offices and freight operations.

## When to use
- You are a customs broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in customs offices and freight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tariff classification | assigning the correct commodity code to goods |
| Duty | tax charged on imported goods |
| Origin | the country goods are deemed to come from for trade rules |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together customs declarations and the latest results for declaration accuracy since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with importers and exporters honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in customs offices and freight operations

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misclassification penalties.
- Never let this happen: clearance delays.
- Never let this happen: prohibited goods shipped.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a customs broker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to customs offices and freight operations's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

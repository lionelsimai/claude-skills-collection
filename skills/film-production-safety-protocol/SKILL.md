# Safety Protocol — Film Production

Work safely in production offices and filming locations: a practical safety protocol for a film producer and production manager and production companies, cast, crew and financiers. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a film producer and production manager working with production companies, cast, crew and financiers in production offices and filming locations.

## When to use
- You are a film producer and production manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in production offices and filming locations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in production offices and filming locations for staff, production companies, cast, crew and financiers and visitors, starting with budget overruns
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as production management software
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in production budgets
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

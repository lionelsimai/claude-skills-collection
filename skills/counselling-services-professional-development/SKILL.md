# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Counselling Services

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising counsellor and psychotherapist. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a counsellor and psychotherapist working with individuals seeking psychological support in clinics, schools, workplaces and online.

## When to use
- You are a counsellor and psychotherapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, schools, workplaces and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Contracting | agreeing the terms of the counselling relationship |
| Supervision | regular professional review of a counsellor's work |
| Boundaries | the limits keeping the therapeutic relationship safe |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for clinics, schools, workplaces and online and the evidence in your recent client notes
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on risk to self or others unrecognised — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for outcome improvement scores
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Counselling Services |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | client notes; contracts; risk records; outcome measures |
| Core tools & equipment | practice management systems; video platforms; outcome tools; supervision records |
| People involved | individuals seeking psychological support; the counsellor and psychotherapist; colleagues and other services in clinics, schools, workplaces and online |
| Primary record | client notes — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in clinics, schools, workplaces and online] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [individuals seeking psychological support and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in client notes.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against outcome improvement scores.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in clinics, schools, workplaces and online.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: risk to self or others unrecognised.
- Never let this happen: boundary breaches.
- Never let this happen: notes not secured.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a counsellor and psychotherapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clinics, schools, workplaces and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

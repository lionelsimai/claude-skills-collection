# Supplies & Procurement — Critical Care Nursing

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in an intensive care unit. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a critical care nurse working with critically ill patients and their families in an intensive care unit.

## When to use
- You are a critical care nurse preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an intensive care unit.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Sedation score | a scale rating how deeply a patient is sedated |
| Weaning | gradually reducing ventilator support |
| Delirium | acute confusion common in critical illness |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on nursing care plans and the kit centred on ventilators, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to unrecognised deterioration
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in an intensive care unit] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [critically ill patients and their families and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in nursing care plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against observation chart completion.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in an intensive care unit.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unrecognised deterioration.
- Never let this happen: pressure damage.
- Never let this happen: device-associated infection.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a critical care nurse; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to an intensive care unit's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

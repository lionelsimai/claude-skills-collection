# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Forensic Accounting

Explain complex forensic accountant matters to courts, insurers and businesses investigating financial wrongdoing in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a forensic accountant working with courts, insurers and businesses investigating financial wrongdoing in an accountancy firm and legal proceedings.

## When to use
- You are a forensic accountant preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy firm and legal proceedings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Quantum | the amount of financial loss in a dispute |
| E-discovery | collecting and reviewing electronic evidence |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what courts, insurers and businesses investigating financial wrongdoing actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind investigation reports — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as evidence contamination
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking courts, insurers and businesses investigating financial wrongdoing to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in an accountancy firm and legal proceedings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [courts, insurers and businesses investigating financial wrongdoing and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: evidence contamination.
- Never let this happen: assumptions challenged in court.
- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a forensic accountant; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to an accountancy firm and legal proceedings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

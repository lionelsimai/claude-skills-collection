# Referral & Coordination — Industrial Engineering

Refer manufacturing and service organisations onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a industrial engineer working with manufacturing and service organisations in factories, warehouses and service operations.

## When to use
- You are a industrial engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in factories, warehouses and service operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what manufacturing and service organisations need that sits outside the industrial engineer scope in factories, warehouses and service operations
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from process maps — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and throughput improvement stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with manufacturing and service organisations: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of changes disrupting production

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: changes disrupting production.
- Never let this happen: worker strain from poor design.
- Never let this happen: unmeasured bottlenecks.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a industrial engineer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to factories, warehouses and service operations's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Strata Management

Explain complex strata manager matters to lot owners, owners corporations and residents in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a strata manager working with lot owners, owners corporations and residents in residential complexes and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a strata manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in residential complexes and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Levy | owners' regular contribution to shared costs |
| By-laws | the rules governing behaviour and use in a strata scheme |
| Quorum | the minimum attendance for a meeting to make decisions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what lot owners, owners corporations and residents actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind meeting minutes — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as fund mismanagement
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking lot owners, owners corporations and residents to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

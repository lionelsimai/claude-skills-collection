# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Customs Brokerage

Explain complex customs broker matters to importers and exporters in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a customs broker working with importers and exporters in customs offices and freight operations.

## When to use
- You are a customs broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in customs offices and freight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tariff classification | assigning the correct commodity code to goods |
| Duty | tax charged on imported goods |
| Origin | the country goods are deemed to come from for trade rules |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what importers and exporters actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind customs declarations — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as misclassification penalties
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking importers and exporters to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

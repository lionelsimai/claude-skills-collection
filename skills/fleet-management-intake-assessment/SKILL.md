# Intake & Initial Assessment — Fleet Management

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a fleet manager working with businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices.

- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold vehicle records
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers, using the standard intake structure for transport depots and offices
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to vehicle utilisation, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially unroadworthy vehicles used — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of vehicle records

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unroadworthy vehicles used.
- Never let this happen: driver fatigue.
- Never let this happen: maintenance backlogs.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a fleet manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to transport depots and offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

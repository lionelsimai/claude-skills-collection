# Risk Assessment — Warehouse Management

Identify, score and control the risks in work for retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients in warehouses and distribution centres. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a warehouse manager working with retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients in warehouses and distribution centres.

## When to use
- You are a warehouse manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in warehouses and distribution centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Pick accuracy | how often the correct items are picked |
| Goods-in | receiving and checking deliveries |
| Cycle counting | counting sections of stock on a rolling basis |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to forklift accidents
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using warehouse management systems where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in inventory records, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in warehouses and distribution centres change

- Never let this happen: forklift accidents.
- Never let this happen: stock shrinkage.
- Never let this happen: picking errors shipped.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a warehouse manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

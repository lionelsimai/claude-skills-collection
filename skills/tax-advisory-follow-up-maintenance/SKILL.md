# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Tax Advisory

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a tax adviser working with individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## When to use
- You are a tax adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy or advisory firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how filing deadline compliance is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using tax software where measurement is needed, and record it in tax computations
3. **Step 3** - Give individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to missed filing deadlines shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

# Progress Review & Adjustment — Cardiology

Review progress for patients with heart and circulation conditions against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a cardiologist working with patients with heart and circulation conditions in a hospital cardiology department and outpatient clinic.

## When to use
- You are a cardiologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a hospital cardiology department and outpatient clinic.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| ECG | a tracing of the heart's electrical activity |
| Ejection fraction | the percentage of blood the left ventricle pumps out each beat |
| Angioplasty | a procedure to widen narrowed coronary arteries |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together ECG reports and the latest results for chest-pain triage time since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with patients with heart and circulation conditions honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in a hospital cardiology department and outpatient clinic

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a hospital cardiology department and outpatient clinic] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients with heart and circulation conditions and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed myocardial infarction.
- Never let this happen: anticoagulant dosing errors.
- Never let this happen: contrast-induced kidney injury.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a cardiologist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a hospital cardiology department and outpatient clinic's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Counselling Services

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for individuals seeking psychological support are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a counsellor and psychotherapist working with individuals seeking psychological support in clinics, schools, workplaces and online.

## When to use
- You are a counsellor and psychotherapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, schools, workplaces and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how outcome improvement scores is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using practice management systems where measurement is needed, and record it in client notes
3. **Step 3** - Give individuals seeking psychological support clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to risk to self or others unrecognised shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in clinics, schools, workplaces and online] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [individuals seeking psychological support and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in client notes.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against outcome improvement scores.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in clinics, schools, workplaces and online.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: risk to self or others unrecognised.
- Never let this happen: boundary breaches.
- Never let this happen: notes not secured.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a counsellor and psychotherapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clinics, schools, workplaces and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

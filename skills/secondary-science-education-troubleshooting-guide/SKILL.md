# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Secondary Science Education

Diagnose and resolve common problems a secondary science teacher meets in a school science department and laboratories. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a secondary science teacher working with secondary school students in a school science department and laboratories.

## When to use
- You are a secondary science teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a school science department and laboratories.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Risk assessment | evaluating hazards in practical work before it happens |
| Hypothesis | a testable prediction |
| Controlled variable | a factor kept constant in an investigation |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in schemes of work — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to practical safety incidents early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using laboratory equipment, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against examination results that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a school science department and laboratories] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [secondary school students and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: practical safety incidents.
- Never let this happen: misconceptions in core concepts.
- Never let this happen: equipment faults.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a secondary science teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a school science department and laboratories's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

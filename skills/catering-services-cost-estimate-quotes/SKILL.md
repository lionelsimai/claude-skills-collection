# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Catering Services

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a caterer. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a caterer working with event clients and their guests in venues, events and production kitchens.

## When to use
- You are a caterer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in venues, events and production kitchens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| BEO | a banquet event order detailing an event's requirements |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and event orders; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, transport equipment usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against food temperature abuse in transit
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as event delivery success
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: food temperature abuse in transit.
- Never let this happen: running out of food.
- Never let this happen: staff shortages on the day.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a caterer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to venues, events and production kitchens's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

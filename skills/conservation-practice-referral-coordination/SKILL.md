# Referral & Coordination — Conservation Practice

Refer landowners, visitors and conservation charities onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a conservation officer working with landowners, visitors and conservation charities in nature reserves and countryside sites.

## When to use
- You are a conservation officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in nature reserves and countryside sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Habitat | the natural home of a species |
| Biodiversity | the variety of living things in an area |
| Invasive species | a non-native species harming the environment |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what landowners, visitors and conservation charities need that sits outside the conservation officer scope in nature reserves and countryside sites
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from management plans — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and habitat condition scores stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with landowners, visitors and conservation charities: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of habitat damage from works

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in nature reserves and countryside sites] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [landowners, visitors and conservation charities and other stakeholders]

1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: habitat damage from works.
- Never let this happen: invasive species spread.
- Never let this happen: public safety incidents.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a conservation officer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to nature reserves and countryside sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

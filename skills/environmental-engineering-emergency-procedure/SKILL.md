# Emergency Response Procedure — Environmental Engineering

Respond to emergencies in treatment plants, client sites and design offices calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a environmental engineer working with water companies, industries and regulators in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a environmental engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for water companies, industries and regulators, especially pollution incidents
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a environmental engineer is trained and authorised for, using modelling software as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in treatment plants, client sites and design offices, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep water companies, industries and regulators and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in impact assessments afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

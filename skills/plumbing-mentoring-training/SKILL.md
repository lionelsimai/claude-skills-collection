# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Plumbing

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe plumber practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a plumber working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, building sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a plumber preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, building sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| First fix | the initial installation stage of pipework |
| Pressure test | checking a system for leaks under pressure |
| Backflow | contaminated water flowing back into the clean supply |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in homes, building sites and commercial premises
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using pipe threading tools under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching water damage from leaks — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from job sheets, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

- Never let this happen: gas safety risks.

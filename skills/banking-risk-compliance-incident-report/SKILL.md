# Incident Reporting & Response — Banking Risk & Compliance

Respond to, record and learn from incidents involving banks, regulators and boards in bank risk and compliance departments. The deliverable is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
This skill is written for a risk and compliance officer working with banks, regulators and boards in bank risk and compliance departments.

## When to use
- You are a risk and compliance officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank risk and compliance departments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Risk register | the recorded list of risks, owners and controls |
| Three lines | the model separating risk ownership, oversight and audit |
| Conduct risk | the risk of harm to customers or markets from behaviour |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if undetected misconduct is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in risk registers
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in bank risk and compliance departments
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with banks, regulators and boards about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in bank risk and compliance departments] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [banks, regulators and boards and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in risk registers.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against monitoring completion.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in bank risk and compliance departments.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: undetected misconduct.
- Never let this happen: late regulatory returns.
- Never let this happen: control failures.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a risk and compliance officer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank risk and compliance departments's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

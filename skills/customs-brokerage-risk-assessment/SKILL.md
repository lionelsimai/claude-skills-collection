# Risk Assessment — Customs Brokerage

Identify, score and control the risks in work for importers and exporters in customs offices and freight operations. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a customs broker working with importers and exporters in customs offices and freight operations.

## When to use
- You are a customs broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in customs offices and freight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tariff classification | assigning the correct commodity code to goods |
| Duty | tax charged on imported goods |
| Origin | the country goods are deemed to come from for trade rules |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for importers and exporters and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to misclassification penalties
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using customs software where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in customs declarations, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in customs offices and freight operations change

# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Insurance Underwriting

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising insurance underwriter. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a insurance underwriter working with brokers, policyholders and insurers in insurance offices.

## When to use
- You are a insurance underwriter preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Loss ratio | claims costs as a share of premium earned |
| Wording | the contractual terms of a policy |
| Referral | passing a risk to a more senior underwriter |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for insurance offices and the evidence in your recent risk assessments
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on underpriced risks — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for quote turnaround
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

- Never let this happen: underpriced risks.
- Never let this happen: wording errors.
- Never let this happen: unauthorised acceptance of risk.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a insurance underwriter; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to insurance offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

# Progress Review & Adjustment — Crane Operations

Review progress for construction and industrial clients against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a crane operator working with construction and industrial clients in construction and industrial sites.

## When to use
- You are a crane operator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction and industrial sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Lift plan | the documented method for a lifting operation |
| Slinger | the person attaching loads to the crane |
| Rated capacity | the maximum load a crane can lift in a configuration |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together lift plans and the latest results for lift completion rates since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with construction and industrial clients honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in construction and industrial sites

- Never let this happen: crane overload.
- Never let this happen: weather-related incidents.

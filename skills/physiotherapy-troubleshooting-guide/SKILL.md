# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Physiotherapy

Diagnose and resolve common problems a physiotherapist meets in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a physiotherapist working with patients recovering movement, strength and function in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.

- You are a physiotherapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in assessment records — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to falls during treatment early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using treatment plinths, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against functional outcome improvement that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: falls during treatment.
- Never let this happen: overloading healing tissue.
- Never let this happen: missed red-flag symptoms.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a physiotherapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

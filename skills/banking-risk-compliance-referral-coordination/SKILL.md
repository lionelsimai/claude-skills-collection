# Referral & Coordination — Banking Risk & Compliance

Refer banks, regulators and boards onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a risk and compliance officer working with banks, regulators and boards in bank risk and compliance departments.

## When to use
- You are a risk and compliance officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank risk and compliance departments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Risk register | the recorded list of risks, owners and controls |
| Three lines | the model separating risk ownership, oversight and audit |
| Conduct risk | the risk of harm to customers or markets from behaviour |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what banks, regulators and boards need that sits outside the risk and compliance officer scope in bank risk and compliance departments
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from risk registers — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and monitoring completion stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with banks, regulators and boards: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of undetected misconduct

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in bank risk and compliance departments] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [banks, regulators and boards and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: undetected misconduct.
- Never let this happen: late regulatory returns.
- Never let this happen: control failures.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a risk and compliance officer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank risk and compliance departments's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

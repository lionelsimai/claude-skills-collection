# Emergency Response Procedure — Crop Farming

Respond to emergencies in farms and fields calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a crop farmer and agronomist working with food companies, markets and land owners in farms and fields.

## When to use
- You are a crop farmer and agronomist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in farms and fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for food companies, markets and land owners, especially pesticide misuse
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a crop farmer and agronomist is trained and authorised for, using tractors and implements as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in farms and fields, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep food companies, markets and land owners and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in crop plans afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: pesticide misuse.
- Never let this happen: crop failure from weather.
- Never let this happen: soil degradation.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a crop farmer and agronomist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to farms and fields's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

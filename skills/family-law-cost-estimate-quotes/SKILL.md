# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Family Law

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a family law solicitor. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Disclosure | the formal exchange of financial information in family cases |
| Consent order | an agreed financial settlement approved by the court |
| Non-molestation order | a court order protecting a person from abuse |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and client care letters; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, case management systems usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against missed court deadlines
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as case progression times
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

# Quality Audit — Orthodontics

Audit the quality of orthodontist work in an orthodontic practice and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a orthodontist working with patients needing teeth alignment and bite correction in an orthodontic practice.

## When to use
- You are a orthodontist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an orthodontic practice.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Occlusion | how the upper and lower teeth meet |
| Aligner | a clear removable tray that moves teeth gradually |
| Retention | holding teeth in their new position after treatment |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to treatment duration accuracy and the standards for an orthodontic practice
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of treatment plans and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including root resorption unnoticed
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in an orthodontic practice] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients needing teeth alignment and bite correction and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in treatment plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against treatment duration accuracy.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in an orthodontic practice.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: root resorption unnoticed.
- Never let this happen: treatment extended by missed appointments.
- Never let this happen: poor retention compliance.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a orthodontist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to an orthodontic practice's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

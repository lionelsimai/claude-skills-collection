# Quality Audit — Bookkeeping Practice

Audit the quality of bookkeeper work in a bookkeeping practice and client premises and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a bookkeeper working with small businesses, sole traders and accountants in a bookkeeping practice and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a bookkeeper preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a bookkeeping practice and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Reconciliation | matching records against bank statements to find differences |
| Ledger | the record of transactions by category |
| Accruals | recognising income or costs in the period they relate to |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to reconciliation timeliness and the standards for a bookkeeping practice and client premises
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of bank reconciliations and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including miscoded transactions
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a bookkeeping practice and client premises] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [small businesses, sole traders and accountants and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: miscoded transactions.
- Never let this happen: missing source documents.
- Never let this happen: VAT errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a bookkeeper; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a bookkeeping practice and client premises's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Safety Protocol — Architectural Technology

Work safely in design studios and construction sites: a practical safety protocol for a architectural technologist and architects, developers and homeowners. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a architectural technologist working with architects, developers and homeowners in design studios and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a architectural technologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design studios and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Building regulations | legal standards for design and construction work |
| Detailing | the precise drawings of how building elements join |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in design studios and construction sites for staff, architects, developers and homeowners and visitors, starting with regulation non-compliance
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as CAD and BIM software
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in technical drawings
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

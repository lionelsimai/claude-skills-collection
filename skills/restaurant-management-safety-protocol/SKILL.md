# Safety Protocol — Restaurant Management

Work safely in restaurants and cafes: a practical safety protocol for a restaurant manager and diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in restaurants and cafes for staff, diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams and visitors, starting with food hygiene failures
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as point of sale systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in menus and costing sheets
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

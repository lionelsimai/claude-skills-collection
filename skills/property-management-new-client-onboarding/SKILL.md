# New Client Onboarding Pack — Property Management

Onboard landlords, tenants and leaseholders smoothly into a property manager's service in managed properties and management offices. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a property manager working with landlords, tenants and leaseholders in managed properties and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a property manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in managed properties and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from tenancy agreements
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on rent collection rates will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to managed properties and management offices, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or safety certificates expired

# Emergency Response Procedure — Aquaculture

This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for seafood buyers and processors, especially disease outbreaks in stock
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a fish farm manager is trained and authorised for, using feeding systems as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep seafood buyers and processors and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in stock records afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

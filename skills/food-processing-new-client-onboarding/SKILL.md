# New Client Onboarding Pack — Food Processing

Onboard retailers, wholesalers and consumers smoothly into a food production manager's service in food factories and processing plants. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a food production manager working with retailers, wholesalers and consumers in food factories and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a food production manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in food factories and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Traceability | following a product through every production stage |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from production plans
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on production yield will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to food factories and processing plants, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or contamination incidents

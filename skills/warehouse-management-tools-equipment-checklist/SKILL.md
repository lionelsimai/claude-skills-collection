# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Warehouse Management

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a warehouse manager relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a warehouse manager working with retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients in warehouses and distribution centres.

## When to use
- You are a warehouse manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in warehouses and distribution centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Pick accuracy | how often the correct items are picked |
| Goods-in | receiving and checking deliveries |
| Cycle counting | counting sections of stock on a rolling basis |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in warehouses and distribution centres, centred on warehouse management systems, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against inventory records
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to forklift accidents
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

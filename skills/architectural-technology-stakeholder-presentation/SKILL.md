# Stakeholder Presentation — Architectural Technology

Present architectural technologist findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a architectural technologist working with architects, developers and homeowners in design studios and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a architectural technologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design studios and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Building regulations | legal standards for design and construction work |
| Detailing | the precise drawings of how building elements join |
| Clash detection | finding conflicts between building systems in a model |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from technical drawings and movement in drawing issue timeliness, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including regulation non-compliance and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in design studios and construction sites
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

- Never let this happen: detailing errors causing site problems.
- Never let this happen: clash detection missed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a architectural technologist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

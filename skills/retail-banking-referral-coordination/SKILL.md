# Referral & Coordination — Retail Banking

Refer personal banking customers onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a retail banking adviser working with personal banking customers in bank branches and contact centres.

## When to use
- You are a retail banking adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank branches and contact centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what personal banking customers need that sits outside the retail banking adviser scope in bank branches and contact centres
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from customer records — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and customer satisfaction stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with personal banking customers: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of mis-selling risks

1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: mis-selling risks.
- Never let this happen: fraud missed.
- Never let this happen: vulnerable customers unrecognised.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a retail banking adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank branches and contact centres's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

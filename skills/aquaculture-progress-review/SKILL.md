# Progress Review & Adjustment — Aquaculture

Review progress for seafood buyers and processors against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## When to use
- You are a fish farm manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together stock records and the latest results for growth rates since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with seafood buyers and processors honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: disease outbreaks in stock.
- Never let this happen: escapes into the wild.
- Never let this happen: water quality crashes.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a fish farm manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to lakes, coastal sites and fish farms's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Corporate Law

Check work in a commercial law firm against the laws, standards and codes that govern a corporate solicitor. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a corporate solicitor working with companies, directors and investors in a commercial law firm.

## When to use
- You are a corporate solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a commercial law firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Due diligence | investigation of a business before a transaction |
| Completion | the point at which a transaction legally takes effect |
| Warranties | contractual statements of fact given by a seller |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in a commercial law firm
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and transaction documents, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching missed completion deadlines — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed completion deadlines.
- Never let this happen: undisclosed liabilities.
- Never let this happen: regulatory filing errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a corporate solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a commercial law firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Service Plan Design — Neurology

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for patients with brain, spinal cord and nerve disorders as a neurologist. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a neurologist working with patients with brain, spinal cord and nerve disorders in a neurology department and outpatient clinic.

## When to use
- You are a neurologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a neurology department and outpatient clinic.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| EEG | a recording of electrical activity in the brain |
| Lesion | an area of damaged tissue |
| Aura | warning symptoms some patients get before a seizure or migraine |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with patients with brain, spinal cord and nerve disorders — specific enough to measure against stroke assessment timeliness
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for a neurology department and outpatient clinic, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around neurological examination records so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, EEG machines and materials, with contingencies for missed stroke presentations
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a neurology department and outpatient clinic] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients with brain, spinal cord and nerve disorders and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in neurological examination records.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against stroke assessment timeliness.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a neurology department and outpatient clinic.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed stroke presentations.
- Never let this happen: status epilepticus delays.
- Never let this happen: medication interactions.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a neurologist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a neurology department and outpatient clinic's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Conveyancing

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for home buyers, sellers and lenders are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a conveyancer working with home buyers, sellers and lenders in a conveyancing firm.

## When to use
- You are a conveyancer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a conveyancing firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Exchange | when a property sale becomes legally binding |
| Title | the legal evidence of property ownership |
| Stamp duty | a tax payable on property purchases in many jurisdictions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how exchange-to-completion times is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using case management systems where measurement is needed, and record it in contracts
3. **Step 3** - Give home buyers, sellers and lenders clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to missed exchange deadlines shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed exchange deadlines.
- Never let this happen: title defects overlooked.
- Never let this happen: fraud attempts undetected.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a conveyancer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a conveyancing firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

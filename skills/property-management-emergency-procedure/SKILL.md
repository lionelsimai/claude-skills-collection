# Emergency Response Procedure — Property Management

This skill is written for a property manager working with landlords, tenants and leaseholders in managed properties and management offices.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for landlords, tenants and leaseholders, especially safety certificates expired
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a property manager is trained and authorised for, using property management systems as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in managed properties and management offices, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep landlords, tenants and leaseholders and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in tenancy agreements afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

# Records & Documentation — Dietetics

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a dietitian working with patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment in hospital wards, clinics and community settings.

## When to use
- You are a dietitian preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hospital wards, clinics and community settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Enteral feeding | nutrition delivered through a tube into the stomach or bowel |
| Malnutrition | deficiency or imbalance of nutrients harming health |
| BMI | body mass index, weight relative to height |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use nutrition assessments as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format hospital wards, clinics and community settings requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed screening completion rate exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in hospital wards, clinics and community settings, guarding against malnutrition unrecognised
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in hospital wards, clinics and community settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against screening completion rate.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in hospital wards, clinics and community settings.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: malnutrition unrecognised.
- Never let this happen: refeeding syndrome.
- Never let this happen: unsafe feeding regimens.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a dietitian; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hospital wards, clinics and community settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Assessment & Findings Report — Masonry

Assess builders, homeowners and heritage organisations and write a defensible findings report as a mason and bricklayer. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a mason and bricklayer working with builders, homeowners and heritage organisations in construction sites and historic buildings.

## When to use
- You are a mason and bricklayer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites and historic buildings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and laying records so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using trowels and levels, following the recognised method for construction sites and historic buildings
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind work completion rates and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for structural defects from poor work; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: structural defects from poor work.
- Never let this happen: falls and manual handling injuries.
- Never let this happen: heritage damage.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a mason and bricklayer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to construction sites and historic buildings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

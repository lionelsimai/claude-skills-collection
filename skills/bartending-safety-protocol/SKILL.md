# Safety Protocol — Bartending

Work safely in bars, restaurants and event venues: a practical safety protocol for a bartender and bar customers and venue managers. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a bartender working with bar customers and venue managers in bars, restaurants and event venues.

## When to use
- You are a bartender preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bars, restaurants and event venues.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Stock variance | the difference between recorded and actual stock |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in bars, restaurants and event venues for staff, bar customers and venue managers and visitors, starting with over-service of alcohol
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as point of sale systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in stock counts
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

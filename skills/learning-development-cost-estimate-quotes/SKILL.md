# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Learning & Development

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a learning and development specialist. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a learning and development specialist working with employees and organisational leaders in organisations across all sectors.

## When to use
- You are a learning and development specialist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in organisations across all sectors.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Needs analysis | identifying the gap between current and required skills |
| Blended learning | mixing formats such as workshops, online and coaching |
| Kirkpatrick | a model evaluating training from reaction to results |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and training needs analyses; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, learning platforms usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against training that changes nothing
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as training completion
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

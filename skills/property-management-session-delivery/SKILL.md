# Session Delivery Guide — Property Management

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for landlords, tenants and leaseholders in managed properties and management offices. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a property manager working with landlords, tenants and leaseholders in managed properties and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a property manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in managed properties and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review tenancy agreements before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as safety certificates expired
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and property management systems; check everything works and is safe before landlords, tenants and leaseholders arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to rent collection rates
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

- Never let this happen: deposit disputes.
- Never let this happen: unresponsive repairs.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a property manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Aquaculture

Explain complex fish farm manager matters to seafood buyers and processors in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## When to use
- You are a fish farm manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Biomass | the total weight of stock in a system |
| Fallowing | leaving a site empty between production cycles to reduce disease |
| Grading | sorting fish by size |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what seafood buyers and processors actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind stock records — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as disease outbreaks in stock
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking seafood buyers and processors to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

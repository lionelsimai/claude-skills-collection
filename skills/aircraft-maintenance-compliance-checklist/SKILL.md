# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Aircraft Maintenance

Check work in hangars and line stations against the laws, standards and codes that govern a aircraft maintenance engineer. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a aircraft maintenance engineer working with airlines, operators and passengers (indirectly) in hangars and line stations.

## When to use
- You are a aircraft maintenance engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hangars and line stations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in hangars and line stations
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and maintenance records, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching missed inspections — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Physiotherapy

Check work in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes against the laws, standards and codes that govern a physiotherapist. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a physiotherapist working with patients recovering movement, strength and function in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.

## When to use
- You are a physiotherapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and assessment records, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching falls during treatment — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

- Never let this happen: overloading healing tissue.
- Never let this happen: missed red-flag symptoms.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a physiotherapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes's rules.

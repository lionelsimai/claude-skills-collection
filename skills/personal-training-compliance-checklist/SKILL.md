# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Personal Training

Check work in gyms, studios, parks and online against the laws, standards and codes that govern a personal trainer. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a personal trainer working with fitness clients of all levels in gyms, studios, parks and online.

## When to use
- You are a personal trainer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in gyms, studios, parks and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in gyms, studios, parks and online
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and programmes, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching injuries from unsafe programming — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: injuries from unsafe programming.
- Never let this happen: health screening missed.
- Never let this happen: unsustainable diet advice.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a personal trainer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to gyms, studios, parks and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

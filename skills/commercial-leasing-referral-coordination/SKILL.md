# Referral & Coordination — Commercial Leasing

This skill is written for a commercial leasing agent working with landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices.

## When to use
- You are a commercial leasing agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial properties and agency offices.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what landlords, tenants and investors need that sits outside the commercial leasing agent scope in commercial properties and agency offices
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from heads of terms — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and time to let stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with landlords, tenants and investors: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of misdescribed premises

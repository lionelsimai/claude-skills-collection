# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Mortgage Advisory

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising mortgage adviser. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a mortgage adviser working with home buyers and homeowners in advice firms, branches and online.

## When to use
- You are a mortgage adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in advice firms, branches and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Affordability | assessment of whether a borrower can sustain repayments |
| Loan to value | the loan as a percentage of the property's value |
| Offer | the lender's formal agreement to lend |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for advice firms, branches and online and the evidence in your recent fact finds
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on unsuitable product recommendations — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for application-to-offer times
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Mortgage Advisory |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | fact finds; suitability letters; application records; product research |
| Core tools & equipment | mortgage sourcing systems; affordability calculators; CRM platforms; conveyancing trackers |
| People involved | home buyers and homeowners; the mortgage adviser; colleagues and other services in advice firms, branches and online |
| Primary record | fact finds — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in advice firms, branches and online] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [home buyers and homeowners and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in fact finds.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against application-to-offer times.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in advice firms, branches and online.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unsuitable product recommendations.
- Never let this happen: affordability miscalculated.
- Never let this happen: deadlines missed losing purchases.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a mortgage adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to advice firms, branches and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

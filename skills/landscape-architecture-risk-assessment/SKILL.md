# Risk Assessment — Landscape Architecture

Identify, score and control the risks in work for developers, councils and private clients in design studios and outdoor sites. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a landscape architect working with developers, councils and private clients in design studios and outdoor sites.

## When to use
- You are a landscape architect preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design studios and outdoor sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for developers, councils and private clients and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to planting failures
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using CAD software where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in design concepts, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in design studios and outdoor sites change

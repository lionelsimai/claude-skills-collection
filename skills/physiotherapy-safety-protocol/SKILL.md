# Safety Protocol — Physiotherapy

Work safely in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes: a practical safety protocol for a physiotherapist and patients recovering movement, strength and function. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a physiotherapist working with patients recovering movement, strength and function in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes for staff, patients recovering movement, strength and function and visitors, starting with falls during treatment
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as treatment plinths
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in assessment records
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

# Session Delivery Guide — Commercial Piloting

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a commercial pilot working with airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations.

## When to use
- You are a commercial pilot preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in cockpits and flight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Flight plan | the planned route and details filed before a flight |
| Type rating | qualification to fly a specific aircraft type |
| Sterile cockpit | periods when only flight-critical conversation is allowed |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review flight plans before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as fatigue risks
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and aircraft systems; check everything works and is safe before airlines, passengers and cargo customers arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to training check pass rates
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in cockpits and flight operations] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [airlines, passengers and cargo customers and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

# Risk Assessment — Physical Education

Identify, score and control the risks in work for school pupils and sports teams in school sports facilities and playing fields. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a PE teacher working with school pupils and sports teams in school sports facilities and playing fields.

## When to use
- You are a PE teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in school sports facilities and playing fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Aerobic fitness | the ability to sustain activity using oxygen |
| Fixture | a scheduled match against another school or team |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for school pupils and sports teams and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to injuries from poor supervision
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using fitness testing equipment where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in lesson plans, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in school sports facilities and playing fields change

## Output Format

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: injuries from poor supervision.
- Never let this happen: unsafe equipment.
- Never let this happen: exclusion of less confident pupils.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a PE teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to school sports facilities and playing fields's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

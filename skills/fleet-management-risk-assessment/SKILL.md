# Risk Assessment — Fleet Management

Identify, score and control the risks in work for businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a fleet manager working with businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices.

## When to use
- You are a fleet manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in transport depots and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Telematics | technology tracking vehicle location and use |
| Utilisation | how much of the time vehicles are productively used |
| Defect report | a driver's record of vehicle faults |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to unroadworthy vehicles used
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using telematics systems where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in vehicle records, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in transport depots and offices change

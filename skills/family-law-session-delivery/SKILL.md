# Session Delivery Guide — Family Law

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

| Disclosure | the formal exchange of financial information in family cases |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review client care letters before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as missed court deadlines
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and case management systems; check everything works and is safe before clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to case progression times
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed court deadlines.
- Never let this happen: safeguarding issues unreported.
- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a family law solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a law firm and family court's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

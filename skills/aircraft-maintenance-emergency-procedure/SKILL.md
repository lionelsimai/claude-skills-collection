# Emergency Response Procedure — Aircraft Maintenance

Respond to emergencies in hangars and line stations calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a aircraft maintenance engineer working with airlines, operators and passengers (indirectly) in hangars and line stations.

## When to use
- You are a aircraft maintenance engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hangars and line stations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for airlines, operators and passengers (indirectly), especially missed inspections
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a aircraft maintenance engineer is trained and authorised for, using aircraft tooling as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in hangars and line stations, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep airlines, operators and passengers (indirectly) and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in maintenance records afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

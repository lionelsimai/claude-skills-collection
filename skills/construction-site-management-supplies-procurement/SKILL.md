# Supplies & Procurement — Construction Site Management

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in construction sites. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a site manager working with clients, contractors and construction teams in construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a site manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on site diaries and the kit centred on project management software, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to site accidents
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: site accidents.
- Never let this happen: programme slippage hidden.
- Never let this happen: quality defects at handover.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a site manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on lift completion rates will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to construction and industrial sites, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or dropped loads

# Progress Review & Adjustment — Electrical Trades

Review progress for homeowners, businesses and contractors against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a electrician working with homeowners, businesses and contractors in homes, sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a electrician preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together installation certificates and the latest results for first-time fix rate since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with homeowners, businesses and contractors honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in homes, sites and commercial premises

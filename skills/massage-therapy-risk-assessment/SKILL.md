# Risk Assessment — Massage Therapy

Identify, score and control the risks in work for clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation in clinics, spas and sports settings. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a massage therapist working with clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation in clinics, spas and sports settings.

## When to use
- You are a massage therapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, spas and sports settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Contraindication | a condition making a treatment unsafe or requiring adaptation |
| Deep tissue | massage working on deeper layers of muscle |
| Aftercare | advice given to a client following treatment |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to contraindications missed causing harm
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using massage tables where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in client health forms, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in clinics, spas and sports settings change

- Never let this happen: contraindications missed causing harm.
- Never let this happen: boundary issues.
- Never let this happen: injuries aggravated.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a massage therapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

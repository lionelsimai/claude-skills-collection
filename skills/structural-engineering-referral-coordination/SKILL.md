# Referral & Coordination — Structural Engineering

Refer architects, builders and building owners onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a structural engineer working with architects, builders and building owners in design offices and construction sites.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what architects, builders and building owners need that sits outside the structural engineer scope in design offices and construction sites
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from structural calculations — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and calculation check completion stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with architects, builders and building owners: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of calculation errors

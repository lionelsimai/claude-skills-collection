# Quality Audit — Building Inspection

Audit the quality of building inspector and surveyor work in properties and construction sites and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a building inspector and surveyor working with homeowners, buyers and local authorities in properties and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a building inspector and surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in properties and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to report turnaround and the standards for properties and construction sites
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of survey reports and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including serious defects missed
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: serious defects missed.
- Never let this happen: unsafe structures passed.
- Never let this happen: damp misdiagnosed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a building inspector and surveyor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to properties and construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in restaurants and wine merchants for staff, restaurant guests and hospitality businesses and visitors, starting with incorrect storage spoiling stock
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as cellar management software

5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

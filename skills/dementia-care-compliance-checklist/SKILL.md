# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Dementia Care

Check work in care homes, hospitals and homes against the laws, standards and codes that govern a dementia care specialist. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a dementia care specialist working with people living with dementia and their families in care homes, hospitals and homes.

## When to use
- You are a dementia care specialist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in care homes, hospitals and homes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Life story work | gathering a person's history to personalise care |
| Capacity | a person's ability to make a specific decision |
| Reminiscence | using memories and familiar items to support wellbeing |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in care homes, hospitals and homes
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and life story documents, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching distress misread as behaviour problems — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Dementia Care |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | life story documents; care plans; behaviour records; capacity assessments |
| Core tools & equipment | memory aids; reminiscence materials; monitoring technology; recording systems |
| People involved | people living with dementia and their families; the dementia care specialist; colleagues and other services in care homes, hospitals and homes |
| Primary record | life story documents — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in care homes, hospitals and homes] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [people living with dementia and their families and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: distress misread as behaviour problems.
- Never let this happen: unsafe wandering.
- Never let this happen: families not involved.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a dementia care specialist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to care homes, hospitals and homes's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

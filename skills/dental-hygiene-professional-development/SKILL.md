# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Dental Hygiene

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising dental hygienist. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a dental hygienist working with patients needing preventive oral care and gum treatment in a dental practice.

## When to use
- You are a dental hygienist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a dental practice.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Plaque | a film of bacteria that forms on teeth |
| Periodontal | relating to the gums and supporting structures of teeth |
| Calculus | hardened plaque deposits on teeth |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for a dental practice and the evidence in your recent oral health assessments
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on gum disease progression missed — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for periodontal improvement scores
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Dental Hygiene |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | oral health assessments; scaling records; periodontal charts; oral hygiene advice notes |
| Core tools & equipment | ultrasonic scalers; polishing equipment; periodontal probes; fluoride application kits |
| People involved | patients needing preventive oral care and gum treatment; the dental hygienist; colleagues and other services in a dental practice |
| Primary record | oral health assessments — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a dental practice] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients needing preventive oral care and gum treatment and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in oral health assessments.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against periodontal improvement scores.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a dental practice.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: gum disease progression missed.
- Never let this happen: infection control lapses.
- Never let this happen: patient anxiety unaddressed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a dental hygienist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a dental practice's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Risk Assessment — Executive Chef Practice

Identify, score and control the risks in work for diners, kitchen teams and restaurant owners in professional kitchens. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a executive chef working with diners, kitchen teams and restaurant owners in professional kitchens.

## When to use
- You are a executive chef preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in professional kitchens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for diners, kitchen teams and restaurant owners and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to food poisoning risks
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using kitchen equipment where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in menu plans, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in professional kitchens change

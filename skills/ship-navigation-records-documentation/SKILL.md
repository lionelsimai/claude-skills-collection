# Records & Documentation — Ship Navigation

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with ship owners, crews and port authorities. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a ship's navigation officer working with ship owners, crews and port authorities in ships' bridges.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use voyage plans as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format ships' bridges requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed voyage plan compliance exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in ships' bridges, guarding against groundings and collisions
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

# Intake & Initial Assessment — Estate Agency

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold market appraisals
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from home sellers, buyers and landlords, using the standard intake structure for estate agency offices and properties
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to instruction conversion, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially misrepresentation of properties — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for home sellers, buyers and landlords in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of market appraisals

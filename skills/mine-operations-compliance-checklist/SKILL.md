# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Mine Operations

Check work in mines and processing plants against the laws, standards and codes that govern a mine operations manager. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a mine operations manager working with mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a mine operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mines and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in mines and processing plants
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and production reports, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching underground incidents — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

# Referral & Coordination — Marine Engineering

Refer ship owners, operators and crews onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a marine engineer working with ship owners, operators and crews in ships' engine rooms and shipyards.

## When to use
- You are a marine engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in ships' engine rooms and shipyards.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what ship owners, operators and crews need that sits outside the marine engineer scope in ships' engine rooms and shipyards
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from maintenance logs — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and machinery downtime stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with ship owners, operators and crews: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of machinery failures at sea

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: machinery failures at sea.
- Never let this happen: oil pollution incidents.
- Never let this happen: burns and injuries.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a marine engineer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to ships' engine rooms and shipyards's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

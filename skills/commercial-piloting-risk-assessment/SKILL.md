# Risk Assessment — Commercial Piloting

Identify, score and control the risks in work for airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a commercial pilot working with airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations.

## When to use
- You are a commercial pilot preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in cockpits and flight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for airlines, passengers and cargo customers and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to fatigue risks
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using aircraft systems where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in flight plans, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in cockpits and flight operations change

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in cockpits and flight operations] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [airlines, passengers and cargo customers and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

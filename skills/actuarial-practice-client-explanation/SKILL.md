# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Actuarial Practice

Explain complex actuary matters to insurers, pension schemes and regulators in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a actuary working with insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices.

## When to use
- You are a actuary preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance and pensions offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what insurers, pension schemes and regulators actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind valuation reports — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as model errors affecting reserves
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking insurers, pension schemes and regulators to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: model errors affecting reserves.
- Never let this happen: stale assumptions.
- Never let this happen: regulatory breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a actuary; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to insurance and pensions offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Family Law

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe family law solicitor practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Disclosure | the formal exchange of financial information in family cases |
| Consent order | an agreed financial settlement approved by the court |
| Non-molestation order | a court order protecting a person from abuse |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in a law firm and family court
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using case management systems under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching missed court deadlines — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from client care letters, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Output Format

The finished output is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against case progression times.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a law firm and family court.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed court deadlines.
- Never let this happen: safeguarding issues unreported.
- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a family law solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a law firm and family court's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

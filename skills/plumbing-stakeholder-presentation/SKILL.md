# Stakeholder Presentation — Plumbing

Present plumber findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a plumber working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, building sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a plumber preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, building sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| First fix | the initial installation stage of pipework |
| Pressure test | checking a system for leaks under pressure |
| Backflow | contaminated water flowing back into the clean supply |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from job sheets and movement in first-time fix rate, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including water damage from leaks and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in homes, building sites and commercial premises
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

# Quality Audit — Conservation Architecture

Audit the quality of conservation architect work in historic buildings and design studios and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a conservation architect working with heritage bodies, owners of historic buildings and councils in historic buildings and design studios.

## When to use
- You are a conservation architect preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in historic buildings and design studios.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to survey turnaround and the standards for historic buildings and design studios
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of condition surveys and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including inappropriate repairs damaging fabric
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

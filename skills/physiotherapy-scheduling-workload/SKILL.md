# Scheduling & Workload Management — Physiotherapy

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a physiotherapist in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a physiotherapist working with patients recovering movement, strength and function in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive functional outcome improvement
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind assessment records — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving falls during treatment) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or treatment plinths availability

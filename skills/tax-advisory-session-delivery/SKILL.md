# Session Delivery Guide — Tax Advisory

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a tax adviser working with individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## When to use
- You are a tax adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy or advisory firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review tax computations before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as missed filing deadlines
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and tax software; check everything works and is safe before individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to filing deadline compliance
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

- Never let this happen: missed filing deadlines.
- Never let this happen: aggressive schemes challenged.
- Never let this happen: relief claims overlooked.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a tax adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

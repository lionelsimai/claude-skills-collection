# Safety Protocol — Structural Engineering

Work safely in design offices and construction sites: a practical safety protocol for a structural engineer and architects, builders and building owners. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a structural engineer working with architects, builders and building owners in design offices and construction sites.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in design offices and construction sites for staff, architects, builders and building owners and visitors, starting with calculation errors
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as structural analysis software
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in structural calculations
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

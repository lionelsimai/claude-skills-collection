# Emergency Response Procedure — Food Processing

Respond to emergencies in food factories and processing plants calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a food production manager working with retailers, wholesalers and consumers in food factories and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a food production manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in food factories and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for retailers, wholesalers and consumers, especially contamination incidents
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a food production manager is trained and authorised for, using processing machinery as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in food factories and processing plants, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep retailers, wholesalers and consumers and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in production plans afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

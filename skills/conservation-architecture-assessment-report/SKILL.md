# Assessment & Findings Report — Conservation Architecture

Assess heritage bodies, owners of historic buildings and councils and write a defensible findings report as a conservation architect. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a conservation architect working with heritage bodies, owners of historic buildings and councils in historic buildings and design studios.

## When to use
- You are a conservation architect preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in historic buildings and design studios.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and condition surveys so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using survey equipment, following the recognised method for historic buildings and design studios
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind survey turnaround and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for inappropriate repairs damaging fabric; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

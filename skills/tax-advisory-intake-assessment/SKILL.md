# Intake & Initial Assessment — Tax Advisory

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a tax adviser working with individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## When to use
- You are a tax adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy or advisory firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially missed filing deadlines — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding

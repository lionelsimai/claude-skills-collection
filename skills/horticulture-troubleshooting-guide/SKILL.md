# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Horticulture

Diagnose and resolve common problems a horticulturist and grower meets in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a horticulturist and grower working with garden centres, landscapers and the public in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.

## When to use
- You are a horticulturist and grower preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Propagation | producing new plants from seeds, cuttings or division |
| Hardening off | acclimatising indoor-grown plants to outdoor conditions |
| Integrated pest management | combining methods to control pests with minimal chemicals |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in planting plans — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to pest outbreaks early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using glasshouse systems, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against crop success rates that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: pest outbreaks.
- Never let this happen: plant disease spread.
- Never let this happen: irrigation failures.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a horticulturist and grower; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to nurseries, glasshouses and gardens's rules.

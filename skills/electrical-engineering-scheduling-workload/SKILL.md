# Scheduling & Workload Management — Electrical Engineering

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a electrical engineer in design offices, plants and substations. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a electrical engineer working with energy, manufacturing and building clients in design offices, plants and substations.

- You are a electrical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, plants and substations.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive test pass rates
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in design offices, plants and substations first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind circuit designs — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving electric shock risks) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or design software availability

# Supplies & Procurement — Corporate Banking

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in bank offices and client sites. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a corporate relationship manager working with companies and institutions in bank offices and client sites.

## When to use
- You are a corporate relationship manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank offices and client sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Facility | an agreed lending arrangement such as a loan or overdraft |
| Covenant | a condition a borrower must maintain |
| Credit paper | the document presenting a lending case for approval |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on credit papers and the kit centred on lending systems, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to credit losses from weak analysis
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: credit losses from weak analysis.
- Never let this happen: covenant breaches spotted late.
- Never let this happen: concentration risks.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a corporate relationship manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank offices and client sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Safety Protocol — Carpentry

Work safely in workshops and construction sites: a practical safety protocol for a carpenter and homeowners, builders and commercial clients. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a carpenter working with homeowners, builders and commercial clients in workshops and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a carpenter preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in workshops and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in workshops and construction sites for staff, homeowners, builders and commercial clients and visitors, starting with machinery injuries
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as power saws
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in cutting lists
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: machinery injuries.
- Never let this happen: measurement errors wasting material.
- Never let this happen: structural work done wrong.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a carpenter; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to workshops and construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

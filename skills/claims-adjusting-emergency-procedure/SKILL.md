# Emergency Response Procedure — Claims Adjusting

Respond to emergencies in insurance offices and loss sites calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a claims adjuster working with policyholders, insurers and repairers in insurance offices and loss sites.

## When to use
- You are a claims adjuster preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance offices and loss sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Reserve | money set aside for the expected cost of a claim |
| Leakage | claim payments higher than they should be |
| Indemnity | restoring the insured to their pre-loss position |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for policyholders, insurers and repairers, especially fraudulent claims paid
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a claims adjuster is trained and authorised for, using claims systems as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in insurance offices and loss sites, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep policyholders, insurers and repairers and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in claims files afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

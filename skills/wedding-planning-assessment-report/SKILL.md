# Assessment & Findings Report — Wedding Planning

Assess couples and their families and write a defensible findings report as a wedding planner. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a wedding planner working with couples and their families in venues, suppliers and planning offices.

## When to use
- You are a wedding planner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in venues, suppliers and planning offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and planning timelines so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using planning software, following the recognised method for venues, suppliers and planning offices
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind milestone completion and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for supplier failures on the day; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

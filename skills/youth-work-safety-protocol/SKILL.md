# Safety Protocol — Youth Work

Work safely in youth centres, schools and outreach settings: a practical safety protocol for a youth worker and young people and their communities. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a youth worker working with young people and their communities in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.

## When to use
- You are a youth worker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in youth centres, schools and outreach settings for staff, young people and their communities and visitors, starting with safeguarding disclosures missed
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as engagement tools
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in session plans
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in youth centres, schools and outreach settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [young people and their communities and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding disclosures missed.
- Never let this happen: unsafe activities.
- Never let this happen: young people disengaging.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a youth worker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to youth centres, schools and outreach settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Emergency Response Procedure — Commercial Leasing

This skill is written for a commercial leasing agent working with landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for landlords, tenants and investors, especially misdescribed premises
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a commercial leasing agent is trained and authorised for, using property portals as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in commercial properties and agency offices, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep landlords, tenants and investors and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in heads of terms afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

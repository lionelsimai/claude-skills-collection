# Service Plan Design — Massage Therapy

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation as a massage therapist. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a massage therapist working with clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation in clinics, spas and sports settings.

## When to use
- You are a massage therapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, spas and sports settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Contraindication | a condition making a treatment unsafe or requiring adaptation |
| Deep tissue | massage working on deeper layers of muscle |
| Aftercare | advice given to a client following treatment |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation — specific enough to measure against client rebooking rates
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for clinics, spas and sports settings, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around client health forms so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, massage tables and materials, with contingencies for contraindications missed causing harm
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in clinics, spas and sports settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in client health forms.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against client rebooking rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in clinics, spas and sports settings.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: contraindications missed causing harm.
- Never let this happen: boundary issues.
- Never let this happen: injuries aggravated.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a massage therapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clinics, spas and sports settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

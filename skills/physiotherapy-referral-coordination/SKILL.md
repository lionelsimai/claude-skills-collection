# Referral & Coordination — Physiotherapy

Refer patients recovering movement, strength and function onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a physiotherapist working with patients recovering movement, strength and function in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.

- You are a physiotherapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what patients recovering movement, strength and function need that sits outside the physiotherapist scope in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from assessment records — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and functional outcome improvement stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with patients recovering movement, strength and function: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of falls during treatment

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: falls during treatment.
- Never let this happen: overloading healing tissue.
- Never let this happen: missed red-flag symptoms.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a physiotherapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Incident Reporting & Response — Facilities Management

Respond to, record and learn from incidents involving building occupants, owners and service teams in commercial buildings and campuses. The deliverable is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
This skill is written for a facilities manager working with building occupants, owners and service teams in commercial buildings and campuses.

## When to use
- You are a facilities manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial buildings and campuses.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| CAFM | computer-aided facilities management software |
| PPM | planned preventive maintenance scheduled in advance |
| Asset register | the inventory of building equipment and its condition |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if life-safety systems unmaintained is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in maintenance schedules
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in commercial buildings and campuses
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with building occupants, owners and service teams about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in commercial buildings and campuses] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [building occupants, owners and service teams and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against work order completion.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in commercial buildings and campuses.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: life-safety systems unmaintained.
- Never let this happen: contractor failures.
- Never let this happen: energy waste.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a facilities manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to commercial buildings and campuses's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

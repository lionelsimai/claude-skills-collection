# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Geological Surveying

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising engineering geologist and surveyor. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a engineering geologist and surveyor working with mining, construction and environmental clients in field sites and laboratories.

## When to use
- You are a engineering geologist and surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in field sites and laboratories.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Core sample | a cylinder of rock extracted by drilling |
| Overburden | material lying above the resource of interest |
| Fault | a fracture in rock along which movement has occurred |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for field sites and laboratories and the evidence in your recent survey data
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on field safety incidents — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for survey turnaround
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

| Component | Details for Geological Surveying |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in field sites and laboratories] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [mining, construction and environmental clients and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in survey data.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against survey turnaround.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in field sites and laboratories.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: field safety incidents.
- Never let this happen: sample mix-ups.
- Never let this happen: misinterpreted ground conditions.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a engineering geologist and surveyor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to field sites and laboratories's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

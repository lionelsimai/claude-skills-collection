# Quality Audit — Customs Brokerage

Audit the quality of customs broker work in customs offices and freight operations and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a customs broker working with importers and exporters in customs offices and freight operations.

## When to use
- You are a customs broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in customs offices and freight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to declaration accuracy and the standards for customs offices and freight operations
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of customs declarations and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including misclassification penalties
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misclassification penalties.
- Never let this happen: clearance delays.
- Never let this happen: prohibited goods shipped.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a customs broker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to customs offices and freight operations's rules.

# Case Conference & Meeting Notes — Tax Law

Prepare for, contribute to and record case conferences and multi-professional meetings. The deliverable is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
This skill is written for a tax solicitor working with individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions in a law firm and tax tribunals.

## When to use
- You are a tax solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and tax tribunals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Clearance | advance confirmation from a tax authority about treatment of a transaction |
| Disclosure | informing the tax authority of relevant facts |
| Penalty mitigation | reducing penalties through cooperation and care |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Prepare a one-page brief from tax opinions: current position, key evidence, the questions the meeting must answer
2. **Step 2** - Present your contribution concisely, leading with the position on opinion turnaround and any live concerns about filing deadlines missed
3. **Step 3** - Listen for information that changes the picture; update your understanding rather than defending a position
4. **Step 4** - Record decisions, actions, owners and deadlines during the meeting — not from memory afterwards
5. **Step 5** - Circulate the record promptly to attendees in a law firm and tax tribunals, correct errors fast, and file it with the main record

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a law firm and tax tribunals] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions and other stakeholders]

The finished output is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: filing deadlines missed.
- Never let this happen: penalties from wrong advice.
- Never let this happen: privilege mishandled.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a tax solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a law firm and tax tribunals's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Intellectual Property Law

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a intellectual property solicitor relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a intellectual property solicitor working with creators, brands and technology companies in a law firm and intellectual property offices.

## When to use
- You are a intellectual property solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and intellectual property offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Trade mark | a sign distinguishing one business's goods or services |
| Infringement | unauthorised use of protected intellectual property |
| Licence | permission to use intellectual property on agreed terms |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in a law firm and intellectual property offices, centred on IP management systems, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against trade mark applications
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to missed renewal deadlines
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

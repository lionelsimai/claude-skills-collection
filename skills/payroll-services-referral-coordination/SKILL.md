# Referral & Coordination — Payroll Services

Refer employers and their staff onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a payroll specialist working with employers and their staff in a payroll bureau or finance department.

## When to use
- You are a payroll specialist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a payroll bureau or finance department.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Gross pay | pay before deductions |
| Statutory payments | legally required payments such as sick or parental pay |
| Auto-enrolment | automatically enrolling staff into a workplace pension |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what employers and their staff need that sits outside the payroll specialist scope in a payroll bureau or finance department
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from payroll reports — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and pay run accuracy stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with employers and their staff: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of wrong pay reaching staff

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a payroll bureau or finance department] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [employers and their staff and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: wrong pay reaching staff.
- Never let this happen: pension contribution errors.
- Never let this happen: statutory deadlines missed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a payroll specialist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a payroll bureau or finance department's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Incident Reporting & Response — Criminal Defence Law

This skill is written for a criminal defence solicitor working with clients accused of criminal offences in police stations, law firms and criminal courts.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if missed disclosure deadlines is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in police station attendance notes
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in police stations, law firms and criminal courts
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with clients accused of criminal offences about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

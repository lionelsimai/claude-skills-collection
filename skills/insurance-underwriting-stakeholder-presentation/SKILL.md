# Stakeholder Presentation — Insurance Underwriting

Present insurance underwriter findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a insurance underwriter working with brokers, policyholders and insurers in insurance offices.

## When to use
- You are a insurance underwriter preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Loss ratio | claims costs as a share of premium earned |

| Referral | passing a risk to a more senior underwriter |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from risk assessments and movement in quote turnaround, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including underpriced risks and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in insurance offices
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

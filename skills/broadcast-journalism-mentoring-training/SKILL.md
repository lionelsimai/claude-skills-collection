# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Broadcast Journalism

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe broadcast journalist practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a broadcast journalist working with audiences, contributors and news editors in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.

## When to use
- You are a broadcast journalist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Running order | the planned sequence of items in a programme |
| Compliance | checking content against broadcast rules and law |
| Package | a pre-recorded report within a programme |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in newsrooms and outside broadcasts
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using broadcast cameras under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching inaccurate reporting broadcast — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from running orders, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Output Format

The finished output is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in running orders.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against audience figures.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: inaccurate reporting broadcast.
- Never let this happen: contributor harm.
- Never let this happen: legal risks in reports.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a broadcast journalist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to newsrooms and outside broadcasts's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

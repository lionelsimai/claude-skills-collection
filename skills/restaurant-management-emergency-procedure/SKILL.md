# Emergency Response Procedure — Restaurant Management

Respond to emergencies in restaurants and cafes calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams, especially food hygiene failures
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a restaurant manager is trained and authorised for, using point of sale systems as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in restaurants and cafes, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in menus and costing sheets afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

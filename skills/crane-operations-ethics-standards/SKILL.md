# Ethics & Professional Standards — Crane Operations

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a crane operator. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a crane operator working with construction and industrial clients in construction and industrial sites.

## When to use
- You are a crane operator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction and industrial sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in construction and industrial sites: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect construction and industrial clients first where duties conflict, and be alert to how dropped loads can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from lift plans, and support colleagues who do the same

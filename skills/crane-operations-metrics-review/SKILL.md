# Metrics & Outcomes Review — Crane Operations

Track, interpret and act on the outcome metrics that matter for a crane operator. The deliverable is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
This skill is written for a crane operator working with construction and industrial clients in construction and industrial sites.

## When to use
- You are a crane operator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction and industrial sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Lift plan | the documented method for a lifting operation |
| Slinger | the person attaching loads to the crane |
| Rated capacity | the maximum load a crane can lift in a configuration |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define each metric precisely — start with lift completion rates — including source data in lift plans and collection method
2. **Step 2** - Set review cadence and comparison baselines so trends, not single data points, drive decisions
3. **Step 3** - Investigate movements before celebrating or panicking: check data quality, case mix and one-off causes
4. **Step 4** - Link metric movements to practice changes and to risks such as dropped loads; test one change at a time where possible
5. **Step 5** - Report results honestly to stakeholders in construction and industrial sites, including where outcomes fell short and what changes

- Never let this happen: dropped loads.
- Never let this happen: crane overload.
- Never let this happen: weather-related incidents.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a crane operator; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to construction and industrial sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

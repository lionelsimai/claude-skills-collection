# Ethics & Professional Standards — Urban Planning

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a town planner. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a town planner working with developers, councils and communities in planning departments and consultancy offices.

## When to use
- You are a town planner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in planning departments and consultancy offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Local plan | the statutory document guiding development in an area |
| Determination | the formal decision on a planning application |
| Material consideration | a planning factor legally relevant to a decision |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in planning departments and consultancy offices: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect developers, councils and communities first where duties conflict, and be alert to how missed consultation deadlines can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from planning applications, and support colleagues who do the same

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed consultation deadlines.
- Never let this happen: policy conflicts.
- Never let this happen: appeals lost on process.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a town planner; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to planning departments and consultancy offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# New Client Onboarding Pack — Gastroenterology

Onboard patients with digestive system conditions smoothly into a gastroenterologist's service in an endoscopy unit and gastroenterology clinic. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a gastroenterologist working with patients with digestive system conditions in an endoscopy unit and gastroenterology clinic.

## When to use
- You are a gastroenterologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an endoscopy unit and gastroenterology clinic.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Endoscopy | examining the digestive tract with a flexible camera |
| Cirrhosis | advanced scarring of the liver |
| IBD | inflammatory bowel disease, mainly Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from endoscopy reports
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on endoscopy waiting times will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to an endoscopy unit and gastroenterology clinic, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or missed gastrointestinal bleeding

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in an endoscopy unit and gastroenterology clinic] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients with digestive system conditions and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against endoscopy waiting times.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in an endoscopy unit and gastroenterology clinic.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed gastrointestinal bleeding.
- Never let this happen: sedation complications.
- Never let this happen: delayed cancer pathway.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a gastroenterologist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to an endoscopy unit and gastroenterology clinic's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

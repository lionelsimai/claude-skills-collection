# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Plumbing

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a plumber relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a plumber working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, building sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a plumber preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, building sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| First fix | the initial installation stage of pipework |
| Pressure test | checking a system for leaks under pressure |
| Backflow | contaminated water flowing back into the clean supply |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in homes, building sites and commercial premises, centred on pipe threading tools, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against job sheets
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to water damage from leaks
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

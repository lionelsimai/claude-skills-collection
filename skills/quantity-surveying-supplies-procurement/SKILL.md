# Supplies & Procurement — Quantity Surveying

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in construction projects and offices. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a quantity surveyor working with developers, contractors and public clients in construction projects and offices.

## When to use
- You are a quantity surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction projects and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Bill of quantities | an itemised list of materials and work for pricing |
| Valuation | an assessment of work completed for interim payment |
| Final account | the agreed total cost at a project's end |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on bills of quantities and the kit centred on measurement software, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to cost overruns unreported
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

- Never let this happen: contractual deadlines missed.

# Quality Audit — Physiotherapy

Audit the quality of physiotherapist work in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a physiotherapist working with patients recovering movement, strength and function in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.

## When to use
- You are a physiotherapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to functional outcome improvement and the standards for a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of assessment records and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including falls during treatment
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: falls during treatment.
- Never let this happen: overloading healing tissue.
- Never let this happen: missed red-flag symptoms.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a physiotherapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Freight Forwarding

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising freight forwarder. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a freight forwarder working with importers, exporters and carriers in freight offices, ports and airports.

## When to use
- You are a freight forwarder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in freight offices, ports and airports.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Bill of lading | the transport document evidencing the carriage contract |
| Cut-off | the deadline for delivering cargo for a sailing or flight |
| Demurrage | charges for keeping containers beyond free time |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for freight offices, ports and airports and the evidence in your recent shipping documents
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on customs delays from bad documents — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for shipment on-time rate
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in freight offices, ports and airports] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [importers, exporters and carriers and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in shipping documents.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against shipment on-time rate.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in freight offices, ports and airports.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: customs delays from bad documents.
- Never let this happen: cargo damage.
- Never let this happen: missed vessel cut-offs.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a freight forwarder; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to freight offices, ports and airports's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

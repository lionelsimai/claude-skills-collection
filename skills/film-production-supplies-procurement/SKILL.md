# Supplies & Procurement — Film Production

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in production offices and filming locations. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a film producer and production manager working with production companies, cast, crew and financiers in production offices and filming locations.

## When to use
- You are a film producer and production manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in production offices and filming locations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Call sheet | the daily document listing schedule, cast and locations |

| Clearance | legal permission to use material in a production |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on production budgets and the kit centred on production management software, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to budget overruns
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

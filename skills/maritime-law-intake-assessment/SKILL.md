# Intake & Initial Assessment — Maritime Law

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for ship owners, insurers and cargo interests in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a maritime solicitor working with ship owners, insurers and cargo interests in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts.

## When to use
- You are a maritime solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold charterparties
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from ship owners, insurers and cargo interests, using the standard intake structure for a shipping law firm and admiralty courts
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to case resolution times, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially cargo deadlines missed — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for ship owners, insurers and cargo interests in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of charterparties

# Records & Documentation — Broadcast Journalism

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with audiences, contributors and news editors. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a broadcast journalist working with audiences, contributors and news editors in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.

## When to use
- You are a broadcast journalist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Running order | the planned sequence of items in a programme |
| Compliance | checking content against broadcast rules and law |
| Package | a pre-recorded report within a programme |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use running orders as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format newsrooms and outside broadcasts requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed audience figures exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in newsrooms and outside broadcasts, guarding against inaccurate reporting broadcast
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

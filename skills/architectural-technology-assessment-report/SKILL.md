# Assessment & Findings Report — Architectural Technology

Assess architects, developers and homeowners and write a defensible findings report as a architectural technologist. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a architectural technologist working with architects, developers and homeowners in design studios and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a architectural technologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design studios and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and technical drawings so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using CAD and BIM software, following the recognised method for design studios and construction sites
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind drawing issue timeliness and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for regulation non-compliance; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

- Never let this happen: detailing errors causing site problems.
- Never let this happen: clash detection missed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a architectural technologist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

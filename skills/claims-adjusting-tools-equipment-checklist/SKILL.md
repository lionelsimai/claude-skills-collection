# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Claims Adjusting

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a claims adjuster relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a claims adjuster working with policyholders, insurers and repairers in insurance offices and loss sites.

## When to use
- You are a claims adjuster preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance offices and loss sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Reserve | money set aside for the expected cost of a claim |
| Leakage | claim payments higher than they should be |
| Indemnity | restoring the insured to their pre-loss position |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in insurance offices and loss sites, centred on claims systems, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against claims files
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to fraudulent claims paid
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

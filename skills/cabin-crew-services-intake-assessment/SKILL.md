# Intake & Initial Assessment — Cabin Crew Services

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold flight reports
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from airline passengers and flight crew, using the standard intake structure for aircraft cabins
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to safety check compliance, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially evacuation readiness gaps — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for airline passengers and flight crew in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of flight reports

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: evacuation readiness gaps.
- Never let this happen: unruly passenger escalation.
- Never let this happen: galley injuries.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a cabin crew member; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to aircraft cabins's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

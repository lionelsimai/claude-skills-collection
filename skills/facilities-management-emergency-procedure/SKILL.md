# Emergency Response Procedure — Facilities Management

Respond to emergencies in commercial buildings and campuses calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a facilities manager working with building occupants, owners and service teams in commercial buildings and campuses.

## When to use
- You are a facilities manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial buildings and campuses.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for building occupants, owners and service teams, especially life-safety systems unmaintained
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a facilities manager is trained and authorised for, using CAFM systems as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in commercial buildings and campuses, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep building occupants, owners and service teams and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in maintenance schedules afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

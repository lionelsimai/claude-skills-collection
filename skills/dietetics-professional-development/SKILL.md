# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Dietetics

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising dietitian. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a dietitian working with patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment in hospital wards, clinics and community settings.

## When to use
- You are a dietitian preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hospital wards, clinics and community settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Enteral feeding | nutrition delivered through a tube into the stomach or bowel |
| Malnutrition | deficiency or imbalance of nutrients harming health |
| BMI | body mass index, weight relative to height |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for hospital wards, clinics and community settings and the evidence in your recent nutrition assessments
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on malnutrition unrecognised — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for screening completion rate
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Dietetics |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | nutrition assessments; diet plans; enteral feeding charts; weight records |
| Core tools & equipment | anthropometric equipment; feeding pumps; nutrition screening tools; body composition analysers |
| People involved | patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment; the dietitian; colleagues and other services in hospital wards, clinics and community settings |
| Primary record | nutrition assessments — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in hospital wards, clinics and community settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients needing nutrition support or dietary treatment and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in nutrition assessments.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against screening completion rate.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in hospital wards, clinics and community settings.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: malnutrition unrecognised.
- Never let this happen: refeeding syndrome.
- Never let this happen: unsafe feeding regimens.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a dietitian; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hospital wards, clinics and community settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

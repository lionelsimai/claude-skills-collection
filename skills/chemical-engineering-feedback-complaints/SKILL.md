# Feedback & Complaints Handling — Chemical Engineering

Collect feedback from process plants and manufacturing clients and handle complaints fairly and visibly. The deliverable is a feedback and complaints process with response standards and learning log.
This skill is written for a chemical engineer working with process plants and manufacturing clients in chemical plants and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a chemical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in chemical plants and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a feedback and complaints process with response standards and learning log.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Invite feedback routinely — not only when things go wrong — and make it easy to give in chemical plants and design offices
2. **Step 2** - Acknowledge complaints quickly, thank the person, and explain the process and timeframe honestly
3. **Step 3** - Investigate from evidence in process flow diagrams; separate the facts from the feelings, and respect both
4. **Step 4** - Respond with findings, an apology where warranted, and concrete remedies or explanations
5. **Step 5** - Log themes and feed them into quality work — repeated complaints about process safety incidents are an early-warning system, not a nuisance

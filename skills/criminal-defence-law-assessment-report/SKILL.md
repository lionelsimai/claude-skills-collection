# Assessment & Findings Report — Criminal Defence Law

Assess clients accused of criminal offences and write a defensible findings report as a criminal defence solicitor. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a criminal defence solicitor working with clients accused of criminal offences in police stations, law firms and criminal courts.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and police station attendance notes so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using case management systems, following the recognised method for police stations, law firms and criminal courts
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind case turnaround and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for missed disclosure deadlines; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

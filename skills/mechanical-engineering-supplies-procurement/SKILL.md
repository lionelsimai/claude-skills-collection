# Supplies & Procurement — Mechanical Engineering

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in design offices, factories and test facilities. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a mechanical engineer working with manufacturing and product clients in design offices, factories and test facilities.

## When to use
- You are a mechanical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, factories and test facilities.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tolerance | the permitted variation in a dimension |
| Fatigue | weakening of material from repeated loading |
| Prototype | an early version built to test a design |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on design files and the kit centred on CAD software, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to component failures
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

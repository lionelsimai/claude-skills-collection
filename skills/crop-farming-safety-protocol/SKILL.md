# Safety Protocol — Crop Farming

Work safely in farms and fields: a practical safety protocol for a crop farmer and agronomist and food companies, markets and land owners. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a crop farmer and agronomist working with food companies, markets and land owners in farms and fields.

## When to use
- You are a crop farmer and agronomist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in farms and fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in farms and fields for staff, food companies, markets and land owners and visitors, starting with pesticide misuse
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as tractors and implements
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in crop plans
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: pesticide misuse.
- Never let this happen: crop failure from weather.
- Never let this happen: soil degradation.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a crop farmer and agronomist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to farms and fields's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

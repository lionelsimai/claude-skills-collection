# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Bartending

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a bartender relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a bartender working with bar customers and venue managers in bars, restaurants and event venues.

## When to use
- You are a bartender preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bars, restaurants and event venues.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Stock variance | the difference between recorded and actual stock |
| Free pouring | pouring measures without a measure, requiring training |
| Cellar management | storing and rotating drinks stock correctly |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in bars, restaurants and event venues, centred on point of sale systems, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against stock counts
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to over-service of alcohol
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: over-service of alcohol.
- Never let this happen: glass injuries.
- Never let this happen: stock theft.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a bartender; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bars, restaurants and event venues's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

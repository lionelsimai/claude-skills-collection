# Service Plan Design — Music Education

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for music students of all ages and their parents as a music teacher. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a music teacher working with music students of all ages and their parents in music services, schools and private studios.

## When to use
- You are a music teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in music services, schools and private studios.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with music students of all ages and their parents — specific enough to measure against examination results
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for music services, schools and private studios, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around lesson plans so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, instruments and materials, with contingencies for hearing damage from loud practice
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

# Quality Audit — Broadcast Journalism

Audit the quality of broadcast journalist work in newsrooms and outside broadcasts and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a broadcast journalist working with audiences, contributors and news editors in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.

## When to use
- You are a broadcast journalist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to audience figures and the standards for newsrooms and outside broadcasts
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of running orders and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including inaccurate reporting broadcast
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

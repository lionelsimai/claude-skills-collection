# Quality Audit — Mine Operations

Audit the quality of mine operations manager work in mines and processing plants and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a mine operations manager working with mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a mine operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mines and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to production tonnage and the standards for mines and processing plants
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of production reports and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including underground incidents
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

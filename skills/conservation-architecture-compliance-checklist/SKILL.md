# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Conservation Architecture

Check work in historic buildings and design studios against the laws, standards and codes that govern a conservation architect. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a conservation architect working with heritage bodies, owners of historic buildings and councils in historic buildings and design studios.

## When to use
- You are a conservation architect preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in historic buildings and design studios.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Listed building | a building protected by law for its heritage value |
| Fabric | the physical material of a historic building |
| Reversibility | repairs that can be undone without harming original material |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in historic buildings and design studios
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and condition surveys, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching inappropriate repairs damaging fabric — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

# Ethics & Professional Standards — Actuarial Practice

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a actuary. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a actuary working with insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices.

## When to use
- You are a actuary preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance and pensions offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in insurance and pensions offices: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect insurers, pension schemes and regulators first where duties conflict, and be alert to how model errors affecting reserves can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from valuation reports, and support colleagues who do the same

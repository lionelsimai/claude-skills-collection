## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for construction and industrial clients and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to dropped loads
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using tower and mobile cranes where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in lift plans, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in construction and industrial sites change

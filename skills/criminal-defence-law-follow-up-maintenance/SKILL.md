# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Criminal Defence Law

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how case turnaround is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using case management systems where measurement is needed, and record it in police station attendance notes
3. **Step 3** - Give clients accused of criminal offences clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to missed disclosure deadlines shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

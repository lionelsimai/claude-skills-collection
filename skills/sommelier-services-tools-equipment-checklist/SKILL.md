# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Sommelier Services

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a sommelier relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a sommelier working with restaurant guests and hospitality businesses in restaurants and wine merchants.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in restaurants and wine merchants, centred on cellar management software, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against wine lists
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to incorrect storage spoiling stock
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

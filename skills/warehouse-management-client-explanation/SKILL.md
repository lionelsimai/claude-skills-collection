# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Warehouse Management

Explain complex warehouse manager matters to retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a warehouse manager working with retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients in warehouses and distribution centres.

## When to use
- You are a warehouse manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in warehouses and distribution centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Pick accuracy | how often the correct items are picked |
| Goods-in | receiving and checking deliveries |
| Cycle counting | counting sections of stock on a rolling basis |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind inventory records — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as forklift accidents
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking retailers, manufacturers and distribution clients to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

- Never let this happen: stock shrinkage.
- Never let this happen: picking errors shipped.

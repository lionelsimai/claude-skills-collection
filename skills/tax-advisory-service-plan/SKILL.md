# Service Plan Design — Tax Advisory

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs as a tax adviser. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a tax adviser working with individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## When to use
- You are a tax adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy or advisory firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs — specific enough to measure against filing deadline compliance
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for an accountancy or advisory firm, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around tax computations so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, tax software and materials, with contingencies for missed filing deadlines
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in an accountancy or advisory firm] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against filing deadline compliance.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed filing deadlines.
- Never let this happen: aggressive schemes challenged.
- Never let this happen: relief claims overlooked.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a tax adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to an accountancy or advisory firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

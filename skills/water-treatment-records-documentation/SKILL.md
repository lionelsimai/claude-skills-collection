# Records & Documentation — Water Treatment

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use process logs as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format treatment works and pumping stations requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed water quality compliance exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in treatment works and pumping stations, guarding against contamination reaching supply
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

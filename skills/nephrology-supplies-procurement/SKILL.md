# Supplies & Procurement — Nephrology

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in a renal unit and dialysis centre. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a nephrologist working with patients with kidney disease, including dialysis patients in a renal unit and dialysis centre.

## When to use
- You are a nephrologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a renal unit and dialysis centre.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Dialysis | artificial filtering of the blood when kidneys fail |
| GFR | glomerular filtration rate, a measure of kidney function |
| Fistula | a surgically created connection used for dialysis access |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on dialysis prescriptions and the kit centred on haemodialysis machines, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to missed hyperkalaemia
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a renal unit and dialysis centre] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients with kidney disease, including dialysis patients and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed hyperkalaemia.
- Never let this happen: dialysis access infection.
- Never let this happen: fluid overload.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a nephrologist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a renal unit and dialysis centre's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

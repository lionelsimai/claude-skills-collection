# Referral & Coordination — Demolition Services

Refer developers, councils and property owners onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a demolition contractor working with developers, councils and property owners in demolition sites.

## When to use
- You are a demolition contractor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in demolition sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what developers, councils and property owners need that sits outside the demolition contractor scope in demolition sites
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from demolition plans — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and project completion times stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with developers, councils and property owners: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of asbestos exposure

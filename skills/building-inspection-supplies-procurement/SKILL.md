# Supplies & Procurement — Building Inspection

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in properties and construction sites. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a building inspector and surveyor working with homeowners, buyers and local authorities in properties and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a building inspector and surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in properties and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on survey reports and the kit centred on moisture meters, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to serious defects missed
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

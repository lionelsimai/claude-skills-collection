# Supplies & Procurement — Chemical Engineering

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in chemical plants and design offices. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a chemical engineer working with process plants and manufacturing clients in chemical plants and design offices.

## When to use

- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on process flow diagrams and the kit centred on process simulators, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to process safety incidents
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

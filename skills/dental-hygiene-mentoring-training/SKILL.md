# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Dental Hygiene

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe dental hygienist practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a dental hygienist working with patients needing preventive oral care and gum treatment in a dental practice.

## When to use
- You are a dental hygienist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a dental practice.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Plaque | a film of bacteria that forms on teeth |
| Periodontal | relating to the gums and supporting structures of teeth |
| Calculus | hardened plaque deposits on teeth |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in a dental practice
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using ultrasonic scalers under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching gum disease progression missed — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from oral health assessments, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

|-----------|-------------------------------|

| Core tools & equipment | ultrasonic scalers; polishing equipment; periodontal probes; fluoride application kits |

| Primary record | oral health assessments — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a dental practice] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients needing preventive oral care and gum treatment and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in oral health assessments.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against periodontal improvement scores.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a dental practice.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: gum disease progression missed.
- Never let this happen: infection control lapses.
- Never let this happen: patient anxiety unaddressed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a dental hygienist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a dental practice's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

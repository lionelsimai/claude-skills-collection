# Emergency Response Procedure — Freight Forwarding

Respond to emergencies in freight offices, ports and airports calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a freight forwarder working with importers, exporters and carriers in freight offices, ports and airports.

## When to use
- You are a freight forwarder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in freight offices, ports and airports.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for importers, exporters and carriers, especially customs delays from bad documents
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a freight forwarder is trained and authorised for, using freight management systems as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in freight offices, ports and airports, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep importers, exporters and carriers and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in shipping documents afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

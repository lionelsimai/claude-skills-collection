# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Massage Therapy

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising massage therapist. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a massage therapist working with clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation in clinics, spas and sports settings.

## When to use
- You are a massage therapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, spas and sports settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Contraindication | a condition making a treatment unsafe or requiring adaptation |
| Deep tissue | massage working on deeper layers of muscle |
| Aftercare | advice given to a client following treatment |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for clinics, spas and sports settings and the evidence in your recent client health forms
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on contraindications missed causing harm — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for client rebooking rates
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Massage Therapy |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | client health forms; treatment notes; appointment records; aftercare advice |
| Core tools & equipment | massage tables; oils and balms; hot stone equipment; booking systems |
| People involved | clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation; the massage therapist; colleagues and other services in clinics, spas and sports settings |
| Primary record | client health forms — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in clinics, spas and sports settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in client health forms.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against client rebooking rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in clinics, spas and sports settings.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: contraindications missed causing harm.
- Never let this happen: boundary issues.
- Never let this happen: injuries aggravated.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a massage therapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clinics, spas and sports settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

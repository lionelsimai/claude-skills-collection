## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to tour ratings and the standards for tourist sites, cities and attractions
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of tour itineraries and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including group members lost
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

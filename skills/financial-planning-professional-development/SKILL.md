# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Financial Planning

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising financial planner. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a financial planner working with individuals and families planning their financial future in a financial planning firm and client meetings.

## When to use
- You are a financial planner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a financial planning firm and client meetings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Fact find | the detailed gathering of a client's circumstances and goals |
| Suitability | how well a recommendation matches a client's needs and risk tolerance |
| Cashflow modelling | projecting income, spending and assets over a lifetime |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for a financial planning firm and client meetings and the evidence in your recent fact finds
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on unsuitable recommendations — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for review completion rates
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Financial Planning |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | fact finds; suitability reports; cashflow models; review documents |
| Core tools & equipment | cashflow modelling software; risk profiling tools; research databases; client portals |
| People involved | individuals and families planning their financial future; the financial planner; colleagues and other services in a financial planning firm and client meetings |
| Primary record | fact finds — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a financial planning firm and client meetings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [individuals and families planning their financial future and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in fact finds.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against review completion rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a financial planning firm and client meetings.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unsuitable recommendations.
- Never let this happen: risk profiles mismatched.
- Never let this happen: reviews overdue.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a financial planner; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a financial planning firm and client meetings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

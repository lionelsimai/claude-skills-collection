# Safety Protocol — Aquaculture

Work safely in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms: a practical safety protocol for a fish farm manager and seafood buyers and processors. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms for staff, seafood buyers and processors and visitors, starting with disease outbreaks in stock
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as feeding systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in stock records
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

# Quality Audit — Primary Education

Audit the quality of primary school teacher work in a primary school classroom and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a primary school teacher working with primary-aged pupils and their parents in a primary school classroom.

## When to use
- You are a primary school teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a primary school classroom.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to pupil progress measures and the standards for a primary school classroom
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of lesson plans and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including safeguarding disclosures mishandled
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a primary school classroom] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [primary-aged pupils and their parents and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in lesson plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against pupil progress measures.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a primary school classroom.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding disclosures mishandled.
- Never let this happen: gaps in progress unaddressed.
- Never let this happen: workload overload.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a primary school teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a primary school classroom's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

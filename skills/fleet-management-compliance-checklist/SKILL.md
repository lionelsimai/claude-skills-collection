# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Fleet Management

Check work in transport depots and offices against the laws, standards and codes that govern a fleet manager. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a fleet manager working with businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices.

## When to use
- You are a fleet manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in transport depots and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Telematics | technology tracking vehicle location and use |
| Utilisation | how much of the time vehicles are productively used |
| Defect report | a driver's record of vehicle faults |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in transport depots and offices
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and vehicle records, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching unroadworthy vehicles used — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

# Referral & Coordination — Secondary Mathematics Education

Refer secondary school students and their parents onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a secondary mathematics teacher working with secondary school students and their parents in a secondary school mathematics department.

## When to use
- You are a secondary mathematics teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a secondary school mathematics department.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Scheme of work | the planned sequence of topics across a term or year |
| Intervention | targeted extra teaching for pupils who are behind |
| Working mathematically | applying maths to problem solving, not just procedures |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what secondary school students and their parents need that sits outside the secondary mathematics teacher scope in a secondary school mathematics department
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from schemes of work — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and examination results stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with secondary school students and their parents: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of misconceptions unaddressed

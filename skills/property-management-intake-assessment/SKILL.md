# Intake & Initial Assessment — Property Management

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for landlords, tenants and leaseholders in managed properties and management offices. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a property manager working with landlords, tenants and leaseholders in managed properties and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a property manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in managed properties and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold tenancy agreements
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from landlords, tenants and leaseholders, using the standard intake structure for managed properties and management offices
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to rent collection rates, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially safety certificates expired — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for landlords, tenants and leaseholders in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of tenancy agreements

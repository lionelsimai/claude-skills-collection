# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Strata Management

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a strata manager relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a strata manager working with lot owners, owners corporations and residents in residential complexes and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a strata manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in residential complexes and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Levy | owners' regular contribution to shared costs |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in residential complexes and management offices, centred on strata software, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against meeting minutes
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to fund mismanagement
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

# Intake & Initial Assessment — Broadcast Journalism

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for audiences, contributors and news editors in newsrooms and outside broadcasts. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a broadcast journalist working with audiences, contributors and news editors in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.

## When to use
- You are a broadcast journalist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold running orders
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from audiences, contributors and news editors, using the standard intake structure for newsrooms and outside broadcasts
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to audience figures, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially inaccurate reporting broadcast — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for audiences, contributors and news editors in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of running orders

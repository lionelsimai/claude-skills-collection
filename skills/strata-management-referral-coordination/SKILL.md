# Referral & Coordination — Strata Management

Refer lot owners, owners corporations and residents onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a strata manager working with lot owners, owners corporations and residents in residential complexes and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a strata manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in residential complexes and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what lot owners, owners corporations and residents need that sits outside the strata manager scope in residential complexes and management offices
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from meeting minutes — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and levy collection rates stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with lot owners, owners corporations and residents: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of fund mismanagement

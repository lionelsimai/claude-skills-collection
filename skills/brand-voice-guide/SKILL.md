# Brand Voice Guide

## Overview
Creates comprehensive brand voice and messaging guidelines that ensure consistency across all communications.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Discover the Brand
1. **Company/personal brand**: Who are you?
2. **Target audience**: Who are you speaking to?
3. **Brand personality**: If your brand were a person, how would they talk?
4. **Competitors**: Who do you NOT want to sound like?
5. **Existing content**: Share examples of content you like

### Step 2: Define Voice Attributes

Choose 3-4 voice attributes with a spectrum:

| Attribute | We ARE | We are NOT |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| [e.g., Bold] | [Confident, direct, decisive] | [Arrogant, aggressive, reckless] |
| [e.g., Smart] | [Insightful, analytical, clear] | [Condescending, jargon-heavy, academic] |
| [e.g., Human] | [Warm, relatable, honest] | [Sappy, unprofessional, sloppy] |
| [e.g., Ambitious] | [Forward-thinking, driven] | [Hypey, unrealistic, salesy] |

### Step 3: Create the Guide

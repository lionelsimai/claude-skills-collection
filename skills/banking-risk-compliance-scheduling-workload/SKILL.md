# Scheduling & Workload Management — Banking Risk & Compliance

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a risk and compliance officer in bank risk and compliance departments. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a risk and compliance officer working with banks, regulators and boards in bank risk and compliance departments.

## When to use
- You are a risk and compliance officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank risk and compliance departments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Risk register | the recorded list of risks, owners and controls |
| Three lines | the model separating risk ownership, oversight and audit |
| Conduct risk | the risk of harm to customers or markets from behaviour |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive monitoring completion
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in bank risk and compliance departments first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind risk registers — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving undetected misconduct) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or risk systems availability

- Never let this happen: late regulatory returns.
- Never let this happen: control failures.

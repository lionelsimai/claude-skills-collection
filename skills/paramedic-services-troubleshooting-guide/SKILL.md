# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Paramedic Services

Diagnose and resolve common problems a paramedic meets in ambulances and community settings. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a paramedic working with patients with emergency and urgent care needs in the community in ambulances and community settings.

## When to use
- You are a paramedic preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in ambulances and community settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in patient report forms — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to scene safety hazards early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using ambulance equipment, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against response times that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

- Never let this happen: scene safety hazards.
- Never let this happen: delayed conveyance decisions.
- Never let this happen: incomplete handovers.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a paramedic; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to ambulances and community settings's rules.

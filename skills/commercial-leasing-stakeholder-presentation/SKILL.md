# Stakeholder Presentation — Commercial Leasing

Present commercial leasing agent findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a commercial leasing agent working with landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices.

## When to use
- You are a commercial leasing agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial properties and agency offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Heads of terms | the agreed key points before a lease is drafted |
| Break clause | a right to end a lease early on set terms |
| Alienation | transferring or sharing a lease interest |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from heads of terms and movement in time to let, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including misdescribed premises and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in commercial properties and agency offices
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

# Scheduling & Workload Management — Fleet Management

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a fleet manager in transport depots and offices. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a fleet manager working with businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices.

## When to use
- You are a fleet manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in transport depots and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Telematics | technology tracking vehicle location and use |
| Utilisation | how much of the time vehicles are productively used |
| Defect report | a driver's record of vehicle faults |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive vehicle utilisation
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in transport depots and offices first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind vehicle records — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving unroadworthy vehicles used) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or telematics systems availability

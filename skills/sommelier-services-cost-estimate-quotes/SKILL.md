# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Sommelier Services

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a sommelier. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a sommelier working with restaurant guests and hospitality businesses in restaurants and wine merchants.

## When to use
- You are a sommelier preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and wine merchants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and wine lists; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, cellar management software usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against incorrect storage spoiling stock
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as wine sales per cover
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

- Never let this happen: breakages of valuable bottles.

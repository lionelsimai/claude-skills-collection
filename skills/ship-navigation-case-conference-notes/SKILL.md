# Case Conference & Meeting Notes — Ship Navigation

Prepare for, contribute to and record case conferences and multi-professional meetings. The deliverable is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
This skill is written for a ship's navigation officer working with ship owners, crews and port authorities in ships' bridges.

## When to use
- You are a ship's navigation officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in ships' bridges.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| ECDIS | the electronic chart display and information system |
| Passage plan | the complete plan for a voyage from berth to berth |
| COLREGs | the international rules for preventing collisions at sea |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Prepare a one-page brief from voyage plans: current position, key evidence, the questions the meeting must answer
2. **Step 2** - Present your contribution concisely, leading with the position on voyage plan compliance and any live concerns about groundings and collisions
3. **Step 3** - Listen for information that changes the picture; update your understanding rather than defending a position
4. **Step 4** - Record decisions, actions, owners and deadlines during the meeting — not from memory afterwards
5. **Step 5** - Circulate the record promptly to attendees in ships' bridges, correct errors fast, and file it with the main record

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: groundings and collisions.
- Never let this happen: outdated charts.
- Never let this happen: fatigue on watch.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a ship's navigation officer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

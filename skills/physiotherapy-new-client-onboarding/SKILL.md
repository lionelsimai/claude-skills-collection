## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from assessment records
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on functional outcome improvement will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to a physiotherapy department, gym and patients' homes, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or falls during treatment

- Never let this happen: overloading healing tissue.
- Never let this happen: missed red-flag symptoms.

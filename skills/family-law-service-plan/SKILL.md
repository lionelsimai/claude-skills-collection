# Service Plan Design — Family Law

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances as a family law solicitor. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances — specific enough to measure against case progression times
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for a law firm and family court, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around client care letters so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, case management systems and materials, with contingencies for missed court deadlines
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates

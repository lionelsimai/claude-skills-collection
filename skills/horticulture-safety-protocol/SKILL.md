# Safety Protocol — Horticulture

Work safely in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens: a practical safety protocol for a horticulturist and grower and garden centres, landscapers and the public. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a horticulturist and grower working with garden centres, landscapers and the public in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.

## When to use
- You are a horticulturist and grower preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens for staff, garden centres, landscapers and the public and visitors, starting with pest outbreaks
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as glasshouse systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in planting plans
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

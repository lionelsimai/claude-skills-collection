# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Procurement Practice

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe procurement officer practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a procurement officer working with businesses and public bodies buying goods and services in procurement departments.

## When to use
- You are a procurement officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in procurement departments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tender | a formal process inviting suppliers to bid |
| Specification | the detailed requirement a supplier must meet |
| Total cost | purchase price plus all associated costs of ownership |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in procurement departments
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using e-procurement systems under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching supplier failure — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from purchase orders, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in procurement departments] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [businesses and public bodies buying goods and services and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in purchase orders.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against savings delivered.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in procurement departments.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: supplier failure.
- Never let this happen: fraud in purchasing.
- Never let this happen: contract deadlines missed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a procurement officer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to procurement departments's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Stakeholder Presentation — Facilities Management

Present facilities manager findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a facilities manager working with building occupants, owners and service teams in commercial buildings and campuses.

## When to use
- You are a facilities manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial buildings and campuses.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| CAFM | computer-aided facilities management software |
| PPM | planned preventive maintenance scheduled in advance |
| Asset register | the inventory of building equipment and its condition |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from maintenance schedules and movement in work order completion, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including life-safety systems unmaintained and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in commercial buildings and campuses
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in commercial buildings and campuses] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [building occupants, owners and service teams and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: life-safety systems unmaintained.
- Never let this happen: contractor failures.
- Never let this happen: energy waste.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a facilities manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to commercial buildings and campuses's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

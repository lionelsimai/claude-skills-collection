# Safety Protocol — Pastry & Baking

Work safely in bakeries and patisserie kitchens: a practical safety protocol for a pastry chef and baker and cafe and restaurant customers, retailers. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a pastry chef and baker working with cafe and restaurant customers, retailers in bakeries and patisserie kitchens.

## When to use
- You are a pastry chef and baker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bakeries and patisserie kitchens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in bakeries and patisserie kitchens for staff, cafe and restaurant customers, retailers and visitors, starting with allergen cross-contact
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as deck and convection ovens
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in recipes and production sheets
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

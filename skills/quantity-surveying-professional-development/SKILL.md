# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Quantity Surveying

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising quantity surveyor. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a quantity surveyor working with developers, contractors and public clients in construction projects and offices.

## When to use
- You are a quantity surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction projects and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Bill of quantities | an itemised list of materials and work for pricing |
| Valuation | an assessment of work completed for interim payment |
| Final account | the agreed total cost at a project's end |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for construction projects and offices and the evidence in your recent bills of quantities
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on cost overruns unreported — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for valuation timeliness
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in construction projects and offices] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [developers, contractors and public clients and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against valuation timeliness.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in construction projects and offices.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: cost overruns unreported.
- Never let this happen: measurement errors.
- Never let this happen: contractual deadlines missed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a quantity surveyor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to construction projects and offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Supplies & Procurement — Payment Operations

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in payment operations centres. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a payments operations specialist working with banks, fintechs and their customers in payment operations centres.

## When to use
- You are a payments operations specialist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in payment operations centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Straight-through processing | payments completed without manual intervention |
| Exception | a payment needing manual review or repair |
| Cut-off | the deadline for same-day payment processing |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on payment queues and the kit centred on payment processing systems, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to failed payments harming customers
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: failed payments harming customers.
- Never let this happen: sanctions screening misses.
- Never let this happen: fraud losses.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a payments operations specialist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to payment operations centres's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Emergency Response Procedure — Sports Coaching

Respond to emergencies in clubs, schools and performance centres calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a sports coach working with athletes and teams from grassroots to elite in clubs, schools and performance centres.

## When to use
- You are a sports coach preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clubs, schools and performance centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for athletes and teams from grassroots to elite, especially safeguarding in sport missed
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a sports coach is trained and authorised for, using video analysis tools as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in clubs, schools and performance centres, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep athletes and teams from grassroots to elite and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in session plans afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

## Output Format

The finished output is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

# Supplies & Procurement — Geological Surveying

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in field sites and laboratories. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a engineering geologist and surveyor working with mining, construction and environmental clients in field sites and laboratories.

## When to use
- You are a engineering geologist and surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in field sites and laboratories.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Core sample | a cylinder of rock extracted by drilling |
| Overburden | material lying above the resource of interest |
| Fault | a fracture in rock along which movement has occurred |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on survey data and the kit centred on drilling equipment, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to field safety incidents
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

- Never let this happen: field safety incidents.
- Never let this happen: sample mix-ups.
- Never let this happen: misinterpreted ground conditions.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a engineering geologist and surveyor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to field sites and laboratories's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

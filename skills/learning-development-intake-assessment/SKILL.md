# Intake & Initial Assessment — Learning & Development

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for employees and organisational leaders in organisations across all sectors. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a learning and development specialist working with employees and organisational leaders in organisations across all sectors.

## When to use
- You are a learning and development specialist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in organisations across all sectors.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold training needs analyses
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from employees and organisational leaders, using the standard intake structure for organisations across all sectors
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to training completion, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially training that changes nothing — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for employees and organisational leaders in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of training needs analyses

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: training that changes nothing.
- Never let this happen: accessibility overlooked.
- Never let this happen: evaluation skipped.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a learning and development specialist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to organisations across all sectors's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

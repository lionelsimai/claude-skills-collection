# Affiliate Program Designer

## Output Format

```markdown
# Affiliate Program: [Product/Company]

## Program Overview
- **Product**: [what affiliates will promote]
- **Commission**: [% or flat rate]
- **Cookie duration**: [days]
- **Payment schedule**: [when payouts happen]
- **Platform**: [recommended affiliate platform]

## Commission Structure
| Tier | Sales/Month | Commission |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Starter | 1-10 | [X%] |
| Silver | 11-25 | [X%] |
| Gold | 26-50 | [X%] |
| Platinum | 50+ | [X%] |

## Affiliate Recruitment
### Ideal Affiliates
- [Profile 1]: [why they're good + where to find them]
- [Profile 2]: [why they're good + where to find them]

### Outreach Templates
[Email templates for recruiting affiliates]

## Affiliate Resources
- Swipe copy (emails, social posts)
- Banner/graphic templates
- Product demo access
- FAQ document
- Tracking link instructions

## Terms & Conditions
[Key rules and restrictions]

## Tracking & Reporting
[What affiliates can see, how to monitor]
```

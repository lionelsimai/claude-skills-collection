# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Building Inspection

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a building inspector and surveyor. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a building inspector and surveyor working with homeowners, buyers and local authorities in properties and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a building inspector and surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in properties and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and survey reports; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, moisture meters usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against serious defects missed
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as report turnaround
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: serious defects missed.
- Never let this happen: unsafe structures passed.
- Never let this happen: damp misdiagnosed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a building inspector and surveyor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to properties and construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

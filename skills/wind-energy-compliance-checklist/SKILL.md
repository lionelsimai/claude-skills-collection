## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in wind farms onshore and offshore
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and maintenance records, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching falls from height — and record the evidence for the judgement

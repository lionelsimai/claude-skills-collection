# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Appliance Repair

Check work in customers' homes and workshops against the laws, standards and codes that govern a appliance repair technician. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a appliance repair technician working with homeowners, landlords and retailers in customers' homes and workshops.

## When to use
- You are a appliance repair technician preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in customers' homes and workshops.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Diagnostic code | a fault code displayed by an appliance's controls |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in customers' homes and workshops
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and job cards, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching electric shock — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

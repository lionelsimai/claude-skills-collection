# Referral & Coordination — Public Relations

Refer clients, journalists and stakeholders onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a public relations consultant working with clients, journalists and stakeholders in PR agencies and client organisations.

## When to use
- You are a public relations consultant preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in PR agencies and client organisations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Embargo | information released for publication at a set future time |
| Coverage | media reporting about a client or story |
| Key message | a core point a campaign aims to communicate |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what clients, journalists and stakeholders need that sits outside the public relations consultant scope in PR agencies and client organisations
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from media lists — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and coverage quality and reach stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with clients, journalists and stakeholders: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of inaccurate statements to media

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in PR agencies and client organisations] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [clients, journalists and stakeholders and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against coverage quality and reach.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in PR agencies and client organisations.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: inaccurate statements to media.
- Never let this happen: crises mishandled.
- Never let this happen: embargo breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a public relations consultant; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to PR agencies and client organisations's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

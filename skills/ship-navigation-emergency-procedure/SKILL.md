## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for ship owners, crews and port authorities, especially groundings and collisions
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a ship's navigation officer is trained and authorised for, using ECDIS chart systems as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in ships' bridges, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep ship owners, crews and port authorities and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in voyage plans afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

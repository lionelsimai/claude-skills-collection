# Metrics & Outcomes Review — Secondary Science Education

Track, interpret and act on the outcome metrics that matter for a secondary science teacher. The deliverable is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
This skill is written for a secondary science teacher working with secondary school students in a school science department and laboratories.

## When to use
- You are a secondary science teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a school science department and laboratories.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Risk assessment | evaluating hazards in practical work before it happens |
| Hypothesis | a testable prediction |
| Controlled variable | a factor kept constant in an investigation |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define each metric precisely — start with examination results — including source data in schemes of work and collection method
2. **Step 2** - Set review cadence and comparison baselines so trends, not single data points, drive decisions
3. **Step 3** - Investigate movements before celebrating or panicking: check data quality, case mix and one-off causes
4. **Step 4** - Link metric movements to practice changes and to risks such as practical safety incidents; test one change at a time where possible
5. **Step 5** - Report results honestly to stakeholders in a school science department and laboratories, including where outcomes fell short and what changes

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a school science department and laboratories] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [secondary school students and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in schemes of work.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against examination results.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a school science department and laboratories.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: practical safety incidents.
- Never let this happen: misconceptions in core concepts.
- Never let this happen: equipment faults.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a secondary science teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a school science department and laboratories's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

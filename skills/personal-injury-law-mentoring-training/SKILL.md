# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Personal Injury Law

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe personal injury solicitor practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a personal injury solicitor working with clients injured in accidents or through negligence in a law firm.

## When to use
- You are a personal injury solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Limitation | the legal deadline for starting a claim |
| Schedule of loss | a document listing the financial losses claimed |
| Liability | legal responsibility for the injury |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in a law firm
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using case management systems under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching limitation deadlines missed — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from claim files, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

| Component | Details for Personal Injury Law |

| Core tools & equipment | case management systems; medical reporting portals; costs software; rehabilitation networks |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a law firm] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [clients injured in accidents or through negligence and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in claim files.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against claim settlement values.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a law firm.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: limitation deadlines missed.
- Never let this happen: evidence lost.
- Never let this happen: under-settlement of claims.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a personal injury solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a law firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

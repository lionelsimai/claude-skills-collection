# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Commercial Piloting

Check work in cockpits and flight operations against the laws, standards and codes that govern a commercial pilot. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a commercial pilot working with airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations.

## When to use
- You are a commercial pilot preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in cockpits and flight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Flight plan | the planned route and details filed before a flight |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in cockpits and flight operations
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and flight plans, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching fatigue risks — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

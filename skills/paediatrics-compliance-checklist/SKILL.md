# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Paediatrics

Check work in a children's hospital ward and paediatric clinic against the laws, standards and codes that govern a paediatrician. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a paediatrician working with infants, children and adolescents and their parents in a children's hospital ward and paediatric clinic.

## When to use
- You are a paediatrician preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a children's hospital ward and paediatric clinic.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Developmental milestones | skills most children reach by a given age |
| Safeguarding | protecting children from abuse and neglect |
| Bronchiolitis | a common viral chest infection in infants |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in a children's hospital ward and paediatric clinic
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and growth charts, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching safeguarding concerns missed — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a children's hospital ward and paediatric clinic] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [infants, children and adolescents and their parents and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding concerns missed.
- Never let this happen: weight-based dosing errors.
- Never let this happen: delayed developmental referral.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a paediatrician; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a children's hospital ward and paediatric clinic's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Welding

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a welder relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a welder working with fabrication companies, construction and repair clients in workshops, shipyards and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a welder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in workshops, shipyards and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| WPS | a weld procedure specification defining how a weld is made |
| Penetration | how deeply a weld fuses into the base metal |
| Slag | a residue that must be removed from some welds |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in workshops, shipyards and construction sites, centred on welding machines, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against weld procedure specifications
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to weld failures in service
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: weld failures in service.
- Never let this happen: eye and skin burns.
- Never let this happen: fumes exposure.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a welder; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to workshops, shipyards and construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

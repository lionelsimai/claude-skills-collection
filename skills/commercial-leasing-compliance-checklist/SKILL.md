# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Commercial Leasing

Check work in commercial properties and agency offices against the laws, standards and codes that govern a commercial leasing agent. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a commercial leasing agent working with landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices.

## When to use
- You are a commercial leasing agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial properties and agency offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Heads of terms | the agreed key points before a lease is drafted |
| Break clause | a right to end a lease early on set terms |
| Alienation | transferring or sharing a lease interest |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in commercial properties and agency offices
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and heads of terms, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching misdescribed premises — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

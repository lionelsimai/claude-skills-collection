# Intake & Initial Assessment — Public Relations

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for clients, journalists and stakeholders in PR agencies and client organisations. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a public relations consultant working with clients, journalists and stakeholders in PR agencies and client organisations.

## When to use
- You are a public relations consultant preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in PR agencies and client organisations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Embargo | information released for publication at a set future time |
| Coverage | media reporting about a client or story |
| Key message | a core point a campaign aims to communicate |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold media lists
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from clients, journalists and stakeholders, using the standard intake structure for PR agencies and client organisations
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to coverage quality and reach, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially inaccurate statements to media — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for clients, journalists and stakeholders in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of media lists

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in PR agencies and client organisations] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [clients, journalists and stakeholders and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in media lists.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against coverage quality and reach.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in PR agencies and client organisations.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: inaccurate statements to media.
- Never let this happen: crises mishandled.
- Never let this happen: embargo breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a public relations consultant; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to PR agencies and client organisations's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

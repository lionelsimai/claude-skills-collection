# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Structural Engineering

Explain complex structural engineer matters to architects, builders and building owners in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a structural engineer working with architects, builders and building owners in design offices and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a structural engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Deflection | the bending or sagging of a structure under load |
| Foundation | the part of a structure transferring loads to the ground |
| Moment | a rotational force acting on a structure |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what architects, builders and building owners actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind structural calculations — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as calculation errors
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking architects, builders and building owners to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Aquaculture

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for seafood buyers and processors are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## When to use
- You are a fish farm manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Biomass | the total weight of stock in a system |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how growth rates is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using feeding systems where measurement is needed, and record it in stock records
3. **Step 3** - Give seafood buyers and processors clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to disease outbreaks in stock shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: disease outbreaks in stock.
- Never let this happen: escapes into the wild.
- Never let this happen: water quality crashes.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a fish farm manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to lakes, coastal sites and fish farms's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Veterinary Nursing

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising veterinary nurse. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a veterinary nurse working with animals in care and their owners in a veterinary practice.

## When to use
- You are a veterinary nurse preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a veterinary practice.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Hospitalisation chart | a record of an inpatient animal's care and observations |
| Analgesia | pain relief |
| Triage | prioritising patients by urgency in practice |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for a veterinary practice and the evidence in your recent nursing care plans
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on unrecognised pain in animals — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for patient monitoring compliance
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

| Component | Details for Veterinary Nursing |
|-----------|-------------------------------|

| Core tools & equipment | monitoring equipment; fluid therapy pumps; warming devices; laboratory analysers |
| People involved | animals in care and their owners; the veterinary nurse; colleagues and other services in a veterinary practice |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a veterinary practice] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [animals in care and their owners and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in nursing care plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against patient monitoring compliance.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a veterinary practice.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unrecognised pain in animals.
- Never let this happen: fluid therapy errors.
- Never let this happen: escape or injury in kennels.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a veterinary nurse; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a veterinary practice's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

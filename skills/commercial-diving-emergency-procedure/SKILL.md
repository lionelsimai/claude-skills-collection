# Emergency Response Procedure — Commercial Diving

Respond to emergencies in underwater work sites and support vessels calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a commercial diver working with offshore, construction and salvage clients in underwater work sites and support vessels.

## When to use
- You are a commercial diver preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in underwater work sites and support vessels.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for offshore, construction and salvage clients, especially decompression sickness
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a commercial diver is trained and authorised for, using diving equipment as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in underwater work sites and support vessels, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep offshore, construction and salvage clients and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in dive plans afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

# Quality Audit — Fleet Management

Audit the quality of fleet manager work in transport depots and offices and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a fleet manager working with businesses operating vehicle fleets and their drivers in transport depots and offices.

## When to use
- You are a fleet manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in transport depots and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Telematics | technology tracking vehicle location and use |
| Utilisation | how much of the time vehicles are productively used |
| Defect report | a driver's record of vehicle faults |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to vehicle utilisation and the standards for transport depots and offices
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of vehicle records and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including unroadworthy vehicles used
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unroadworthy vehicles used.
- Never let this happen: driver fatigue.
- Never let this happen: maintenance backlogs.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a fleet manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to transport depots and offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

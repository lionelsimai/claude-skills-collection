# Referral & Coordination — Physical Education

Refer school pupils and sports teams onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a PE teacher working with school pupils and sports teams in school sports facilities and playing fields.

## When to use
- You are a PE teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in school sports facilities and playing fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what school pupils and sports teams need that sits outside the PE teacher scope in school sports facilities and playing fields
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from lesson plans — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and participation rates stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with school pupils and sports teams: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of injuries from poor supervision

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: injuries from poor supervision.
- Never let this happen: unsafe equipment.
- Never let this happen: exclusion of less confident pupils.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a PE teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to school sports facilities and playing fields's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

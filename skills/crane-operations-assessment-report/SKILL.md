# Assessment & Findings Report — Crane Operations

This skill is written for a crane operator working with construction and industrial clients in construction and industrial sites.

## When to use
- You are a crane operator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction and industrial sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and lift plans so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using tower and mobile cranes, following the recognised method for construction and industrial sites
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind lift completion rates and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for dropped loads; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

# Ethics & Professional Standards — Freight Forwarding

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a freight forwarder. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a freight forwarder working with importers, exporters and carriers in freight offices, ports and airports.

## When to use
- You are a freight forwarder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in freight offices, ports and airports.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Bill of lading | the transport document evidencing the carriage contract |
| Cut-off | the deadline for delivering cargo for a sailing or flight |
| Demurrage | charges for keeping containers beyond free time |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in freight offices, ports and airports: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect importers, exporters and carriers first where duties conflict, and be alert to how customs delays from bad documents can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from shipping documents, and support colleagues who do the same

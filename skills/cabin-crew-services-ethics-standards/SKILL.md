# Ethics & Professional Standards — Cabin Crew Services

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a cabin crew member. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a cabin crew member working with airline passengers and flight crew in aircraft cabins.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in aircraft cabins: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect airline passengers and flight crew first where duties conflict, and be alert to how evacuation readiness gaps can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from flight reports, and support colleagues who do the same

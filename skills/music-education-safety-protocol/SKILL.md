## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in music services, schools and private studios for staff, music students of all ages and their parents and visitors, starting with hearing damage from loud practice
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as instruments
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in lesson plans
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

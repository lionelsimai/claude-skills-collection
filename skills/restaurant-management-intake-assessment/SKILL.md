# Intake & Initial Assessment — Restaurant Management

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold menus and costing sheets
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams, using the standard intake structure for restaurants and cafes
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to food cost percentage, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially food hygiene failures — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of menus and costing sheets

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: food hygiene failures.
- Never let this happen: allergen incidents.
- Never let this happen: cash handling discrepancies.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a restaurant manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to restaurants and cafes's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

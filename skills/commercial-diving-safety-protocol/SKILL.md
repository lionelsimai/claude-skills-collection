# Safety Protocol — Commercial Diving

Work safely in underwater work sites and support vessels: a practical safety protocol for a commercial diver and offshore, construction and salvage clients. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a commercial diver working with offshore, construction and salvage clients in underwater work sites and support vessels.

## When to use
- You are a commercial diver preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in underwater work sites and support vessels.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in underwater work sites and support vessels for staff, offshore, construction and salvage clients and visitors, starting with decompression sickness
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as diving equipment
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in dive plans
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

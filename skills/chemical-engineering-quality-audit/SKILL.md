# Quality Audit — Chemical Engineering

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to plant uptime and the standards for chemical plants and design offices
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of process flow diagrams and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including process safety incidents
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

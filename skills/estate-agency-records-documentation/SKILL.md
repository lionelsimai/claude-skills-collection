# Records & Documentation — Estate Agency

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with home sellers, buyers and landlords. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a estate agent working with home sellers, buyers and landlords in estate agency offices and properties.

## When to use
- You are a estate agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in estate agency offices and properties.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use market appraisals as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format estate agency offices and properties requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed instruction conversion exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in estate agency offices and properties, guarding against misrepresentation of properties
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

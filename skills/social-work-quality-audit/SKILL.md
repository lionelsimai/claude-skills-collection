# Quality Audit — Social Work

Audit the quality of social worker work in local authority offices, homes and community settings and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a social worker working with children, adults and families needing support in local authority offices, homes and community settings.

## When to use
- You are a social worker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in local authority offices, homes and community settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Safeguarding | protecting people from abuse and neglect |
| Care Act assessment | an assessment of an adult's care and support needs |
| Best interests | decisions made for a person who lacks capacity to decide |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to assessment timeliness and the standards for local authority offices, homes and community settings
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of assessments and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including safeguarding concerns missed
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in local authority offices, homes and community settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [children, adults and families needing support and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding concerns missed.
- Never let this happen: unsafe situations unvisited.
- Never let this happen: recording delays.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a social worker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to local authority offices, homes and community settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

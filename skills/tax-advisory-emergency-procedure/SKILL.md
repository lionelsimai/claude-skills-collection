# Emergency Response Procedure — Tax Advisory

Respond to emergencies in an accountancy or advisory firm calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a tax adviser working with individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs in an accountancy or advisory firm.

## When to use
- You are a tax adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy or advisory firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs, especially missed filing deadlines
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a tax adviser is trained and authorised for, using tax software as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in an accountancy or advisory firm, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep individuals and businesses planning their tax affairs and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in tax computations afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

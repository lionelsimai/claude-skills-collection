# Session Delivery Guide — Pulmonology

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for patients with lung and breathing conditions in a respiratory ward and lung function laboratory. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a pulmonologist working with patients with lung and breathing conditions in a respiratory ward and lung function laboratory.

## When to use
- You are a pulmonologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a respiratory ward and lung function laboratory.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Spirometry | a breathing test measuring how much and how fast air moves |
| Exacerbation | a sudden worsening of a chronic condition |
| Hypoxia | abnormally low oxygen levels in the body |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review spirometry reports before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as missed lung cancer signs
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and spirometers; check everything works and is safe before patients with lung and breathing conditions arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to spirometry quality rate
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a respiratory ward and lung function laboratory] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients with lung and breathing conditions and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in spirometry reports.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against spirometry quality rate.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a respiratory ward and lung function laboratory.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed lung cancer signs.
- Never let this happen: ventilator-associated harm.
- Never let this happen: untreated sleep apnoea.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a pulmonologist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a respiratory ward and lung function laboratory's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

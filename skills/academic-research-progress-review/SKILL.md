# Progress Review & Adjustment — Academic Research

Review progress for funders, collaborators and the wider research community against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a academic researcher working with funders, collaborators and the wider research community in a university or research institute.

## When to use
- You are a academic researcher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a university or research institute.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Peer review | evaluation of research by independent experts |
| Pre-registration | recording a study plan before data collection |
| Impact | the effect of research beyond academia |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together research proposals and the latest results for grant success rates since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with funders, collaborators and the wider research community honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in a university or research institute

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a university or research institute] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [funders, collaborators and the wider research community and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in research proposals.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against grant success rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a university or research institute.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: ethics breaches.
- Never let this happen: data loss.
- Never let this happen: unreproducible results.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a academic researcher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a university or research institute's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

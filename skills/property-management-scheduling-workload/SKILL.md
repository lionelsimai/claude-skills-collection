## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive rent collection rates
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in managed properties and management offices first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind tenancy agreements — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving safety certificates expired) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or property management systems availability

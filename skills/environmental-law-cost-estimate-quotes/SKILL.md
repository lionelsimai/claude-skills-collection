# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Environmental Law

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a environmental solicitor. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a environmental solicitor working with developers, businesses and community groups in a law firm and planning inquiries.

## When to use
- You are a environmental solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and planning inquiries.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Environmental permit | official permission for activities affecting the environment |
| EIA | environmental impact assessment of a proposed development |
| Contaminated land | land polluted by previous use |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and permit applications; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, planning portals usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against permit breaches unadvised
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as permit success rates
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Painting & Decorating

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising painter and decorator. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a painter and decorator working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, offices and building sites.

## When to use
- You are a painter and decorator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, offices and building sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for homes, offices and building sites and the evidence in your recent quotes
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on falls from height — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for job completion times
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: falls from height.
- Never let this happen: poor preparation causing early failure.
- Never let this happen: lead paint hazards.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a painter and decorator; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to homes, offices and building sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

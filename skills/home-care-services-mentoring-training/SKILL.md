# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Home Care Services

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe home care manager and carer practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a home care manager and carer working with people receiving care at home and their families in clients' homes.

## When to use
- You are a home care manager and carer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clients' homes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Call monitoring | verifying care visits happened as scheduled |
| Continuity | being cared for by familiar, regular carers |
| Double-up visit | a visit requiring two carers for safe support |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in clients' homes
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using scheduling systems under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching missed visits leaving people unsafe — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from care plans, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Home Care Services |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | care plans; visit schedules; daily notes; medication records |
| Core tools & equipment | scheduling systems; electronic call monitoring; medication apps; personal alarms |
| People involved | people receiving care at home and their families; the home care manager and carer; colleagues and other services in clients' homes |
| Primary record | care plans — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in clients' homes] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [people receiving care at home and their families and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in care plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against visit punctuality.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in clients' homes.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed visits leaving people unsafe.
- Never let this happen: medication errors.
- Never let this happen: lone working incidents.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a home care manager and carer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clients' homes's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

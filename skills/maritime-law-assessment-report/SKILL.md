# Assessment & Findings Report — Maritime Law

Assess ship owners, insurers and cargo interests and write a defensible findings report as a maritime solicitor. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a maritime solicitor working with ship owners, insurers and cargo interests in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts.

## When to use
- You are a maritime solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and charterparties so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using shipping databases, following the recognised method for a shipping law firm and admiralty courts
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind case resolution times and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for cargo deadlines missed; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

# Quality Audit — Funeral Directing

Audit the quality of funeral director work in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a funeral director working with bereaved families in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.

## When to use
- You are a funeral director preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to arrangement accuracy and the standards for funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of funeral arrangements and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including wrong details in services
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

# Intake & Initial Assessment — Commercial Leasing

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a commercial leasing agent working with landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices.

## When to use
- You are a commercial leasing agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial properties and agency offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Heads of terms | the agreed key points before a lease is drafted |
| Break clause | a right to end a lease early on set terms |
| Alienation | transferring or sharing a lease interest |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold heads of terms
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from landlords, tenants and investors, using the standard intake structure for commercial properties and agency offices
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to time to let, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially misdescribed premises — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for landlords, tenants and investors in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of heads of terms

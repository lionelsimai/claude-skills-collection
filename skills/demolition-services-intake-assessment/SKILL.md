# Intake & Initial Assessment — Demolition Services

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for developers, councils and property owners in demolition sites. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a demolition contractor working with developers, councils and property owners in demolition sites.

## When to use
- You are a demolition contractor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in demolition sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold demolition plans
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from developers, councils and property owners, using the standard intake structure for demolition sites
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to project completion times, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially asbestos exposure — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for developers, councils and property owners in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of demolition plans

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: asbestos exposure.
- Never let this happen: uncontrolled collapse.
- Never let this happen: dust and noise breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a demolition contractor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to demolition sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in travel agencies and online
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and booking records, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching booking errors costing clients — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

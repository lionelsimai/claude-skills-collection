# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Bookkeeping Practice

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising bookkeeper. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a bookkeeper working with small businesses, sole traders and accountants in a bookkeeping practice and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a bookkeeper preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a bookkeeping practice and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Reconciliation | matching records against bank statements to find differences |
| Ledger | the record of transactions by category |
| Accruals | recognising income or costs in the period they relate to |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for a bookkeeping practice and client premises and the evidence in your recent bank reconciliations
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on miscoded transactions — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for reconciliation timeliness
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Bookkeeping Practice |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | bank reconciliations; sales and purchase ledgers; VAT returns; payroll journals |
| Core tools & equipment | cloud accounting software; receipt capture apps; bank feeds; document storage |
| People involved | small businesses, sole traders and accountants; the bookkeeper; colleagues and other services in a bookkeeping practice and client premises |
| Primary record | bank reconciliations — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a bookkeeping practice and client premises] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [small businesses, sole traders and accountants and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in bank reconciliations.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against reconciliation timeliness.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a bookkeeping practice and client premises.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: miscoded transactions.
- Never let this happen: missing source documents.
- Never let this happen: VAT errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a bookkeeper; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a bookkeeping practice and client premises's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

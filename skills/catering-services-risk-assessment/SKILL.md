# Risk Assessment — Catering Services

Identify, score and control the risks in work for event clients and their guests in venues, events and production kitchens. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a caterer working with event clients and their guests in venues, events and production kitchens.

## When to use
- You are a caterer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in venues, events and production kitchens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| BEO | a banquet event order detailing an event's requirements |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for event clients and their guests and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to food temperature abuse in transit
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using transport equipment where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in event orders, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in venues, events and production kitchens change

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: food temperature abuse in transit.
- Never let this happen: running out of food.
- Never let this happen: staff shortages on the day.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a caterer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to venues, events and production kitchens's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

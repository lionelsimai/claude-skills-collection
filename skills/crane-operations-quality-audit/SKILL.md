# Quality Audit — Crane Operations

Audit the quality of crane operator work in construction and industrial sites and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a crane operator working with construction and industrial clients in construction and industrial sites.

## When to use
- You are a crane operator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction and industrial sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to lift completion rates and the standards for construction and industrial sites
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of lift plans and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including dropped loads
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

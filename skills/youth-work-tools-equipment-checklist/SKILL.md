# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Youth Work

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a youth worker relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a youth worker working with young people and their communities in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.

## When to use
- You are a youth worker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Detached work | youth work delivered on streets and in public spaces |
| Outcome star | a visual tool tracking a young person's progress |
| Participation | young people's active involvement in decisions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in youth centres, schools and outreach settings, centred on engagement tools, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against session plans
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to safeguarding disclosures missed
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding disclosures missed.
- Never let this happen: unsafe activities.
- Never let this happen: young people disengaging.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a youth worker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to youth centres, schools and outreach settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Quality Audit — Structural Engineering

Audit the quality of structural engineer work in design offices and construction sites and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a structural engineer working with architects, builders and building owners in design offices and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a structural engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to calculation check completion and the standards for design offices and construction sites
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of structural calculations and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including calculation errors
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

# Feedback & Complaints Handling — Personal Training

Collect feedback from fitness clients of all levels and handle complaints fairly and visibly. The deliverable is a feedback and complaints process with response standards and learning log.
This skill is written for a personal trainer working with fitness clients of all levels in gyms, studios, parks and online.

## When to use
- You are a personal trainer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in gyms, studios, parks and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a feedback and complaints process with response standards and learning log.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Invite feedback routinely — not only when things go wrong — and make it easy to give in gyms, studios, parks and online
2. **Step 2** - Acknowledge complaints quickly, thank the person, and explain the process and timeframe honestly
3. **Step 3** - Investigate from evidence in programmes; separate the facts from the feelings, and respect both
4. **Step 4** - Respond with findings, an apology where warranted, and concrete remedies or explanations
5. **Step 5** - Log themes and feed them into quality work — repeated complaints about injuries from unsafe programming are an early-warning system, not a nuisance

2. Detail, following the workflow above

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: injuries from unsafe programming.
- Never let this happen: health screening missed.
- Never let this happen: unsustainable diet advice.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a personal trainer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to gyms, studios, parks and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

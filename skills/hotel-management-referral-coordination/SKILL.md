# Referral & Coordination — Hotel Management

Refer guests, hotel teams and owners onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a hotel manager working with guests, hotel teams and owners in hotels and resorts.

## When to use
- You are a hotel manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hotels and resorts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what guests, hotel teams and owners need that sits outside the hotel manager scope in hotels and resorts
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from occupancy reports — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and occupancy rate stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with guests, hotel teams and owners: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of guest safety incidents

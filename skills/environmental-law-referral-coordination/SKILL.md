# Referral & Coordination — Environmental Law

Refer developers, businesses and community groups onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a environmental solicitor working with developers, businesses and community groups in a law firm and planning inquiries.

## When to use
- You are a environmental solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and planning inquiries.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what developers, businesses and community groups need that sits outside the environmental solicitor scope in a law firm and planning inquiries
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from permit applications — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and permit success rates stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with developers, businesses and community groups: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of permit breaches unadvised

- Never let this happen: permit breaches unadvised.
- Never let this happen: consultation deadlines missed.
- Never let this happen: contaminated land liabilities.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a environmental solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a law firm and planning inquiries's rules.

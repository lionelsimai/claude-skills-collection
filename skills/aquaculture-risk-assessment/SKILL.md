# Risk Assessment — Aquaculture

Identify, score and control the risks in work for seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## When to use
- You are a fish farm manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for seafood buyers and processors and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to disease outbreaks in stock
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using feeding systems where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in stock records, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms change

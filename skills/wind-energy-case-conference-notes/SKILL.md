# Case Conference & Meeting Notes — Wind Energy

Prepare for, contribute to and record case conferences and multi-professional meetings. The deliverable is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
This skill is written for a wind turbine technician working with energy companies and grid operators in wind farms onshore and offshore.

## When to use
- You are a wind turbine technician preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in wind farms onshore and offshore.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Prepare a one-page brief from maintenance records: current position, key evidence, the questions the meeting must answer
2. **Step 2** - Present your contribution concisely, leading with the position on turbine availability and any live concerns about falls from height
3. **Step 3** - Listen for information that changes the picture; update your understanding rather than defending a position
4. **Step 4** - Record decisions, actions, owners and deadlines during the meeting — not from memory afterwards
5. **Step 5** - Circulate the record promptly to attendees in wind farms onshore and offshore, correct errors fast, and file it with the main record

# Troubleshooting & Problem Solving — Mortgage Advisory

Diagnose and resolve common problems a mortgage adviser meets in advice firms, branches and online. The deliverable is a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
This skill is written for a mortgage adviser working with home buyers and homeowners in advice firms, branches and online.

## When to use
- You are a mortgage adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in advice firms, branches and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a troubleshooting guide: symptoms, likely causes, checks and fixes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Affordability | assessment of whether a borrower can sustain repayments |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Describe the problem precisely from the record in fact finds — when it started, what changed, who it affects
2. **Step 2** - Work from most likely and most dangerous causes first; rule out anything connected to unsuitable product recommendations early
3. **Step 3** - Run checks in a logical order using mortgage sourcing systems, changing one variable at a time so the cause can be isolated
4. **Step 4** - Apply the fix, then verify against application-to-offer times that the problem is actually resolved, not just quieter
5. **Step 5** - Document the diagnosis and fix so the next occurrence is faster, and flag recurring problems for root-cause work

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unsuitable product recommendations.
- Never let this happen: affordability miscalculated.
- Never let this happen: deadlines missed losing purchases.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a mortgage adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to advice firms, branches and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Incident Reporting & Response — Wind Energy

Respond to, record and learn from incidents involving energy companies and grid operators in wind farms onshore and offshore. The deliverable is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
This skill is written for a wind turbine technician working with energy companies and grid operators in wind farms onshore and offshore.

## When to use
- You are a wind turbine technician preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in wind farms onshore and offshore.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if falls from height is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in maintenance records
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in wind farms onshore and offshore
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with energy companies and grid operators about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

# Metrics & Outcomes Review — Housekeeping Management

Track, interpret and act on the outcome metrics that matter for a housekeeping manager. The deliverable is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
This skill is written for a housekeeping manager working with hotel guests and housekeeping teams in hotels and serviced apartments.

## When to use
- You are a housekeeping manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hotels and serviced apartments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Turndown | an evening service preparing a room for the night |
| Deep clean | a thorough clean beyond the daily standard |
| Par stock | the standard quantity of linen or supplies kept available |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define each metric precisely — start with inspection pass rates — including source data in room inspection checklists and collection method
2. **Step 2** - Set review cadence and comparison baselines so trends, not single data points, drive decisions
3. **Step 3** - Investigate movements before celebrating or panicking: check data quality, case mix and one-off causes
4. **Step 4** - Link metric movements to practice changes and to risks such as chemical injuries; test one change at a time where possible
5. **Step 5** - Report results honestly to stakeholders in hotels and serviced apartments, including where outcomes fell short and what changes

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: chemical injuries.
- Never let this happen: guest property losses.
- Never let this happen: missed safety hazards in rooms.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a housekeeping manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hotels and serviced apartments's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

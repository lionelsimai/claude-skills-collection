# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Banking Risk & Compliance

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a risk and compliance officer relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a risk and compliance officer working with banks, regulators and boards in bank risk and compliance departments.

## When to use
- You are a risk and compliance officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank risk and compliance departments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Risk register | the recorded list of risks, owners and controls |
| Three lines | the model separating risk ownership, oversight and audit |
| Conduct risk | the risk of harm to customers or markets from behaviour |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in bank risk and compliance departments, centred on risk systems, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against risk registers
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to undetected misconduct
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

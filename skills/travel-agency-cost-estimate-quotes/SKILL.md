# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Travel Agency Practice

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a travel consultant. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a travel consultant working with holidaymakers and business travellers in travel agencies and online.

## When to use
- You are a travel consultant preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in travel agencies and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and booking records; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, global distribution systems usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against booking errors costing clients
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as booking conversion
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

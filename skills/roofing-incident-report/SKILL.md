# Incident Reporting & Response — Roofing

Respond to, record and learn from incidents involving homeowners, builders and property managers in rooftops and construction sites. The deliverable is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
This skill is written for a roofer working with homeowners, builders and property managers in rooftops and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a roofer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in rooftops and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if falls from height is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in roof surveys
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in rooftops and construction sites
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with homeowners, builders and property managers about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

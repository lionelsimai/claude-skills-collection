# Safety Protocol — Landscape Architecture

Work safely in design studios and outdoor sites: a practical safety protocol for a landscape architect and developers, councils and private clients. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a landscape architect working with developers, councils and private clients in design studios and outdoor sites.

## When to use
- You are a landscape architect preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design studios and outdoor sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in design studios and outdoor sites for staff, developers, councils and private clients and visitors, starting with planting failures
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as CAD software
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in design concepts
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

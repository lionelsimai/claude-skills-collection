# Intake & Initial Assessment — Dermatology

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for patients with skin, hair and nail conditions in a dermatology outpatient clinic and minor surgery suite. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a dermatologist working with patients with skin, hair and nail conditions in a dermatology outpatient clinic and minor surgery suite.

## When to use
- You are a dermatologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a dermatology outpatient clinic and minor surgery suite.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold skin examination records
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from patients with skin, hair and nail conditions, using the standard intake structure for a dermatology outpatient clinic and minor surgery suite
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to urgent suspected-cancer referral timeliness, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially melanoma missed at triage — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for patients with skin, hair and nail conditions in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of skin examination records

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: melanoma missed at triage.
- Never let this happen: inappropriate long-term steroid use.
- Never let this happen: infection after minor surgery.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a dermatologist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a dermatology outpatient clinic and minor surgery suite's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Referral & Coordination — Commercial Diving

Refer offshore, construction and salvage clients onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a commercial diver working with offshore, construction and salvage clients in underwater work sites and support vessels.

## When to use
- You are a commercial diver preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in underwater work sites and support vessels.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what offshore, construction and salvage clients need that sits outside the commercial diver scope in underwater work sites and support vessels
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from dive plans — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and dive plan compliance stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with offshore, construction and salvage clients: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of decompression sickness

- Never let this happen: equipment failure underwater.
- Never let this happen: communication loss.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a commercial diver; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

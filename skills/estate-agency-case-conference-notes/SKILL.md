# Case Conference & Meeting Notes — Estate Agency

Prepare for, contribute to and record case conferences and multi-professional meetings. The deliverable is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
This skill is written for a estate agent working with home sellers, buyers and landlords in estate agency offices and properties.

## When to use
- You are a estate agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in estate agency offices and properties.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Instruction | being appointed to sell a property |
| Particulars | the marketing description of a property |
| Chain | a sequence of linked property sales dependent on each other |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Prepare a one-page brief from market appraisals: current position, key evidence, the questions the meeting must answer
2. **Step 2** - Present your contribution concisely, leading with the position on instruction conversion and any live concerns about misrepresentation of properties
3. **Step 3** - Listen for information that changes the picture; update your understanding rather than defending a position
4. **Step 4** - Record decisions, actions, owners and deadlines during the meeting — not from memory afterwards
5. **Step 5** - Circulate the record promptly to attendees in estate agency offices and properties, correct errors fast, and file it with the main record

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in estate agency offices and properties] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [home sellers, buyers and landlords and other stakeholders]

The finished output is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against instruction conversion.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in estate agency offices and properties.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misrepresentation of properties.
- Never let this happen: offers not passed on.
- Never let this happen: data protection breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a estate agent; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to estate agency offices and properties's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

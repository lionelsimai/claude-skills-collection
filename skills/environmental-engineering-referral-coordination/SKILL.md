# Referral & Coordination — Environmental Engineering

Refer water companies, industries and regulators onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a environmental engineer working with water companies, industries and regulators in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a environmental engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in treatment plants, client sites and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Consent | permission to discharge treated water or waste |
| Effluent | liquid waste discharged from a process |
| Remediation | cleaning up contaminated land or water |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what water companies, industries and regulators need that sits outside the environmental engineer scope in treatment plants, client sites and design offices
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from impact assessments — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and permit compliance stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with water companies, industries and regulators: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of pollution incidents

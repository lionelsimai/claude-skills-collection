# Assessment & Findings Report — Carpentry

Assess homeowners, builders and commercial clients and write a defensible findings report as a carpenter. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a carpenter working with homeowners, builders and commercial clients in workshops and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a carpenter preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in workshops and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and cutting lists so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using power saws, following the recognised method for workshops and construction sites
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind job completion times and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for machinery injuries; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: machinery injuries.
- Never let this happen: measurement errors wasting material.
- Never let this happen: structural work done wrong.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a carpenter; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to workshops and construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

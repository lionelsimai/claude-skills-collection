# Service Plan Design — Hotel Management

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for guests, hotel teams and owners as a hotel manager. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a hotel manager working with guests, hotel teams and owners in hotels and resorts.

## When to use
- You are a hotel manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hotels and resorts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| RevPAR | revenue per available room, a core hotel performance measure |
| Occupancy | the percentage of rooms sold |
| Yield management | adjusting prices to maximise revenue from capacity |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with guests, hotel teams and owners — specific enough to measure against occupancy rate
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for hotels and resorts, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around occupancy reports so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, property management systems and materials, with contingencies for guest safety incidents
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in hotels and resorts] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [guests, hotel teams and owners and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against occupancy rate.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: guest safety incidents.
- Never let this happen: overbooking crises.
- Never let this happen: data breaches on guest records.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a hotel manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hotels and resorts's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

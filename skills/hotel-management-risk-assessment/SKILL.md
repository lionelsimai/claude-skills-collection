# Risk Assessment — Hotel Management

Identify, score and control the risks in work for guests, hotel teams and owners in hotels and resorts. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a hotel manager working with guests, hotel teams and owners in hotels and resorts.

## When to use
- You are a hotel manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hotels and resorts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for guests, hotel teams and owners and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to guest safety incidents
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using property management systems where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in occupancy reports, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in hotels and resorts change

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in hotels and resorts] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [guests, hotel teams and owners and other stakeholders]

2. Detail, following the workflow above

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: guest safety incidents.
- Never let this happen: overbooking crises.
- Never let this happen: data breaches on guest records.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a hotel manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

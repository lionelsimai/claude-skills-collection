# Case Conference & Meeting Notes — Cabin Crew Services

Prepare for, contribute to and record case conferences and multi-professional meetings. The deliverable is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
This skill is written for a cabin crew member working with airline passengers and flight crew in aircraft cabins.

## When to use
- You are a cabin crew member preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in aircraft cabins.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Safety demonstration | the pre-flight briefing of safety features to passengers |

| SEP | safety and emergency procedures training |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Prepare a one-page brief from flight reports: current position, key evidence, the questions the meeting must answer
2. **Step 2** - Present your contribution concisely, leading with the position on safety check compliance and any live concerns about evacuation readiness gaps
3. **Step 3** - Listen for information that changes the picture; update your understanding rather than defending a position
4. **Step 4** - Record decisions, actions, owners and deadlines during the meeting — not from memory afterwards
5. **Step 5** - Circulate the record promptly to attendees in aircraft cabins, correct errors fast, and file it with the main record

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: evacuation readiness gaps.
- Never let this happen: unruly passenger escalation.
- Never let this happen: galley injuries.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a cabin crew member; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to aircraft cabins's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

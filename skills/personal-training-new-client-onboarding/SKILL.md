# New Client Onboarding Pack — Personal Training

Onboard fitness clients of all levels smoothly into a personal trainer's service in gyms, studios, parks and online. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a personal trainer working with fitness clients of all levels in gyms, studios, parks and online.

## When to use
- You are a personal trainer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in gyms, studios, parks and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| PAR-Q | a physical activity readiness questionnaire |
| Progressive overload | gradually increasing training demands |
| Periodisation | structuring training into planned phases |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from programmes
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on client goal achievement will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to gyms, studios, parks and online, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or injuries from unsafe programming

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in gyms, studios, parks and online] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [fitness clients of all levels and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in programmes.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against client goal achievement.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in gyms, studios, parks and online.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: injuries from unsafe programming.
- Never let this happen: health screening missed.
- Never let this happen: unsustainable diet advice.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a personal trainer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to gyms, studios, parks and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

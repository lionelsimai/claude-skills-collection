# Risk Assessment — Mine Operations

Identify, score and control the risks in work for mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a mine operations manager working with mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a mine operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mines and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Grade | the concentration of valuable mineral in ore |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for mining companies and mineral buyers and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to underground incidents
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using mining machinery where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in production reports, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in mines and processing plants change

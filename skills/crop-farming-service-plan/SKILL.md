# Service Plan Design — Crop Farming

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for food companies, markets and land owners as a crop farmer and agronomist. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a crop farmer and agronomist working with food companies, markets and land owners in farms and fields.

## When to use
- You are a crop farmer and agronomist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in farms and fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Rotation | changing crops grown on a field across seasons |
| Agronomy | the science of soil and crop production |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with food companies, markets and land owners — specific enough to measure against yield per hectare
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for farms and fields, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around crop plans so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, tractors and implements and materials, with contingencies for pesticide misuse
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in farms and fields] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [food companies, markets and land owners and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: pesticide misuse.
- Never let this happen: crop failure from weather.
- Never let this happen: soil degradation.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a crop farmer and agronomist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to farms and fields's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

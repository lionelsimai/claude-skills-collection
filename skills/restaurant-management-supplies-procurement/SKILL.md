# Supplies & Procurement — Restaurant Management

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in restaurants and cafes. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on menus and costing sheets and the kit centred on point of sale systems, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to food hygiene failures
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

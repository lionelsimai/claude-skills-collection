# Stakeholder Presentation — Forensic Accounting

Present forensic accountant findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a forensic accountant working with courts, insurers and businesses investigating financial wrongdoing in an accountancy firm and legal proceedings.

## When to use
- You are a forensic accountant preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in an accountancy firm and legal proceedings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Quantum | the amount of financial loss in a dispute |
| E-discovery | collecting and reviewing electronic evidence |
| Chain of custody | the documented handling of evidence |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from investigation reports and movement in investigation turnaround, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including evidence contamination and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in an accountancy firm and legal proceedings
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in an accountancy firm and legal proceedings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [courts, insurers and businesses investigating financial wrongdoing and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: evidence contamination.
- Never let this happen: assumptions challenged in court.
- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a forensic accountant; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to an accountancy firm and legal proceedings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

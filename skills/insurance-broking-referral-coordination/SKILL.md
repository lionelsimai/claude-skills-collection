# Referral & Coordination — Insurance Broking

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what businesses and individuals buying insurance need that sits outside the insurance broker scope in brokerages and client premises
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from needs assessments — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and renewal retention stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with businesses and individuals buying insurance: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of cover gaps at renewal

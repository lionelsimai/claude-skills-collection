# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Secondary Mathematics Education

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising secondary mathematics teacher. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a secondary mathematics teacher working with secondary school students and their parents in a secondary school mathematics department.

## When to use
- You are a secondary mathematics teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a secondary school mathematics department.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Scheme of work | the planned sequence of topics across a term or year |
| Intervention | targeted extra teaching for pupils who are behind |
| Working mathematically | applying maths to problem solving, not just procedures |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for a secondary school mathematics department and the evidence in your recent schemes of work
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on misconceptions unaddressed — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for examination results
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

|-----------|-------------------------------|

| Core tools & equipment | graphing software; maths platforms; exam board resources; data trackers |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a secondary school mathematics department] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [secondary school students and their parents and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in schemes of work.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against examination results.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a secondary school mathematics department.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: misconceptions unaddressed.
- Never let this happen: exam entry errors.
- Never let this happen: low-attaining pupils overlooked.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a secondary mathematics teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a secondary school mathematics department's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

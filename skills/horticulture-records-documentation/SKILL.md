# Records & Documentation — Horticulture

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with garden centres, landscapers and the public. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a horticulturist and grower working with garden centres, landscapers and the public in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.

## When to use
- You are a horticulturist and grower preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use planting plans as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format nurseries, glasshouses and gardens requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed crop success rates exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens, guarding against pest outbreaks
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: pest outbreaks.
- Never let this happen: plant disease spread.
- Never let this happen: irrigation failures.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a horticulturist and grower; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to nurseries, glasshouses and gardens's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

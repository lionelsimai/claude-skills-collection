# Assessment & Findings Report — Dental Hygiene

Assess patients needing preventive oral care and gum treatment and write a defensible findings report as a dental hygienist. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a dental hygienist working with patients needing preventive oral care and gum treatment in a dental practice.

## When to use
- You are a dental hygienist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a dental practice.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Plaque | a film of bacteria that forms on teeth |
| Periodontal | relating to the gums and supporting structures of teeth |
| Calculus | hardened plaque deposits on teeth |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and oral health assessments so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using ultrasonic scalers, following the recognised method for a dental practice
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind periodontal improvement scores and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for gum disease progression missed; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a dental practice] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [patients needing preventive oral care and gum treatment and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: gum disease progression missed.
- Never let this happen: infection control lapses.
- Never let this happen: patient anxiety unaddressed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a dental hygienist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a dental practice's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

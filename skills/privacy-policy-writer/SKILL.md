# Privacy Policy Writer

## Overview
Creates privacy policy templates covering data collection, usage, storage, and user rights. Covers GDPR, CCPA, and general best practices.

⚠️ Always note: "This is a template. Consult with a privacy attorney to ensure compliance with applicable laws."

## Sections Covered
1. Information we collect
2. How we use your information
3. How we share your information
4. Data retention
5. Your rights (GDPR/CCPA)
6. Cookies and tracking
7. Children's privacy
8. Security measures
9. Changes to this policy
10. Contact information

# Emergency Response Procedure — Broadcast Journalism

Respond to emergencies in newsrooms and outside broadcasts calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a broadcast journalist working with audiences, contributors and news editors in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.

## When to use
- You are a broadcast journalist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in newsrooms and outside broadcasts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for audiences, contributors and news editors, especially inaccurate reporting broadcast
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a broadcast journalist is trained and authorised for, using broadcast cameras as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in newsrooms and outside broadcasts, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep audiences, contributors and news editors and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in running orders afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

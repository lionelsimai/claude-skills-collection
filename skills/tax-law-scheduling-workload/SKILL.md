# Scheduling & Workload Management — Tax Law

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a tax solicitor in a law firm and tax tribunals. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a tax solicitor working with individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions in a law firm and tax tribunals.

## When to use
- You are a tax solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and tax tribunals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive opinion turnaround
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in a law firm and tax tribunals first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind tax opinions — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving filing deadlines missed) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or tax research databases availability

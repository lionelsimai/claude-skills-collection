# Quality Audit — Property Management

Audit the quality of property manager work in managed properties and management offices and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a property manager working with landlords, tenants and leaseholders in managed properties and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a property manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in managed properties and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to rent collection rates and the standards for managed properties and management offices
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of tenancy agreements and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including safety certificates expired
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

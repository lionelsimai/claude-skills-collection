# Formal Report Writing — Wind Energy

Write formal reports as a wind turbine technician that stand up to scrutiny from any reader. The deliverable is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
This skill is written for a wind turbine technician working with energy companies and grid operators in wind farms onshore and offshore.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Establish the report's purpose, audience and deadline before writing; gather source material from maintenance records
2. **Step 2** - Structure: summary, background, method, findings, analysis, recommendations — in that order
3. **Step 3** - Write findings factually and separately from opinion; label professional judgement as judgement
4. **Step 4** - Support key points with evidence and with turbine availability where outcomes are claimed; acknowledge limitations openly
5. **Step 5** - Proofread for accuracy and tone, check names, dates and figures twice, and consider falls from height before release

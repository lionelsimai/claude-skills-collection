# Service Plan Design — Crane Operations

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for construction and industrial clients as a crane operator. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a crane operator working with construction and industrial clients in construction and industrial sites.

## When to use
- You are a crane operator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction and industrial sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |

| Lift plan | the documented method for a lifting operation |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with construction and industrial clients — specific enough to measure against lift completion rates
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for construction and industrial sites, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around lift plans so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, tower and mobile cranes and materials, with contingencies for dropped loads
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in construction and industrial sites] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [construction and industrial clients and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

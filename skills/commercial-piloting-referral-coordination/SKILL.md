# Referral & Coordination — Commercial Piloting

Refer airlines, passengers and cargo customers onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a commercial pilot working with airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations.

## When to use
- You are a commercial pilot preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in cockpits and flight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what airlines, passengers and cargo customers need that sits outside the commercial pilot scope in cockpits and flight operations
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from flight plans — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and training check pass rates stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with airlines, passengers and cargo customers: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of fatigue risks

- Never let this happen: fatigue risks.
- Never let this happen: airspace infringements.
- Never let this happen: documentation errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a commercial pilot; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

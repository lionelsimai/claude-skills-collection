# Service Plan Design — Cabin Crew Services

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for airline passengers and flight crew as a cabin crew member. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a cabin crew member working with airline passengers and flight crew in aircraft cabins.

## When to use
- You are a cabin crew member preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in aircraft cabins.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with airline passengers and flight crew — specific enough to measure against safety check compliance
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for aircraft cabins, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around flight reports so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, safety equipment and materials, with contingencies for evacuation readiness gaps
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

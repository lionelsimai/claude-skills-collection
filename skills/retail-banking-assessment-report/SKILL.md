# Assessment & Findings Report — Retail Banking

Assess personal banking customers and write a defensible findings report as a retail banking adviser. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a retail banking adviser working with personal banking customers in bank branches and contact centres.

## When to use
- You are a retail banking adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank branches and contact centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and customer records so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using core banking systems, following the recognised method for bank branches and contact centres
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind customer satisfaction and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for mis-selling risks; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: mis-selling risks.
- Never let this happen: fraud missed.
- Never let this happen: vulnerable customers unrecognised.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a retail banking adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank branches and contact centres's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Listed building | a building protected by law for its heritage value |

| Reversibility | repairs that can be undone without harming original material |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what heritage bodies, owners of historic buildings and councils actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind condition surveys — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as inappropriate repairs damaging fabric
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking heritage bodies, owners of historic buildings and councils to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

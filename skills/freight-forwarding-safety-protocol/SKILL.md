# Safety Protocol — Freight Forwarding

Work safely in freight offices, ports and airports: a practical safety protocol for a freight forwarder and importers, exporters and carriers. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a freight forwarder working with importers, exporters and carriers in freight offices, ports and airports.

## When to use
- You are a freight forwarder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in freight offices, ports and airports.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in freight offices, ports and airports for staff, importers, exporters and carriers and visitors, starting with customs delays from bad documents
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as freight management systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in shipping documents
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

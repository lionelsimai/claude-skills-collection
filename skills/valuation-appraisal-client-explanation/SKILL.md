# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Valuation & Appraisal

Explain complex chartered valuation surveyor matters to lenders, buyers, sellers and investors in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a chartered valuation surveyor working with lenders, buyers, sellers and investors in properties and surveying offices.

## When to use
- You are a chartered valuation surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in properties and surveying offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Comparable | evidence from similar properties used to judge value |
| Red Book | the professional valuation standards of RICS |
| Yield | rental income as a percentage of a property's value |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what lenders, buyers, sellers and investors actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind valuation reports — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as overvaluation losses
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking lenders, buyers, sellers and investors to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

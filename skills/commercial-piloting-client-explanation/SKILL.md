# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Commercial Piloting

Explain complex commercial pilot matters to airlines, passengers and cargo customers in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a commercial pilot working with airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations.

## When to use
- You are a commercial pilot preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in cockpits and flight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Flight plan | the planned route and details filed before a flight |
| Type rating | qualification to fly a specific aircraft type |
| Sterile cockpit | periods when only flight-critical conversation is allowed |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what airlines, passengers and cargo customers actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind flight plans — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as fatigue risks
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking airlines, passengers and cargo customers to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

- Never let this happen: fatigue risks.
- Never let this happen: airspace infringements.
- Never let this happen: documentation errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a commercial pilot; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Strata Management

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising strata manager. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a strata manager working with lot owners, owners corporations and residents in residential complexes and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a strata manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in residential complexes and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Levy | owners' regular contribution to shared costs |
| By-laws | the rules governing behaviour and use in a strata scheme |
| Quorum | the minimum attendance for a meeting to make decisions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for residential complexes and management offices and the evidence in your recent meeting minutes
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on fund mismanagement — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for levy collection rates
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: fund mismanagement.
- Never let this happen: meeting procedure errors.
- Never let this happen: by-law disputes escalated.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a strata manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to residential complexes and management offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

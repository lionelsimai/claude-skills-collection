# Quality Audit — Aquaculture

Audit the quality of fish farm manager work in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## When to use
- You are a fish farm manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to growth rates and the standards for lakes, coastal sites and fish farms
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of stock records and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including disease outbreaks in stock
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

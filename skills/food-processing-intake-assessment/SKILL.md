# Intake & Initial Assessment — Food Processing

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for retailers, wholesalers and consumers in food factories and processing plants. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a food production manager working with retailers, wholesalers and consumers in food factories and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a food production manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in food factories and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold production plans
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from retailers, wholesalers and consumers, using the standard intake structure for food factories and processing plants
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to production yield, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially contamination incidents — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for retailers, wholesalers and consumers in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of production plans

# Service Plan Design — Pastry & Baking

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for cafe and restaurant customers, retailers as a pastry chef and baker. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a pastry chef and baker working with cafe and restaurant customers, retailers in bakeries and patisserie kitchens.

## When to use
- You are a pastry chef and baker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bakeries and patisserie kitchens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with cafe and restaurant customers, retailers — specific enough to measure against production waste rates
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for bakeries and patisserie kitchens, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around recipes and production sheets so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, deck and convection ovens and materials, with contingencies for allergen cross-contact
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: allergen cross-contact.
- Never let this happen: burns from ovens.
- Never let this happen: stale stock losses.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a pastry chef and baker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bakeries and patisserie kitchens's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

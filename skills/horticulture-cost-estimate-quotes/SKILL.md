# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Horticulture

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a horticulturist and grower. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a horticulturist and grower working with garden centres, landscapers and the public in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.

## When to use
- You are a horticulturist and grower preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and planting plans; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, glasshouse systems usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against pest outbreaks
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as crop success rates
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

# Quality Audit — Conveyancing

Audit the quality of conveyancer work in a conveyancing firm and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a conveyancer working with home buyers, sellers and lenders in a conveyancing firm.

## When to use
- You are a conveyancer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a conveyancing firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to exchange-to-completion times and the standards for a conveyancing firm
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of contracts and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including missed exchange deadlines
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed exchange deadlines.
- Never let this happen: title defects overlooked.
- Never let this happen: fraud attempts undetected.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a conveyancer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a conveyancing firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

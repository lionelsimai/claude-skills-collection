## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what insurers, pension schemes and regulators need that sits outside the actuary scope in insurance and pensions offices
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from valuation reports — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and model validation results stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with insurers, pension schemes and regulators: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of model errors affecting reserves

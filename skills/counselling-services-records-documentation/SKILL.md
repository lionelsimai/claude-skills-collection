# Records & Documentation — Counselling Services

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with individuals seeking psychological support. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a counsellor and psychotherapist working with individuals seeking psychological support in clinics, schools, workplaces and online.

## When to use
- You are a counsellor and psychotherapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, schools, workplaces and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Contracting | agreeing the terms of the counselling relationship |
| Supervision | regular professional review of a counsellor's work |
| Boundaries | the limits keeping the therapeutic relationship safe |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use client notes as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format clinics, schools, workplaces and online requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed outcome improvement scores exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in clinics, schools, workplaces and online, guarding against risk to self or others unrecognised
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: risk to self or others unrecognised.
- Never let this happen: boundary breaches.
- Never let this happen: notes not secured.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a counsellor and psychotherapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clinics, schools, workplaces and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

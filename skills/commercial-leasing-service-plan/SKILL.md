# Service Plan Design — Commercial Leasing

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for landlords, tenants and investors as a commercial leasing agent. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a commercial leasing agent working with landlords, tenants and investors in commercial properties and agency offices.

## When to use
- You are a commercial leasing agent preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial properties and agency offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Heads of terms | the agreed key points before a lease is drafted |
| Break clause | a right to end a lease early on set terms |
| Alienation | transferring or sharing a lease interest |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with landlords, tenants and investors — specific enough to measure against time to let
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for commercial properties and agency offices, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around heads of terms so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, property portals and materials, with contingencies for misdescribed premises
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

- Never let this happen: missed break clauses.
- Never let this happen: unagreed incentives.

# Risk Assessment — Horticulture

Identify, score and control the risks in work for garden centres, landscapers and the public in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a horticulturist and grower working with garden centres, landscapers and the public in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.

## When to use
- You are a horticulturist and grower preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Propagation | producing new plants from seeds, cuttings or division |
| Hardening off | acclimatising indoor-grown plants to outdoor conditions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for garden centres, landscapers and the public and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to pest outbreaks
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using glasshouse systems where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in planting plans, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in nurseries, glasshouses and gardens change

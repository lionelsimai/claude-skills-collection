# Intake & Initial Assessment — Hotel Management

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for guests, hotel teams and owners in hotels and resorts. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a hotel manager working with guests, hotel teams and owners in hotels and resorts.

## When to use
- You are a hotel manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hotels and resorts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold occupancy reports
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from guests, hotel teams and owners, using the standard intake structure for hotels and resorts
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to occupancy rate, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially guest safety incidents — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for guests, hotel teams and owners in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of occupancy reports

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: guest safety incidents.
- Never let this happen: overbooking crises.
- Never let this happen: data breaches on guest records.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a hotel manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hotels and resorts's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

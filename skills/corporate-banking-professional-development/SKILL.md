# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Corporate Banking

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising corporate relationship manager. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a corporate relationship manager working with companies and institutions in bank offices and client sites.

## When to use
- You are a corporate relationship manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank offices and client sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Facility | an agreed lending arrangement such as a loan or overdraft |
| Covenant | a condition a borrower must maintain |
| Credit paper | the document presenting a lending case for approval |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for bank offices and client sites and the evidence in your recent credit papers
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on credit losses from weak analysis — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for portfolio quality
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

| Component | Details for Corporate Banking |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | credit papers; facility documents; client reviews; covenant reports |
| Core tools & equipment | lending systems; credit analysis tools; CRM platforms; market data services |
| People involved | companies and institutions; the corporate relationship manager; colleagues and other services in bank offices and client sites |
| Primary record | credit papers — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in bank offices and client sites] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [companies and institutions and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in credit papers.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against portfolio quality.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in bank offices and client sites.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: credit losses from weak analysis.
- Never let this happen: covenant breaches spotted late.
- Never let this happen: concentration risks.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a corporate relationship manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank offices and client sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

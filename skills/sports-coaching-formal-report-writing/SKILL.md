# Formal Report Writing — Sports Coaching

Write formal reports as a sports coach that stand up to scrutiny from any reader. The deliverable is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
This skill is written for a sports coach working with athletes and teams from grassroots to elite in clubs, schools and performance centres.

## When to use
- You are a sports coach preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clubs, schools and performance centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Safeguarding | protecting children and vulnerable adults in sport |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Establish the report's purpose, audience and deadline before writing; gather source material from session plans
2. **Step 2** - Structure: summary, background, method, findings, analysis, recommendations — in that order
3. **Step 3** - Write findings factually and separately from opinion; label professional judgement as judgement
4. **Step 4** - Support key points with evidence and with athlete performance progress where outcomes are claimed; acknowledge limitations openly
5. **Step 5** - Proofread for accuracy and tone, check names, dates and figures twice, and consider safeguarding in sport missed before release

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in clubs, schools and performance centres] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [athletes and teams from grassroots to elite and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against athlete performance progress.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in clubs, schools and performance centres.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding in sport missed.
- Never let this happen: overtraining.
- Never let this happen: unsafe practice conditions.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a sports coach; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clubs, schools and performance centres's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

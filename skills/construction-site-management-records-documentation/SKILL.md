# Records & Documentation — Construction Site Management

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with clients, contractors and construction teams. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a site manager working with clients, contractors and construction teams in construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a site manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use site diaries as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format construction sites requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed programme adherence exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in construction sites, guarding against site accidents
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: site accidents.
- Never let this happen: programme slippage hidden.
- Never let this happen: quality defects at handover.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a site manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

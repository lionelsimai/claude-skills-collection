# Ethics & Professional Standards — Funeral Directing

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a funeral director. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a funeral director working with bereaved families in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.

## When to use
- You are a funeral director preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Arrangement | the meeting where a funeral is planned |
| Embalming | preserving and preparing a deceased person |
| Cortege | the procession of vehicles at a funeral |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect bereaved families first where duties conflict, and be alert to how wrong details in services can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from funeral arrangements, and support colleagues who do the same

# Safety Protocol — Educational Leadership

Work safely in a school or multi-school trust: a practical safety protocol for a headteacher and school leader and schools, staff, pupils and governing bodies. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a headteacher and school leader working with schools, staff, pupils and governing bodies in a school or multi-school trust.

## When to use
- You are a headteacher and school leader preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a school or multi-school trust.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in a school or multi-school trust for staff, schools, staff, pupils and governing bodies and visitors, starting with safeguarding failures
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as management information systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in school improvement plans
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a school or multi-school trust] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [schools, staff, pupils and governing bodies and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in school improvement plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against inspection outcomes.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a school or multi-school trust.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding failures.
- Never let this happen: budget deficits.
- Never let this happen: staff burnout.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a headteacher and school leader; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a school or multi-school trust's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

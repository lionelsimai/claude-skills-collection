# Referral & Coordination — Childminding

Refer young children and their parents onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a registered childminder working with young children and their parents in the childminder's home.

## When to use
- You are a registered childminder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in the childminder's home.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what young children and their parents need that sits outside the registered childminder scope in the childminder's home
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from learning records — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and occupancy rates stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with young children and their parents: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of safeguarding concerns missed

|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | learning records; attendance registers; accident records; contracts and policies |
| Core tools & equipment | toys and learning resources; safety equipment; parent communication apps; observation tools |
| People involved | young children and their parents; the registered childminder; colleagues and other services in the childminder's home |
| Primary record | learning records — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in the childminder's home] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [young children and their parents and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in learning records.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against occupancy rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in the childminder's home.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding concerns missed.
- Never let this happen: accidents from unsafe environments.
- Never let this happen: ratio breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a registered childminder; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to the childminder's home's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# New Client Onboarding Pack — Commercial Piloting

Onboard airlines, passengers and cargo customers smoothly into a commercial pilot's service in cockpits and flight operations. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a commercial pilot working with airlines, passengers and cargo customers in cockpits and flight operations.

## When to use
- You are a commercial pilot preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in cockpits and flight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from flight plans
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on training check pass rates will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to cockpits and flight operations, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or fatigue risks

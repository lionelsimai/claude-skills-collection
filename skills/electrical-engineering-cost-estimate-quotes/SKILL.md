# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Electrical Engineering

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a electrical engineer. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a electrical engineer working with energy, manufacturing and building clients in design offices, plants and substations.

## When to use
- You are a electrical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, plants and substations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Load | the power drawn by a system or device |
| Protection relay | a device that detects faults and isolates circuits |
| Earthing | connecting equipment to earth for safety |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and circuit designs; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, design software usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against electric shock risks
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as test pass rates
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Marine Engineering

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe marine engineer practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a marine engineer working with ship owners, operators and crews in ships' engine rooms and shipyards.

## When to use
- You are a marine engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in ships' engine rooms and shipyards.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in ships' engine rooms and shipyards
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using engine monitoring systems under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching machinery failures at sea — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from maintenance logs, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: machinery failures at sea.
- Never let this happen: oil pollution incidents.
- Never let this happen: burns and injuries.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a marine engineer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to ships' engine rooms and shipyards's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

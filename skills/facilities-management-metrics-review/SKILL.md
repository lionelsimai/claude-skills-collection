# Metrics & Outcomes Review — Facilities Management

Track, interpret and act on the outcome metrics that matter for a facilities manager. The deliverable is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
This skill is written for a facilities manager working with building occupants, owners and service teams in commercial buildings and campuses.

## When to use
- You are a facilities manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial buildings and campuses.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| CAFM | computer-aided facilities management software |
| PPM | planned preventive maintenance scheduled in advance |
| Asset register | the inventory of building equipment and its condition |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define each metric precisely — start with work order completion — including source data in maintenance schedules and collection method
2. **Step 2** - Set review cadence and comparison baselines so trends, not single data points, drive decisions
3. **Step 3** - Investigate movements before celebrating or panicking: check data quality, case mix and one-off causes
4. **Step 4** - Link metric movements to practice changes and to risks such as life-safety systems unmaintained; test one change at a time where possible
5. **Step 5** - Report results honestly to stakeholders in commercial buildings and campuses, including where outcomes fell short and what changes

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in commercial buildings and campuses] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [building occupants, owners and service teams and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against work order completion.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in commercial buildings and campuses.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: life-safety systems unmaintained.
- Never let this happen: contractor failures.
- Never let this happen: energy waste.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a facilities manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to commercial buildings and campuses's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

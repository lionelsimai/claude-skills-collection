# New Client Onboarding Pack — Family Law

Onboard clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances smoothly into a family law solicitor's service in a law firm and family court. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Disclosure | the formal exchange of financial information in family cases |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from client care letters
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on case progression times will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to a law firm and family court, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or missed court deadlines

- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest.

# New Client Onboarding Pack — Aerospace Engineering

Onboard aircraft manufacturers, airlines and space agencies smoothly into a aerospace engineer's service in design offices, test facilities and hangars. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a aerospace engineer working with aircraft manufacturers, airlines and space agencies in design offices, test facilities and hangars.

## When to use
- You are a aerospace engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, test facilities and hangars.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Certification | official approval that a design meets safety standards |
| Fatigue life | how long a component withstands repeated loading |
| Thrust | the force produced by an engine |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from design analyses
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on test programme milestones will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to design offices, test facilities and hangars, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or test incidents

- Never let this happen: test incidents.
- Never let this happen: certification delays.
- Never let this happen: fatigue failures.

# New Client Onboarding Pack — Sports Coaching

Onboard athletes and teams from grassroots to elite smoothly into a sports coach's service in clubs, schools and performance centres. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a sports coach working with athletes and teams from grassroots to elite in clubs, schools and performance centres.

## When to use
- You are a sports coach preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clubs, schools and performance centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Safeguarding | protecting children and vulnerable adults in sport |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from session plans
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on athlete performance progress will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to clubs, schools and performance centres, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or safeguarding in sport missed

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in clubs, schools and performance centres] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [athletes and teams from grassroots to elite and other stakeholders]

The finished output is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in session plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against athlete performance progress.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in clubs, schools and performance centres.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding in sport missed.
- Never let this happen: overtraining.
- Never let this happen: unsafe practice conditions.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a sports coach; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to clubs, schools and performance centres's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

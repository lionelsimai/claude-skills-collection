# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Customs Brokerage

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a customs broker. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a customs broker working with importers and exporters in customs offices and freight operations.

## When to use
- You are a customs broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in customs offices and freight operations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tariff classification | assigning the correct commodity code to goods |
| Duty | tax charged on imported goods |
| Origin | the country goods are deemed to come from for trade rules |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and customs declarations; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, customs software usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against misclassification penalties
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as declaration accuracy
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

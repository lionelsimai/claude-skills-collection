# Formal Report Writing — Civil Engineering

Write formal reports as a civil engineer that stand up to scrutiny from any reader. The deliverable is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
This skill is written for a civil engineer working with infrastructure clients, contractors and the public in engineering consultancies and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a civil engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in engineering consultancies and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| BIM | building information modelling, a shared digital model of an asset |
| Load path | the route forces take through a structure to the ground |
| Specification | the detailed technical requirements for works or materials |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Establish the report's purpose, audience and deadline before writing; gather source material from design calculations
2. **Step 2** - Structure: summary, background, method, findings, analysis, recommendations — in that order
3. **Step 3** - Write findings factually and separately from opinion; label professional judgement as judgement
4. **Step 4** - Support key points with evidence and with design review completion where outcomes are claimed; acknowledge limitations openly
5. **Step 5** - Proofread for accuracy and tone, check names, dates and figures twice, and consider structural failures before release

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: structural failures.
- Never let this happen: design errors discovered late.
- Never let this happen: site safety incidents.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a civil engineer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to engineering consultancies and construction sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Formal Report Writing — Youth Work

Write formal reports as a youth worker that stand up to scrutiny from any reader. The deliverable is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
This skill is written for a youth worker working with young people and their communities in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.

## When to use
- You are a youth worker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Detached work | youth work delivered on streets and in public spaces |
| Outcome star | a visual tool tracking a young person's progress |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Establish the report's purpose, audience and deadline before writing; gather source material from session plans
2. **Step 2** - Structure: summary, background, method, findings, analysis, recommendations — in that order
3. **Step 3** - Write findings factually and separately from opinion; label professional judgement as judgement
4. **Step 4** - Support key points with evidence and with attendance and engagement where outcomes are claimed; acknowledge limitations openly
5. **Step 5** - Proofread for accuracy and tone, check names, dates and figures twice, and consider safeguarding disclosures missed before release

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in youth centres, schools and outreach settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [young people and their communities and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in session plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against attendance and engagement.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding disclosures missed.
- Never let this happen: unsafe activities.
- Never let this happen: young people disengaging.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a youth worker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to youth centres, schools and outreach settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

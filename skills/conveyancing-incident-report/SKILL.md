# Incident Reporting & Response — Conveyancing

Respond to, record and learn from incidents involving home buyers, sellers and lenders in a conveyancing firm. The deliverable is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
This skill is written for a conveyancer working with home buyers, sellers and lenders in a conveyancing firm.

## When to use
- You are a conveyancer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a conveyancing firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Exchange | when a property sale becomes legally binding |
| Title | the legal evidence of property ownership |
| Stamp duty | a tax payable on property purchases in many jurisdictions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if missed exchange deadlines is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in contracts
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in a conveyancing firm
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with home buyers, sellers and lenders about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a conveyancing firm] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [home buyers, sellers and lenders and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in contracts.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against exchange-to-completion times.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in a conveyancing firm.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed exchange deadlines.
- Never let this happen: title defects overlooked.
- Never let this happen: fraud attempts undetected.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a conveyancer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a conveyancing firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Quality Audit — Architectural Technology

Audit the quality of architectural technologist work in design studios and construction sites and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a architectural technologist working with architects, developers and homeowners in design studios and construction sites.

## When to use
- You are a architectural technologist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design studios and construction sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to drawing issue timeliness and the standards for design studios and construction sites
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of technical drawings and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including regulation non-compliance
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

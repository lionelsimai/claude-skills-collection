# Records & Documentation — Housekeeping Management

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with hotel guests and housekeeping teams. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a housekeeping manager working with hotel guests and housekeeping teams in hotels and serviced apartments.

## When to use
- You are a housekeeping manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in hotels and serviced apartments.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use room inspection checklists as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format hotels and serviced apartments requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed inspection pass rates exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in hotels and serviced apartments, guarding against chemical injuries
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: chemical injuries.
- Never let this happen: guest property losses.
- Never let this happen: missed safety hazards in rooms.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a housekeeping manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to hotels and serviced apartments's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

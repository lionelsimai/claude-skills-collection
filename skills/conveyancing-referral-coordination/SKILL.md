# Referral & Coordination — Conveyancing

Refer home buyers, sellers and lenders onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a conveyancer working with home buyers, sellers and lenders in a conveyancing firm.

## When to use
- You are a conveyancer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a conveyancing firm.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Exchange | when a property sale becomes legally binding |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what home buyers, sellers and lenders need that sits outside the conveyancer scope in a conveyancing firm
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from contracts — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and exchange-to-completion times stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with home buyers, sellers and lenders: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of missed exchange deadlines

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed exchange deadlines.
- Never let this happen: title defects overlooked.
- Never let this happen: fraud attempts undetected.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a conveyancer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a conveyancing firm's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Ethics & Professional Standards — Investment Analysis

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a investment analyst. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a investment analyst working with fund managers, investors and research subscribers in investment firms.

## When to use
- You are a investment analyst preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in investment firms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in investment firms: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect fund managers, investors and research subscribers first where duties conflict, and be alert to how conflicts of interest undisclosed can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from research reports, and support colleagues who do the same

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest undisclosed.
- Never let this happen: model errors.
- Never let this happen: insider information mishandled.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a investment analyst; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to investment firms's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

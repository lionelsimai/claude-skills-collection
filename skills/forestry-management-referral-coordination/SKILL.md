# Referral & Coordination — Forestry Management

This skill is written for a forest manager working with timber buyers, landowners and conservation bodies in forests and woodland estates.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what timber buyers, landowners and conservation bodies need that sits outside the forest manager scope in forests and woodland estates
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from management plans — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and timber yield stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with timber buyers, landowners and conservation bodies: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of illegal felling

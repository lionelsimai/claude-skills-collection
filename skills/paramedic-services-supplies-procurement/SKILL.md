# Supplies & Procurement — Paramedic Services

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in ambulances and community settings. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a paramedic working with patients with emergency and urgent care needs in the community in ambulances and community settings.

## When to use
- You are a paramedic preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in ambulances and community settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on patient report forms and the kit centred on ambulance equipment, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to scene safety hazards
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

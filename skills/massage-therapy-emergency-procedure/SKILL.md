# Emergency Response Procedure — Massage Therapy

## When to use
- You are a massage therapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, spas and sports settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a massage therapist is trained and authorised for, using massage tables as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in clinics, spas and sports settings, situation, what has been done, what is needed

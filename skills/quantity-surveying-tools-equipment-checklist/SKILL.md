# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Quantity Surveying

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a quantity surveyor relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a quantity surveyor working with developers, contractors and public clients in construction projects and offices.

## When to use
- You are a quantity surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction projects and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Bill of quantities | an itemised list of materials and work for pricing |
| Valuation | an assessment of work completed for interim payment |
| Final account | the agreed total cost at a project's end |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in construction projects and offices, centred on measurement software, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against bills of quantities
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to cost overruns unreported
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

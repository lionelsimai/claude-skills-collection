# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Ship Navigation

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for ship owners, crews and port authorities are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a ship's navigation officer working with ship owners, crews and port authorities in ships' bridges.

## When to use
- You are a ship's navigation officer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in ships' bridges.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| ECDIS | the electronic chart display and information system |
| Passage plan | the complete plan for a voyage from berth to berth |
| COLREGs | the international rules for preventing collisions at sea |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how voyage plan compliance is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using ECDIS chart systems where measurement is needed, and record it in voyage plans
3. **Step 3** - Give ship owners, crews and port authorities clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to groundings and collisions shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

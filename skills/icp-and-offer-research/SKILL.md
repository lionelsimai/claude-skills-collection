# ICP and Offer Research

## Learn the offer first

- Read the business website, presentation deck and pricing page before drafting anything.

- Write the offer in one sentence: who it helps, what outcome it produces, and what proof exists.

- List proof points into an allow list. Use only claims on that list in any later outreach, and never invent clients, numbers or results.

- Study three to five competitors. Note what they promise, who they sell to, and where the gap is that this business fills.

## Build the ICP segments

- Split buyers into two to four segments. Give each segment a name, the roles that buy, the company type, the region, and the size range.

- Score fit per segment from the evidence: role match, company match, region match, size match, and a live buying signal such as a launch, a hire or an expansion.

- Rank the segments and work the highest fit segment first. Say why it ranks first, in one line a human can audit.

- Attach one offer per segment. Keep the entry offer small and the full offer clear, and never mix two offers in one campaign.

## Present the result

- Return the one-sentence offer, the allow list, and the ranked segments with their fit reasons.

- Flag any claim you could not verify instead of smoothing it over.

## Guardrails and quality checks

- Never fabricate facts, numbers, clients or results. Use only sources the user provides or verifies.
- Keep drafts as drafts until a human approves any external send, payment or commitment.
- Check the final output against the workflow above before delivering it, and state anything you could not verify.

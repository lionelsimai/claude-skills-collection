# Referral & Coordination — Oil & Gas Operations

Refer energy companies and regulators onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a petroleum operations engineer working with energy companies and regulators in offshore platforms and onshore terminals.

## When to use
- You are a petroleum operations engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in offshore platforms and onshore terminals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what energy companies and regulators need that sits outside the petroleum operations engineer scope in offshore platforms and onshore terminals
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from production reports — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and production rates stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with energy companies and regulators: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of hydrocarbon releases

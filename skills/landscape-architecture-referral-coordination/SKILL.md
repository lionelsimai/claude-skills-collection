# Referral & Coordination — Landscape Architecture

Refer developers, councils and private clients onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a landscape architect working with developers, councils and private clients in design studios and outdoor sites.

## When to use
- You are a landscape architect preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design studios and outdoor sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what developers, councils and private clients need that sits outside the landscape architect scope in design studios and outdoor sites
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from design concepts — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and design approval rates stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with developers, councils and private clients: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of planting failures

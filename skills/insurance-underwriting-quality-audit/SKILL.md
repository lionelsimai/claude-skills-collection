# Quality Audit — Insurance Underwriting

Audit the quality of insurance underwriter work in insurance offices and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a insurance underwriter working with brokers, policyholders and insurers in insurance offices.

## When to use
- You are a insurance underwriter preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to quote turnaround and the standards for insurance offices
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of risk assessments and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including underpriced risks
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

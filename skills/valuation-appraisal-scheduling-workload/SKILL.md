# Scheduling & Workload Management — Valuation & Appraisal

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a chartered valuation surveyor in properties and surveying offices. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a chartered valuation surveyor working with lenders, buyers, sellers and investors in properties and surveying offices.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive report turnaround
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in properties and surveying offices first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind valuation reports — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving overvaluation losses) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or valuation software availability

# Service Plan Design — Strata Management

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for lot owners, owners corporations and residents as a strata manager. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a strata manager working with lot owners, owners corporations and residents in residential complexes and management offices.

## When to use
- You are a strata manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in residential complexes and management offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Levy | owners' regular contribution to shared costs |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with lot owners, owners corporations and residents — specific enough to measure against levy collection rates
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for residential complexes and management offices, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around meeting minutes so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, strata software and materials, with contingencies for fund mismanagement
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

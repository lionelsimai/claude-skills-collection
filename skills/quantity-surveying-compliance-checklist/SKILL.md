# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Quantity Surveying

Check work in construction projects and offices against the laws, standards and codes that govern a quantity surveyor. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a quantity surveyor working with developers, contractors and public clients in construction projects and offices.

## When to use
- You are a quantity surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction projects and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Bill of quantities | an itemised list of materials and work for pricing |
| Valuation | an assessment of work completed for interim payment |
| Final account | the agreed total cost at a project's end |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in construction projects and offices
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and bills of quantities, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching cost overruns unreported — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

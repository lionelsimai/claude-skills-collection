# Mentoring & Training Junior Staff — Demolition Services

Train and mentor junior colleagues toward competent, safe demolition contractor practice. The deliverable is a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
This skill is written for a demolition contractor working with developers, councils and property owners in demolition sites.

## When to use
- You are a demolition contractor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in demolition sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a training plan with competencies, supervised milestones and sign-off criteria.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Soft strip | removing non-structural elements before demolition |
| Asbestos survey | inspection identifying asbestos before work starts |
| Arisings | materials produced by demolition for reuse or disposal |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Assess the trainee's current level honestly against the competencies required in demolition sites
2. **Step 2** - Sequence learning from observation to supervised practice to independent work, using excavators with attachments under supervision first
3. **Step 3** - Set explicit sign-off criteria for high-risk tasks — especially anything touching asbestos exposure — and never sign off early
4. **Step 4** - Give feedback from direct observation and from demolition plans, specific and timely, with strengths named too
5. **Step 5** - Record progress, keep the training file current, and escalate persistent gaps through supervision, not gossip

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: asbestos exposure.
- Never let this happen: uncontrolled collapse.
- Never let this happen: dust and noise breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a demolition contractor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to demolition sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

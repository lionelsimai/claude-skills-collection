## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what fund managers, investors and research subscribers need that sits outside the investment analyst scope in investment firms
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from research reports — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and forecast accuracy stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with fund managers, investors and research subscribers: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of conflicts of interest undisclosed

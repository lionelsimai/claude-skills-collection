# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Restaurant Management

Explain complex restaurant manager matters to diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Food cost | the cost of ingredients as a share of menu price |
| Cover | one diner served in a service |
| Allergen management | controlling ingredients that can cause allergic reactions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind menus and costing sheets — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as food hygiene failures
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

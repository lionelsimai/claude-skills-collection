## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in tourist sites, cities and attractions, centred on booking platforms, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against tour itineraries
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to group members lost
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

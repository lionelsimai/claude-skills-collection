# Scheduling & Workload Management — Environmental Consulting

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a environmental consultant in client sites and consultancy offices. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a environmental consultant working with developers, industries and regulators in client sites and consultancy offices.

## When to use
- You are a environmental consultant preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in client sites and consultancy offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Baseline | conditions measured before a change, for comparison |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive report turnaround
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in client sites and consultancy offices first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind assessment reports — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving missed contamination) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or sampling equipment availability

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: missed contamination.
- Never let this happen: sampling errors.
- Never let this happen: missed permit deadlines.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a environmental consultant; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to client sites and consultancy offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

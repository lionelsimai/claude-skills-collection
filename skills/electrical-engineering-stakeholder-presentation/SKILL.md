# Stakeholder Presentation — Electrical Engineering

Present electrical engineer findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a electrical engineer working with energy, manufacturing and building clients in design offices, plants and substations.

## When to use
- You are a electrical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, plants and substations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Load | the power drawn by a system or device |
| Protection relay | a device that detects faults and isolates circuits |
| Earthing | connecting equipment to earth for safety |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from circuit designs and movement in test pass rates, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including electric shock risks and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in design offices, plants and substations
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in design offices, plants and substations] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [energy, manufacturing and building clients and other stakeholders]

1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

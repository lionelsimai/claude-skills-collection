# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Massage Therapy

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a massage therapist relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a massage therapist working with clients seeking treatment for tension, pain or relaxation in clinics, spas and sports settings.

## When to use
- You are a massage therapist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in clinics, spas and sports settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Contraindication | a condition making a treatment unsafe or requiring adaptation |
| Deep tissue | massage working on deeper layers of muscle |
| Aftercare | advice given to a client following treatment |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in clinics, spas and sports settings, centred on massage tables, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against client health forms
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to contraindications missed causing harm
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

- Never let this happen: boundary issues.
- Never let this happen: injuries aggravated.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a massage therapist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

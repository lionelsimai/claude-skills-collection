# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Electrical Engineering

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for energy, manufacturing and building clients are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a electrical engineer working with energy, manufacturing and building clients in design offices, plants and substations.

## When to use
- You are a electrical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, plants and substations.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Load | the power drawn by a system or device |
| Protection relay | a device that detects faults and isolates circuits |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how test pass rates is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using design software where measurement is needed, and record it in circuit designs
3. **Step 3** - Give energy, manufacturing and building clients clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to electric shock risks shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

# Safety Protocol — Facilities Management

Work safely in commercial buildings and campuses: a practical safety protocol for a facilities manager and building occupants, owners and service teams. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a facilities manager working with building occupants, owners and service teams in commercial buildings and campuses.

## When to use
- You are a facilities manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in commercial buildings and campuses.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in commercial buildings and campuses for staff, building occupants, owners and service teams and visitors, starting with life-safety systems unmaintained
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as CAFM systems
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in maintenance schedules
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

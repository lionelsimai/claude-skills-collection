# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Freight Forwarding

Explain complex freight forwarder matters to importers, exporters and carriers in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a freight forwarder working with importers, exporters and carriers in freight offices, ports and airports.

## When to use
- You are a freight forwarder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in freight offices, ports and airports.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Bill of lading | the transport document evidencing the carriage contract |
| Cut-off | the deadline for delivering cargo for a sailing or flight |
| Demurrage | charges for keeping containers beyond free time |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what importers, exporters and carriers actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind shipping documents — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as customs delays from bad documents
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking importers, exporters and carriers to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

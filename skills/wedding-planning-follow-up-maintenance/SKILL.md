# Follow-Up & Maintenance Schedule — Wedding Planning

Plan follow-up and maintenance so gains for couples and their families are checked and sustained. The deliverable is a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
This skill is written for a wedding planner working with couples and their families in venues, suppliers and planning offices.

## When to use
- You are a wedding planner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in venues, suppliers and planning offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a follow-up schedule with intervals, checks and escalation triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Set follow-up intervals from the plan and from how milestone completion is expected to move over time
2. **Step 2** - Define what is checked at each contact, using planning software where measurement is needed, and record it in planning timelines
3. **Step 3** - Give couples and their families clear guidance for the interval: what to do, what to watch for, when to make contact sooner
4. **Step 4** - Act early on warning signs — deterioration linked to supplier failures on the day shortens the interval, never lengthens it
5. **Step 5** - Close the follow-up cycle formally when goals are met, with a summary and re-entry route if needs return

- Never let this happen: budget overruns.
- Never let this happen: timeline collapse on the day.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a wedding planner; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

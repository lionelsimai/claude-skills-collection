# Referral & Coordination — Sustainability Management

Refer businesses, investors and regulators onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a sustainability manager working with businesses, investors and regulators in organisations across all sectors.

## When to use
- You are a sustainability manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in organisations across all sectors.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what businesses, investors and regulators need that sits outside the sustainability manager scope in organisations across all sectors
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from carbon inventories — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and emissions reduction progress stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with businesses, investors and regulators: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of greenwashing accusations

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: greenwashing accusations.
- Never let this happen: data gaps in reporting.
- Never let this happen: missed regulatory deadlines.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a sustainability manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to organisations across all sectors's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

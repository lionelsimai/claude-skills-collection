# Scheduling & Workload Management — Secondary Mathematics Education

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a secondary mathematics teacher in a secondary school mathematics department. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a secondary mathematics teacher working with secondary school students and their parents in a secondary school mathematics department.

## When to use
- You are a secondary mathematics teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a secondary school mathematics department.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Scheme of work | the planned sequence of topics across a term or year |
| Intervention | targeted extra teaching for pupils who are behind |
| Working mathematically | applying maths to problem solving, not just procedures |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive examination results
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in a secondary school mathematics department first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind schemes of work — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving misconceptions unaddressed) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or graphing software availability

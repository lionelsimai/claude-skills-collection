# Records & Documentation — Insurance Broking

Create and maintain accurate, auditable records for work with businesses and individuals buying insurance. The deliverable is a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
This skill is written for a insurance broker working with businesses and individuals buying insurance in brokerages and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a insurance broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in brokerages and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a documentation standard, templates and a completed record set.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Placement | arranging cover with an insurer |
| Renewal | continuing a policy for a further period |
| Schedule | the document listing the specific cover details |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Use needs assessments as the single source of truth; record facts, observations and decisions — not speculation
2. **Step 2** - Write entries contemporaneously where possible, dated and attributable, in the format brokerages and client premises requires
3. **Step 3** - Record measurements and outcomes that feed renewal retention exactly as observed, with units and method noted
4. **Step 4** - Store and share records under the confidentiality and retention rules that apply in brokerages and client premises, guarding against cover gaps at renewal
5. **Step 5** - Audit a sample of records monthly for completeness and correct anything missing while the work is still current

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: cover gaps at renewal.
- Never let this happen: missed deadlines leaving clients uninsured.
- Never let this happen: advice errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a insurance broker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to brokerages and client premises's rules.

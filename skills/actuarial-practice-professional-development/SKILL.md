# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Actuarial Practice

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising actuary. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a actuary working with insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices.

## When to use
- You are a actuary preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance and pensions offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for insurance and pensions offices and the evidence in your recent valuation reports
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on model errors affecting reserves — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for model validation results
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: model errors affecting reserves.
- Never let this happen: stale assumptions.
- Never let this happen: regulatory breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a actuary; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to insurance and pensions offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Solar Energy

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising solar PV engineer. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a solar PV engineer working with homeowners, businesses and energy companies in rooftops, solar farms and offices.

## When to use
- You are a solar PV engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in rooftops, solar farms and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Inverter | the device converting panel DC output to usable AC |
| Performance ratio | actual output compared with theoretical maximum |
| Feed-in | electricity exported to the grid |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for rooftops, solar farms and offices and the evidence in your recent system designs
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on falls from roofs — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for system performance ratio
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in rooftops, solar farms and offices] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [homeowners, businesses and energy companies and other stakeholders]

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: falls from roofs.
- Never let this happen: electrical faults.
- Never let this happen: underperforming systems unnoticed.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a solar PV engineer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to rooftops, solar farms and offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

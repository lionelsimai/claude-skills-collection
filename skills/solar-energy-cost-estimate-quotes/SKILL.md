# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Solar Energy

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a solar PV engineer. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a solar PV engineer working with homeowners, businesses and energy companies in rooftops, solar farms and offices.

## When to use
- You are a solar PV engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in rooftops, solar farms and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Inverter | the device converting panel DC output to usable AC |
| Performance ratio | actual output compared with theoretical maximum |
| Feed-in | electricity exported to the grid |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and system designs; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, PV panels and inverters usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against falls from roofs
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as system performance ratio
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Music Education

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising music teacher. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a music teacher working with music students of all ages and their parents in music services, schools and private studios.

## When to use
- You are a music teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in music services, schools and private studios.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Sight-reading | playing music at first sight without rehearsal |
| Ensemble | a group of musicians performing together |
| Graded exams | staged practical examinations in instrumental performance |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for music services, schools and private studios and the evidence in your recent lesson plans
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on hearing damage from loud practice — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for examination results
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in music services, schools and private studios] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [music students of all ages and their parents and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against examination results.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in music services, schools and private studios.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: hearing damage from loud practice.
- Never let this happen: exam entry errors.
- Never let this happen: safeguarding at one-to-one lessons.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a music teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to music services, schools and private studios's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

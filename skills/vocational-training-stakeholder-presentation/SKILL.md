# Stakeholder Presentation — Vocational Training

Present vocational trainer and assessor findings and proposals to decision-makers and stakeholders. The deliverable is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
This skill is written for a vocational trainer and assessor working with apprentices, trainees and employers in training centres, workplaces and online.

## When to use
- You are a vocational trainer and assessor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in training centres, workplaces and online.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Competence | the ability to perform work to the required standard |
| Portfolio | a collection of evidence of a learner's skills |
| IQA | internal quality assurance of assessment decisions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Open with the decision or conclusion, then the evidence — busy stakeholders should not wait for the point
2. **Step 2** - Support claims with data from training plans and movement in achievement rates, with sources and limits stated
3. **Step 3** - Address the hard parts directly, including unsafe practice assessed as competent and what it would take to reduce it
4. **Step 4** - Make the ask specific: what decision, by when, with what resources for work in training centres, workplaces and online
5. **Step 5** - Follow up in writing the same day: decisions made, actions agreed, owners and dates

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Vocational Training |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | training plans; assessment records; portfolios of evidence; progress reviews |
| Core tools & equipment | e-portfolio systems; workshop equipment; simulators; video observation tools |
| People involved | apprentices, trainees and employers; the vocational trainer and assessor; colleagues and other services in training centres, workplaces and online |
| Primary record | training plans — keep it current, dated and attributable |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in training centres, workplaces and online] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [apprentices, trainees and employers and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a presentation structure with evidence summary and decision asks, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in training plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against achievement rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in training centres, workplaces and online.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: unsafe practice assessed as competent.
- Never let this happen: evidence gaps in portfolios.
- Never let this happen: learner disengagement.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a vocational trainer and assessor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to training centres, workplaces and online's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

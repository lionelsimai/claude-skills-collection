# New Client Onboarding Pack — Immigration Law

Onboard individuals, families and employers navigating immigration rules smoothly into a immigration solicitor's service in a law firm and immigration tribunals. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a immigration solicitor working with individuals, families and employers navigating immigration rules in a law firm and immigration tribunals.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from application bundles
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on application success rates will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to a law firm and immigration tribunals, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or missed application deadlines

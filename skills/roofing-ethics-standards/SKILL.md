# Ethics & Professional Standards — Roofing

This skill is written for a roofer working with homeowners, builders and property managers in rooftops and construction sites.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in rooftops and construction sites: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect homeowners, builders and property managers first where duties conflict, and be alert to how falls from height can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from roof surveys, and support colleagues who do the same

# Safety Protocol — Geotechnical Engineering

Work safely in site investigation teams and design offices: a practical safety protocol for a geotechnical engineer and developers, contractors and infrastructure owners. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a geotechnical engineer working with developers, contractors and infrastructure owners in site investigation teams and design offices.

## When to use
- You are a geotechnical engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in site investigation teams and design offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Borehole | a drilled hole used to sample ground and groundwater |
| Bearing capacity | the load the ground can safely support |
| Settlement | downward movement of the ground or a structure |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in site investigation teams and design offices for staff, developers, contractors and infrastructure owners and visitors, starting with ground conditions misjudged
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as drilling rigs
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in ground investigation reports
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

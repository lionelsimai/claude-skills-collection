# Session Delivery Guide — Roofing

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for homeowners, builders and property managers in rooftops and construction sites. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a roofer working with homeowners, builders and property managers in rooftops and construction sites.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review roof surveys before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as falls from height
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and safety harnesses; check everything works and is safe before homeowners, builders and property managers arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to job completion times
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

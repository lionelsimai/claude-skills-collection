# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Wedding Planning

Explain complex wedding planner matters to couples and their families in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a wedding planner working with couples and their families in venues, suppliers and planning offices.

## When to use
- You are a wedding planner preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in venues, suppliers and planning offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Run sheet | the minute-by-minute schedule for the wedding day |
| Contingency | backup plans for weather, suppliers or timings |
| Venue styling | the decoration and layout of the celebration space |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what couples and their families actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind planning timelines — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as supplier failures on the day
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking couples and their families to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

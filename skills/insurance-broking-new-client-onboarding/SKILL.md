# New Client Onboarding Pack — Insurance Broking

Onboard businesses and individuals buying insurance smoothly into a insurance broker's service in brokerages and client premises. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a insurance broker working with businesses and individuals buying insurance in brokerages and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a insurance broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in brokerages and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from needs assessments
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on renewal retention will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to brokerages and client premises, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or cover gaps at renewal

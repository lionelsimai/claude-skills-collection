# Session Delivery Guide — Chemical Engineering

This skill is written for a chemical engineer working with process plants and manufacturing clients in chemical plants and design offices.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review process flow diagrams before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as process safety incidents
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and process simulators; check everything works and is safe before process plants and manufacturing clients arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to plant uptime
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

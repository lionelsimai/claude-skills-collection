# Compliance & Standards Checklist — Retail Banking

Check work in bank branches and contact centres against the laws, standards and codes that govern a retail banking adviser. The deliverable is a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
This skill is written for a retail banking adviser working with personal banking customers in bank branches and contact centres.

## When to use
- You are a retail banking adviser preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in bank branches and contact centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed compliance checklist with gaps and corrective actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| KYC | know your customer identity and risk checks |
| Overdraft | an agreed facility to spend beyond an account balance |
| Vulnerable customer | a customer needing extra care due to their circumstances |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the obligations that apply: legislation, regulator guidance, professional codes and local policy in bank branches and contact centres
2. **Step 2** - Work through each obligation against current practice and customer records, marking compliant, partial or missing
3. **Step 3** - Investigate every gap — especially anything touching mis-selling risks — and record the evidence for the judgement
4. **Step 4** - Turn gaps into corrective actions with owners and deadlines, proportionate to the risk
5. **Step 5** - Re-check completed actions, file the checklist, and diarise the next compliance review

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: mis-selling risks.
- Never let this happen: fraud missed.
- Never let this happen: vulnerable customers unrecognised.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a retail banking adviser; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to bank branches and contact centres's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

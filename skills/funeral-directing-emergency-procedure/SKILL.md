# Emergency Response Procedure — Funeral Directing

Respond to emergencies in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a funeral director working with bereaved families in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for bereaved families, especially wrong details in services
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a funeral director is trained and authorised for, using chapel facilities as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep bereaved families and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in funeral arrangements afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

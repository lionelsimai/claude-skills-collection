# Referral & Coordination — Freight Forwarding

Refer importers, exporters and carriers onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a freight forwarder working with importers, exporters and carriers in freight offices, ports and airports.

## When to use
- You are a freight forwarder preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in freight offices, ports and airports.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what importers, exporters and carriers need that sits outside the freight forwarder scope in freight offices, ports and airports
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from shipping documents — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked

# Service Plan Design — Painting & Decorating

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for homeowners, landlords and commercial clients as a painter and decorator. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a painter and decorator working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, offices and building sites.

## When to use
- You are a painter and decorator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, offices and building sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients — specific enough to measure against job completion times
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for homes, offices and building sites, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around quotes so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, sanders and materials, with contingencies for falls from height
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates

# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Actuarial Practice

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a actuary. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a actuary working with insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices.

## When to use
- You are a actuary preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance and pensions offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and valuation reports; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, statistical software usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against model errors affecting reserves
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as model validation results
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

- Never let this happen: stale assumptions.
- Never let this happen: regulatory breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a actuary; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to insurance and pensions offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.

# Session Delivery Guide — Physical Education

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for school pupils and sports teams in school sports facilities and playing fields. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a PE teacher working with school pupils and sports teams in school sports facilities and playing fields.

## When to use
- You are a PE teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in school sports facilities and playing fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Aerobic fitness | the ability to sustain activity using oxygen |
| Fixture | a scheduled match against another school or team |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review lesson plans before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as injuries from poor supervision
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and fitness testing equipment; check everything works and is safe before school pupils and sports teams arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to participation rates
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: injuries from poor supervision.
- Never let this happen: unsafe equipment.
- Never let this happen: exclusion of less confident pupils.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a PE teacher; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to school sports facilities and playing fields's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

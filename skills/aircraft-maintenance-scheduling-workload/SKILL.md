# Scheduling & Workload Management — Aircraft Maintenance

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a aircraft maintenance engineer in hangars and line stations. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a aircraft maintenance engineer working with airlines, operators and passengers (indirectly) in hangars and line stations.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive task completion times
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in hangars and line stations first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind maintenance records — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving missed inspections) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or aircraft tooling availability

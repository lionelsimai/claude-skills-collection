# Scheduling & Workload Management — Building Inspection

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a building inspector and surveyor in properties and construction sites. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a building inspector and surveyor working with homeowners, buyers and local authorities in properties and construction sites.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive report turnaround
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in properties and construction sites first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind survey reports — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving serious defects missed) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or moisture meters availability

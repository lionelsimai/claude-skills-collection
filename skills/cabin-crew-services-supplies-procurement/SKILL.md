# Supplies & Procurement — Cabin Crew Services

Specify, source and manage the supplies and materials used in aircraft cabins. The deliverable is a procurement list with specifications, stock levels and supplier checks.
This skill is written for a cabin crew member working with airline passengers and flight crew in aircraft cabins.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List what the work consumes, drawing on flight reports and the kit centred on safety equipment, with exact specifications
2. **Step 2** - Set minimum stock levels from usage rates and lead times so work never stalls waiting for supplies
3. **Step 3** - Compare suppliers on quality, reliability and total cost — not unit price alone — and check credentials
4. **Step 4** - Receive and inspect deliveries; reject or quarantine substandard stock before it can contribute to evacuation readiness gaps
5. **Step 5** - Track usage and waste monthly; adjust order quantities and challenge specifications that no longer fit

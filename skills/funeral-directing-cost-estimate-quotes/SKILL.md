# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Funeral Directing

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a funeral director. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a funeral director working with bereaved families in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.

## When to use
- You are a funeral director preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in funeral homes, crematoria and cemeteries.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Arrangement | the meeting where a funeral is planned |
| Embalming | preserving and preparing a deceased person |
| Cortege | the procession of vehicles at a funeral |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and funeral arrangements; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, chapel facilities usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against wrong details in services
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as arrangement accuracy
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

- Never let this happen: identification mistakes.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a funeral director; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

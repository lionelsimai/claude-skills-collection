# Quality Audit — Plumbing

Audit the quality of plumber work in homes, building sites and commercial premises and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a plumber working with homeowners, landlords and commercial clients in homes, building sites and commercial premises.

## When to use
- You are a plumber preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in homes, building sites and commercial premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to first-time fix rate and the standards for homes, building sites and commercial premises
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of job sheets and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including water damage from leaks
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

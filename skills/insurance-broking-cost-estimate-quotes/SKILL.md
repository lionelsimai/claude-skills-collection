# Cost Estimates & Quotes — Insurance Broking

Build transparent, defensible cost estimates and quotes as a insurance broker. The deliverable is an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
This skill is written for a insurance broker working with businesses and individuals buying insurance in brokerages and client premises.

## When to use
- You are a insurance broker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in brokerages and client premises.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an itemised estimate with assumptions, exclusions and validity terms.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Placement | arranging cover with an insurer |
| Renewal | continuing a policy for a further period |
| Schedule | the document listing the specific cover details |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Scope the work precisely from the assessment and needs assessments; list what is included and excluded
2. **Step 2** - Cost each element: professional time, broking platforms usage, materials, travel and third-party fees
3. **Step 3** - Add contingency proportionate to uncertainty — and say so openly rather than burying it, which protects against cover gaps at renewal
4. **Step 4** - Present the quote with options where genuine choices exist, and relate value to outcomes such as renewal retention
5. **Step 5** - Record acceptance, variations and final costs against the estimate so future quotes get more accurate

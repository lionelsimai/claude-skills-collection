# Quality Audit — Investment Analysis

Audit the quality of investment analyst work in investment firms and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a investment analyst working with fund managers, investors and research subscribers in investment firms.

## When to use
- You are a investment analyst preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in investment firms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to forecast accuracy and the standards for investment firms
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of research reports and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including conflicts of interest undisclosed
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest undisclosed.
- Never let this happen: model errors.
- Never let this happen: insider information mishandled.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a investment analyst; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to investment firms's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

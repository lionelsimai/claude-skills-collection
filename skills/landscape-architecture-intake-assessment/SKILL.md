# Intake & Initial Assessment — Landscape Architecture

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for developers, councils and private clients in design studios and outdoor sites. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a landscape architect working with developers, councils and private clients in design studios and outdoor sites.

## When to use
- You are a landscape architect preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design studios and outdoor sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold design concepts
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from developers, councils and private clients, using the standard intake structure for design studios and outdoor sites
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to design approval rates, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially planting failures — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for developers, councils and private clients in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of design concepts

# Scheduling & Workload Management — Payment Operations

Plan schedules, caseloads and turnaround times for a payments operations specialist in payment operations centres. The deliverable is a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
This skill is written for a payments operations specialist working with banks, fintechs and their customers in payment operations centres.

## When to use
- You are a payments operations specialist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in payment operations centres.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a workable schedule template with priorities, buffers and escalation rules.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Straight-through processing | payments completed without manual intervention |
| Exception | a payment needing manual review or repair |
| Cut-off | the deadline for same-day payment processing |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map demand: appointment or job types, durations, and the deadlines that drive straight-through processing rate
2. **Step 2** - Build the schedule around fixed commitments in payment operations centres first, then fit flexible work around them
3. **Step 3** - Protect buffer time for overruns, urgent cases and the admin behind payment queues — schedules without buffers fail by week two
4. **Step 4** - Define triage rules so urgent work (for example anything involving failed payments harming customers) displaces routine work predictably
5. **Step 5** - Review the schedule weekly against actuals and adjust booking rules, staffing or payment processing systems availability

# Service Plan Design — Tax Law

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions as a tax solicitor. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a tax solicitor working with individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions in a law firm and tax tribunals.

## When to use
- You are a tax solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and tax tribunals.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Clearance | advance confirmation from a tax authority about treatment of a transaction |

| Penalty mitigation | reducing penalties through cooperation and care |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions — specific enough to measure against opinion turnaround
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for a law firm and tax tribunals, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around tax opinions so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, tax research databases and materials, with contingencies for filing deadlines missed
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in a law firm and tax tribunals] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [individuals, businesses and charities with tax questions and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: filing deadlines missed.
- Never let this happen: penalties from wrong advice.
- Never let this happen: privilege mishandled.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a tax solicitor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to a law firm and tax tribunals's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Safety Protocol — Valuation & Appraisal

Work safely in properties and surveying offices: a practical safety protocol for a chartered valuation surveyor and lenders, buyers, sellers and investors. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.
This skill is written for a chartered valuation surveyor working with lenders, buyers, sellers and investors in properties and surveying offices.

- You are a chartered valuation surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in properties and surveying offices.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in properties and surveying offices for staff, lenders, buyers, sellers and investors and visitors, starting with overvaluation losses
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as valuation software
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in valuation reports
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

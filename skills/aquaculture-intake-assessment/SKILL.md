# Intake & Initial Assessment — Aquaculture

Run a structured intake and initial assessment for seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms. The deliverable is a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
This skill is written for a fish farm manager working with seafood buyers and processors in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.

## When to use
- You are a fish farm manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in lakes, coastal sites and fish farms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a completed intake record and initial assessment summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold stock records
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from seafood buyers and processors, using the standard intake structure for lakes, coastal sites and fish farms
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to growth rates, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially disease outbreaks in stock — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for seafood buyers and processors in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of stock records

- Never let this happen: disease outbreaks in stock.
- Never let this happen: escapes into the wild.
- Never let this happen: water quality crashes.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a fish farm manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to lakes, coastal sites and fish farms's rules.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in hotels and serviced apartments for staff, hotel guests and housekeeping teams and visitors, starting with chemical injuries
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as housekeeping software
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in room inspection checklists
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

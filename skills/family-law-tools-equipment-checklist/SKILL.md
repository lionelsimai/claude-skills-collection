# Tools & Equipment Checklist — Family Law

Select, check and maintain the tools and equipment a family law solicitor relies on. The deliverable is an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an equipment checklist with inspection points, maintenance dates and replacement triggers.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Disclosure | the formal exchange of financial information in family cases |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - List the essential kit for work in a law firm and family court, centred on case management systems, with what each item is used for
2. **Step 2** - Define pre-use checks: condition, calibration or charge status, cleanliness and safety features
3. **Step 3** - Set maintenance and replacement intervals based on usage and manufacturer guidance, and log them against client care letters
4. **Step 4** - Quarantine faulty items immediately — faulty kit is a direct route to missed court deadlines
5. **Step 5** - Review the kit list quarterly against the work actually done and retire or add items deliberately

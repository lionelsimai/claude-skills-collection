# Service Plan Design — Masonry

Design a realistic, measurable service plan for builders, homeowners and heritage organisations as a mason and bricklayer. The deliverable is a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
This skill is written for a mason and bricklayer working with builders, homeowners and heritage organisations in construction sites and historic buildings.

## When to use
- You are a mason and bricklayer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction sites and historic buildings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a written plan with goals, actions, owners, timelines and review dates.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Start from the assessment findings and agree goals with builders, homeowners and heritage organisations — specific enough to measure against work completion rates
2. **Step 2** - Break each goal into actions sequenced for construction sites and historic buildings, naming who does what and by when
3. **Step 3** - Build the plan around laying records so progress, changes and decisions are recorded in one place
4. **Step 4** - Price or resource the plan honestly: time, trowels and levels and materials, with contingencies for structural defects from poor work
5. **Step 5** - Set the review date and the trigger points that would change the plan, and confirm the plan with all parties

# Progress Review & Adjustment — Maritime Law

Review progress for ship owners, insurers and cargo interests against their plan and adjust it with evidence. The deliverable is a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
This skill is written for a maritime solicitor working with ship owners, insurers and cargo interests in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts.

## When to use
- You are a maritime solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a progress review summary with decisions and an updated plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Pull together charterparties and the latest results for case resolution times since the last review
2. **Step 2** - Compare actual progress with the plan's goals and timelines; identify what moved, what stalled and why
3. **Step 3** - Discuss the evidence with ship owners, insurers and cargo interests honestly, including where the original plan no longer fits
4. **Step 4** - Agree adjustments: continue, intensify, change approach or close — and record the reasoning
5. **Step 5** - Update the plan and review date, and communicate changes to everyone involved in a shipping law firm and admiralty courts

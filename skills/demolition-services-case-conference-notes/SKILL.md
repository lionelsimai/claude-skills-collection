# Case Conference & Meeting Notes — Demolition Services

Prepare for, contribute to and record case conferences and multi-professional meetings. The deliverable is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
This skill is written for a demolition contractor working with developers, councils and property owners in demolition sites.

## When to use
- You are a demolition contractor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in demolition sites.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Soft strip | removing non-structural elements before demolition |
| Asbestos survey | inspection identifying asbestos before work starts |
| Arisings | materials produced by demolition for reuse or disposal |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Prepare a one-page brief from demolition plans: current position, key evidence, the questions the meeting must answer
2. **Step 2** - Present your contribution concisely, leading with the position on project completion times and any live concerns about asbestos exposure
3. **Step 3** - Listen for information that changes the picture; update your understanding rather than defending a position
4. **Step 4** - Record decisions, actions, owners and deadlines during the meeting — not from memory afterwards
5. **Step 5** - Circulate the record promptly to attendees in demolition sites, correct errors fast, and file it with the main record

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in demolition sites] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [developers, councils and property owners and other stakeholders]

The finished output is meeting preparation notes and a decision-focused record of outcomes, structured as:

2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: asbestos exposure.
- Never let this happen: uncontrolled collapse.
- Never let this happen: dust and noise breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a demolition contractor; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to demolition sites's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

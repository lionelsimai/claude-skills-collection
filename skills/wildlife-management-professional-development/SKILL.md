# Continuing Professional Development Plan — Wildlife Management

Plan and evidence continuing professional development for a practising wildlife manager. The deliverable is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
This skill is written for a wildlife manager working with conservation bodies, landowners and the public in reserves, estates and rural areas.

## When to use
- You are a wildlife manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in reserves, estates and rural areas.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Protected species | an animal or plant given legal protection |
| Cull | selective reduction of a population for management reasons |
| Telemetry | tracking animals remotely with transmitters |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Audit current competence against the standards for reserves, estates and rural areas and the evidence in your recent population surveys
2. **Step 2** - Choose development priorities that close real gaps — including staying current on harm to protected species — not just easy hours
3. **Step 3** - Mix formats: courses, supervised practice, case review, reading and teaching others
4. **Step 4** - After each activity, write a short reflection: what changed in your practice and what it means for population trends
5. **Step 5** - Keep the CPD record inspection-ready and review the plan at appraisal or revalidation time

## Profession-Specific Components
| Component | Details for Wildlife Management |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Core documentation | population surveys; management plans; licence applications; incident records |
| Core tools & equipment | trapping and tracking equipment; cameras; drones; vehicles |
| People involved | conservation bodies, landowners and the public; the wildlife manager; colleagues and other services in reserves, estates and rural areas |

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in reserves, estates and rural areas] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [conservation bodies, landowners and the public and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a CPD plan mapped to competency gaps, with evidence and reflection records, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in population surveys.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against population trends.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in reserves, estates and rural areas.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: harm to protected species.
- Never let this happen: public conflict over control.
- Never let this happen: disease spread.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a wildlife manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to reserves, estates and rural areas's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

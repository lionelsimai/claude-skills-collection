# Referral & Coordination — Mediation & Arbitration

Refer parties in dispute and their representatives onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a mediator and arbitrator working with parties in dispute and their representatives in mediation venues and hearing rooms.

## When to use
- You are a mediator and arbitrator preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mediation venues and hearing rooms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Mediation | a voluntary process where a neutral helps parties settle |
| Arbitration | private dispute resolution producing a binding award |
| Without prejudice | communications that cannot be used in court if settlement fails |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what parties in dispute and their representatives need that sits outside the mediator and arbitrator scope in mediation venues and hearing rooms
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from mediation agreements — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and settlement rates stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with parties in dispute and their representatives: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of bias perceived or actual

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in mediation venues and hearing rooms] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [parties in dispute and their representatives and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in mediation agreements.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against settlement rates.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in mediation venues and hearing rooms.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: bias perceived or actual.
- Never let this happen: settlement terms unenforceable.
- Never let this happen: confidentiality breached.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a mediator and arbitrator; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to mediation venues and hearing rooms's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

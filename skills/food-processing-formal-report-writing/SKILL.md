# Formal Report Writing — Food Processing

Write formal reports as a food production manager that stand up to scrutiny from any reader. The deliverable is a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
This skill is written for a food production manager working with retailers, wholesalers and consumers in food factories and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a food production manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in food factories and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a report template with structure, evidence standards and review steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Traceability | following a product through every production stage |
| Shelf life | how long a product remains safe and acceptable |
| Critical control point | a step where control prevents a food safety hazard |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Establish the report's purpose, audience and deadline before writing; gather source material from production plans
2. **Step 2** - Structure: summary, background, method, findings, analysis, recommendations — in that order
3. **Step 3** - Write findings factually and separately from opinion; label professional judgement as judgement
4. **Step 4** - Support key points with evidence and with production yield where outcomes are claimed; acknowledge limitations openly
5. **Step 5** - Proofread for accuracy and tone, check names, dates and figures twice, and consider contamination incidents before release

- Never let this happen: traceability failures.
- Never let this happen: labelling errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a food production manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.

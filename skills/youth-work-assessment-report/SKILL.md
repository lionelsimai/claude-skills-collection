# Assessment & Findings Report — Youth Work

Assess young people and their communities and write a defensible findings report as a youth worker. The deliverable is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
This skill is written for a youth worker working with young people and their communities in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.

## When to use
- You are a youth worker preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in youth centres, schools and outreach settings.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Detached work | youth work delivered on streets and in public spaces |
| Outcome star | a visual tool tracking a young person's progress |
| Participation | young people's active involvement in decisions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review the intake record and session plans so the assessment builds on verified information, not assumptions
2. **Step 2** - Carry out the assessment using engagement tools, following the recognised method for youth centres, schools and outreach settings
3. **Step 3** - Compare findings against the benchmarks behind attendance and engagement and note where evidence is strong, weak or missing
4. **Step 4** - Draft the report: findings first, then interpretation, then recommendations in priority order
5. **Step 5** - Check the draft for accuracy and for safeguarding disclosures missed; have it reviewed where the profession's standards require a second sign-off

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in youth centres, schools and outreach settings] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [young people and their communities and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a structured assessment report with findings, evidence and recommendations, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against attendance and engagement.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: safeguarding disclosures missed.
- Never let this happen: unsafe activities.
- Never let this happen: young people disengaging.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a youth worker; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to youth centres, schools and outreach settings's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

1. **Step 1** - Identify what individuals, families and employers navigating immigration rules actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind application bundles — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as missed application deadlines
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking individuals, families and employers navigating immigration rules to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

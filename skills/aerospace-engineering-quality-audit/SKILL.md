# Quality Audit — Aerospace Engineering

Audit the quality of aerospace engineer work in design offices, test facilities and hangars and drive measurable improvement. The deliverable is an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
This skill is written for a aerospace engineer working with aircraft manufacturers, airlines and space agencies in design offices, test facilities and hangars.

## When to use
- You are a aerospace engineer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in design offices, test facilities and hangars.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an audit report with scores, evidence and an improvement plan.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define the audit sample and criteria up front, anchored to test programme milestones and the standards for design offices, test facilities and hangars
2. **Step 2** - Review a sample of design analyses and observe practice where permitted, scoring consistently
3. **Step 3** - Separate isolated errors from patterns — patterns point to system fixes, not individual blame
4. **Step 4** - Report findings with evidence, and agree improvement actions that address the root causes, including test incidents
5. **Step 5** - Re-audit after the agreed interval and publish the change in scores so improvement is visible

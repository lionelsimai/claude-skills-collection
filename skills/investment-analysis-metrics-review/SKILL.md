# Metrics & Outcomes Review — Investment Analysis

Track, interpret and act on the outcome metrics that matter for a investment analyst. The deliverable is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
This skill is written for a investment analyst working with fund managers, investors and research subscribers in investment firms.

## When to use
- You are a investment analyst preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in investment firms.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Valuation | estimating what an investment is worth |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define each metric precisely — start with forecast accuracy — including source data in research reports and collection method
2. **Step 2** - Set review cadence and comparison baselines so trends, not single data points, drive decisions
3. **Step 3** - Investigate movements before celebrating or panicking: check data quality, case mix and one-off causes
4. **Step 4** - Link metric movements to practice changes and to risks such as conflicts of interest undisclosed; test one change at a time where possible
5. **Step 5** - Report results honestly to stakeholders in investment firms, including where outcomes fell short and what changes

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: conflicts of interest undisclosed.
- Never let this happen: model errors.
- Never let this happen: insider information mishandled.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a investment analyst; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to investment firms's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

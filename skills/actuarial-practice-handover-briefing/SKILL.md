# Handover Briefing — Actuarial Practice

Hand over insurers, pension schemes and regulators or active work to another professional without losing critical detail. The deliverable is a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions.
This skill is written for a actuary working with insurers, pension schemes and regulators in insurance and pensions offices.

## When to use
- You are a actuary preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in insurance and pensions offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a structured handover brief covering status, risks and next actions.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Summarise the current position from valuation reports: what is done, what is in progress, what is waiting
2. **Step 2** - Highlight live risks first — model errors affecting reserves — and the controls or watchpoints already in place
3. **Step 3** - State the next actions with owners and deadlines, and the results expected for model validation results
4. **Step 4** - Walk the receiving professional through the brief, answer questions, and confirm understanding
5. **Step 5** - Record that handover happened, who accepted it, and where the full record lives in insurance and pensions offices

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: model errors affecting reserves.
- Never let this happen: stale assumptions.
- Never let this happen: regulatory breaches.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a actuary; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to insurance and pensions offices's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

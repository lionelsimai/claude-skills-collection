# Emergency Response Procedure — Mine Operations

Respond to emergencies in mines and processing plants calmly, in role and in the right order. The deliverable is an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
This skill is written for a mine operations manager working with mining companies and mineral buyers in mines and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a mine operations manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in mines and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an emergency procedure card: recognition, first actions, escalation and recording.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Recognise the emergency early: know the presentations that matter for mining companies and mineral buyers, especially underground incidents
2. **Step 2** - Take the immediate actions a mine operations manager is trained and authorised for, using mining machinery as drilled
3. **Step 3** - Call for help with a structured message: location in mines and processing plants, situation, what has been done, what is needed
4. **Step 4** - Keep mining companies and mineral buyers and bystanders informed and safe while help arrives; hand over with a concise summary
5. **Step 5** - Record the event fully in production reports afterwards, debrief the team, and update the procedure with lessons learned

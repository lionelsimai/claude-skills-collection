# Ethics & Professional Standards — Quantity Surveying

Apply the ethical codes and professional standards that govern a quantity surveyor. The deliverable is an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
This skill is written for a quantity surveyor working with developers, contractors and public clients in construction projects and offices.

## When to use
- You are a quantity surveyor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in construction projects and offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an ethics guide with principles, common dilemmas and decision steps.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Bill of quantities | an itemised list of materials and work for pricing |
| Valuation | an assessment of work completed for interim payment |
| Final account | the agreed total cost at a project's end |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Know the code that applies in construction projects and offices: consent, confidentiality, honesty, competence and conflicts of interest
2. **Step 2** - Work through dilemmas with a framework: facts, stakeholders, options, code provisions, then decide and record
3. **Step 3** - Protect developers, contractors and public clients first where duties conflict, and be alert to how cost overruns unreported can arise from ethical shortcuts
4. **Step 4** - Declare conflicts of interest early and manage them transparently rather than hoping they stay invisible
5. **Step 5** - Raise concerns about others' practice through the proper channels, with evidence from bills of quantities, and support colleagues who do the same

# Incident Reporting & Response — Commercial Diving

Respond to, record and learn from incidents involving offshore, construction and salvage clients in underwater work sites and support vessels. The deliverable is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
This skill is written for a commercial diver working with offshore, construction and salvage clients in underwater work sites and support vessels.

## When to use
- You are a commercial diver preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in underwater work sites and support vessels.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Umbilical | the cable and hoses supplying air and communications to a diver |
| Decompression | controlled ascent stops letting gases leave the body safely |
| Dive plan | the documented method and limits for a dive |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Make the situation safe first; attend to people before paperwork, and escalate if decompression sickness is still live
2. **Step 2** - Record facts while they are fresh: what happened, when, who was involved, what was observed — in dive plans
3. **Step 3** - Notify the people and bodies who must know, within the timeframes that apply in underwater work sites and support vessels
4. **Step 4** - Support those affected, including honest communication with offshore, construction and salvage clients about what happened and what happens next
5. **Step 5** - Analyse causes without blame, agree preventive actions with owners and dates, and track them to closure

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in underwater work sites and support vessels] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [offshore, construction and salvage clients and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a factual incident report and a lessons-learned action list, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate:** Confirm scope, consent and the evidence available in dive plans.
2. **Short-term:** Complete the workflow steps and record outcomes against dive plan compliance.
3. **Medium-term:** Review results, close actions, and update local procedures in underwater work sites and support vessels.

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: decompression sickness.
- Never let this happen: equipment failure underwater.
- Never let this happen: communication loss.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a commercial diver; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to underwater work sites and support vessels's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm identity, consent and the reason for referral or engagement, and open the record that will hold valuation reports
2. **Step 2** - Gather history and current circumstances from insurers, pension schemes and regulators, using the standard intake structure for insurance and pensions offices
3. **Step 3** - Record baseline observations and measurements relevant to model validation results, noting anything that changes the priority of the case
4. **Step 4** - Flag immediate concerns — especially model errors affecting reserves — and escalate or fast-track anything urgent before proceeding
5. **Step 5** - Summarise findings for insurers, pension schemes and regulators in plain language, agree the next step, and file the intake section of valuation reports

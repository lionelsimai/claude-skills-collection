# New Client Onboarding Pack — Physical Education

Onboard school pupils and sports teams smoothly into a PE teacher's service in school sports facilities and playing fields. The deliverable is an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
This skill is written for a PE teacher working with school pupils and sports teams in school sports facilities and playing fields.

## When to use
- You are a PE teacher preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in school sports facilities and playing fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces an onboarding pack: welcome information, expectations, first steps and contacts.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Send a welcome pack before the first appointment: what the service does, what happens first, what to bring from lesson plans
2. **Step 2** - Set expectations honestly — timescales, costs where relevant, and how progress on participation rates will be shared
3. **Step 3** - Cover rights and responsibilities on both sides, including confidentiality limits and how to raise concerns
4. **Step 4** - Make the first contact easy: named contact, directions to school sports facilities and playing fields, and what to do if plans change
5. **Step 5** - Check in after the first session or milestone and fix friction early, before it becomes disengagement or injuries from poor supervision

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: injuries from poor supervision.
- Never let this happen: unsafe equipment.
- Never let this happen: exclusion of less confident pupils.

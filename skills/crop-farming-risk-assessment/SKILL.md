# Risk Assessment — Crop Farming

Identify, score and control the risks in work for food companies, markets and land owners in farms and fields. The deliverable is a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
This skill is written for a crop farmer and agronomist working with food companies, markets and land owners in farms and fields.

## When to use
- You are a crop farmer and agronomist preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in farms and fields.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a risk assessment with likelihood/impact scores, controls and owners.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Map the activity step by step and list what could go wrong at each stage for food companies, markets and land owners and staff
2. **Step 2** - Score each risk for likelihood and impact, giving special weight to pesticide misuse
3. **Step 3** - For every high risk, define a control that removes, reduces or contains it, using tractors and implements where relevant
4. **Step 4** - Record the assessment in crop plans, assign an owner to each control, and set a review date
5. **Step 5** - Brief everyone involved before work starts; stop and reassess if conditions in farms and fields change

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: pesticide misuse.
- Never let this happen: crop failure from weather.
- Never let this happen: soil degradation.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a crop farmer and agronomist; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to farms and fields's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

# Referral & Coordination — Food Processing

Refer retailers, wholesalers and consumers onward and coordinate with other professionals and services. The deliverable is a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
This skill is written for a food production manager working with retailers, wholesalers and consumers in food factories and processing plants.

## When to use
- You are a food production manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in food factories and processing plants.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a referral pack and a coordination log with responses tracked.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Confirm the referral is appropriate: what retailers, wholesalers and consumers need that sits outside the food production manager scope in food factories and processing plants
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the referral from production plans — relevant history, current status, the specific question being asked
3. **Step 3** - With consent, send the referral and log who received it and when; chase non-responses on a set schedule
4. **Step 4** - Share incoming findings back into the main record so the plan and production yield stay joined up
5. **Step 5** - Close the loop with retailers, wholesalers and consumers: explain what happens next, who owns it, and what to do if things worsen — mindful of contamination incidents

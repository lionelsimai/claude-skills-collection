# Metrics & Outcomes Review — Restaurant Management

Track, interpret and act on the outcome metrics that matter for a restaurant manager. The deliverable is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
This skill is written for a restaurant manager working with diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams in restaurants and cafes.

## When to use
- You are a restaurant manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in restaurants and cafes.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Food cost | the cost of ingredients as a share of menu price |
| Cover | one diner served in a service |
| Allergen management | controlling ingredients that can cause allergic reactions |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Define each metric precisely — start with food cost percentage — including source data in menus and costing sheets and collection method
2. **Step 2** - Set review cadence and comparison baselines so trends, not single data points, drive decisions
3. **Step 3** - Investigate movements before celebrating or panicking: check data quality, case mix and one-off causes
4. **Step 4** - Link metric movements to practice changes and to risks such as food hygiene failures; test one change at a time where possible
5. **Step 5** - Report results honestly to stakeholders in restaurants and cafes, including where outcomes fell short and what changes

## Output Format
**Context:** [Situation in restaurants and cafes] | **Objective:** [What this must achieve] | **Audience:** [diners, kitchen and front-of-house teams and other stakeholders]

The finished output is a metrics dashboard definition and an interpretation/action review, structured as:
1. Summary of position and evidence
2. Detail, following the workflow above
3. Decisions, actions, owners and dates
4. Review point and escalation route

### Action Plan

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: food hygiene failures.
- Never let this happen: allergen incidents.
- Never let this happen: cash handling discrepancies.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a restaurant manager; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to restaurants and cafes's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

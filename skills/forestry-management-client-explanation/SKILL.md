# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Forestry Management

Explain complex forest manager matters to timber buyers, landowners and conservation bodies in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a forest manager working with timber buyers, landowners and conservation bodies in forests and woodland estates.

## When to use
- You are a forest manager preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in forests and woodland estates.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Restocking | replanting trees after felling |
| Thinning | removing some trees to help others grow |
| Certification | independent confirmation of sustainable forest management |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what timber buyers, landowners and conservation bodies actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind management plans — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as illegal felling
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking timber buyers, landowners and conservation bodies to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

# Session Delivery Guide — Livestock Farming

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for meat, milk and egg buyers and the food chain in farms and livestock units. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a livestock farmer working with meat, milk and egg buyers and the food chain in farms and livestock units.

## When to use
- You are a livestock farmer preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in farms and livestock units.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review animal health records before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as disease outbreaks
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and handling systems; check everything works and is safe before meat, milk and egg buyers and the food chain arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to animal health scores
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day

## Guardrails & Quality Checks
- Never let this happen: disease outbreaks.
- Never let this happen: animal welfare failures.
- Never let this happen: medicine record errors.
- Work only within the competence and authority of a livestock farmer; escalate or refer anything beyond it.
- Record facts and observations, not speculation, and keep records confidential to farms and livestock units's rules.
- Verify names, dates, figures and measurements twice before anything is issued or signed off.
- Use only real, verified information from the case in hand — never invent results, credentials or evidence.

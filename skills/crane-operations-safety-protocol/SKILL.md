# Safety Protocol — Crane Operations

Work safely in construction and industrial sites: a practical safety protocol for a crane operator and construction and industrial clients. The deliverable is a written safety protocol with checks, responsibilities and emergency actions.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify the hazards present in construction and industrial sites for staff, construction and industrial clients and visitors, starting with dropped loads
2. **Step 2** - Write controls in order: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administrate, then protective equipment such as tower and mobile cranes
3. **Step 3** - Set pre-start checks and stop-work authority — anyone can halt work that looks unsafe, without penalty
4. **Step 4** - Define what happens when something goes wrong: first response, escalation, recording in lift plans
5. **Step 5** - Review the protocol after every incident and at least annually; brief and re-brief everyone it covers

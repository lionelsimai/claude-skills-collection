# Client Explanation & Communication Guide — Family Law

Explain complex family law solicitor matters to clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in plain, honest language. The deliverable is a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
This skill is written for a family law solicitor working with clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances in a law firm and family court.

## When to use
- You are a family law solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and family court.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a communication pack: explanation script, FAQs and a written summary.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Identify what clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances actually need to decide or understand, and the misconceptions to clear up first
2. **Step 2** - Translate the technical content — including terms behind client care letters — into plain language without losing accuracy
3. **Step 3** - Structure the explanation: what we found, what it means, the options, the recommendation and the risks such as missed court deadlines
4. **Step 4** - Check understanding by asking clients dealing with divorce, children and family finances to play it back in their own words; correct gaps kindly
5. **Step 5** - Follow up with a written summary the same day and record the conversation in the file

# Session Delivery Guide — Intellectual Property Law

Prepare for and deliver a professional session or appointment for creators, brands and technology companies in a law firm and intellectual property offices. The deliverable is a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
This skill is written for a intellectual property solicitor working with creators, brands and technology companies in a law firm and intellectual property offices.

## When to use
- You are a intellectual property solicitor preparing, delivering or reviewing this part of your work in a law firm and intellectual property offices.
- You need a defensible, repeatable approach that produces a session plan, delivery checklist and post-session record.
- A colleague, auditor or client asks how this was done and what evidence supports it.

## Key Concepts
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Trade mark | a sign distinguishing one business's goods or services |
| Infringement | unauthorised use of protected intellectual property |
| Licence | permission to use intellectual property on agreed terms |

## Workflow
1. **Step 1** - Review trade mark applications before the session so you know the history, goals and any cautions such as missed renewal deadlines
2. **Step 2** - Prepare the space and IP management systems; check everything works and is safe before creators, brands and technology companies arrive
3. **Step 3** - Open the session by agreeing the focus and what a good outcome looks like, linked to filing success rates
4. **Step 4** - Deliver the session, adapting in the moment while staying inside the agreed plan and professional scope
5. **Step 5** - Close by summarising what was covered, confirming follow-ups, and completing the session record the same day
